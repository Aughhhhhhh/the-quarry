Choose an outcome

On a fresh game, follow Rough Night until you reach a decision below. Keep Aim Assist off and use manual directional prompts, button mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe. Pass inputs unless the route calls for a miss. Continue past Death Rewind on a planned death. Input settings

  • Drown or recover the evidence · replay Chapter 3 with the rotor arm stolen. Otherwise start in Chapter 1.
  • Die in the bushes · replay Chapter 4 with Jacob alive.
  • Take a bite or die in a trap · replay Chapter 6. For the walkway death, replay Chapter 4 to kill Emma first.
  • Free Jacob or get a cage death · replay Chapter 8 with Jacob alive in his cage. The three Nick death recaps need him uninfected.
  • Reunite with Emma · replay Chapter 4 for Emma’s bite, or Chapter 1 to change the bracelet for an infected reunion.
  • Escape or die in Chris’s chase · replay Chapter 9 with Jacob rescued and uninfected. Replay Chapter 10 if Chris already survived.
  • Change the cure or leave Jacob infected · replay Chapter 9 with Jacob bitten and alive. Replay Chapter 10 if the lodge and hunt are already set up for the outcome you want.

Chapter Select restores the start of the chosen chapter. Continue through the epilogue after changing a choice. For a new Jacob infection, replay from Chapter 6 or earlier. Save and replay rules

Chapter 3

Trouble in Paradise

Use the walkthrough for Abi and Nick before Jacob takes over.

Optional tarot · The Star

Leave the dock toward the boathouse. Take the card at the first camera change. If the prompt expires, back up and approach again before meeting Emma. Pickup video

Jacob · Dive for the rotor arm

  1. Check the empty towel cabinet upstairs and return to Emma on the dock. Choose Enthusiastic, let Splash Emma expire, then choose Flirt.
  2. When Abi screams, choose Help Abi if you want Jacob to avoid the water. For the evidence or drowning, choose Dive In → Grab and press Up. The dive appears if Jacob stole the rotor arm.
Optional evidence · Bloated Corpse

The evidence registers after Dive In → Grab → Up, before Jacob gets tangled in the wire. Choosing Surface or missing Up skips it. Pickup video

  1. At the wire, choose Detangle to survive. One Pull gives you another chance to Detangle. Choose Pull twice or let either wire choice expire to drown. Drowning recap

Jacob · Decide whether Bobby marks him

  1. Follow Ryan’s run to Nick. Back as Jacob, let Call Out? expire, pass the snare struggle mash and choose Pleading.
  2. Let Throw Dirt? expire for the protective blood mark. If you want the opening bite in Chapter 6, take Throw Dirt? so Jacob gets away before Bobby marks him. The later Run mash can infect Jacob on either branch.

Chapter 4 · Chapter 5 follows

Bushes and island

Ryan · Aim into the bushes

  1. Take Apply Pressure? and pass the mash. Choose Dismissive. Take Call Out? when the bushes rustle. Hold fire through the first aiming window.
  2. With Jacob alive, the bushes move again. Hit him in the second window to kill him. To save him, hold fire, choose Questioning when he emerges and let Let Go? expire. If he died, choose Thoughtful to leave Emma a note. Bushes recap

Emma · Set up Jacob’s later scene

  • Jacob’s reunion. Take Max’s Chapter 4 bite. At the later zipline choose Reel Slowly and let Speed Up? expire.
  • Jacob’s walkway death. In the treehouse choose Open Trapdoor immediately to kill Emma.
  • Jacob’s other survival, cage or woods outcomes. Use the unbitten island escape.

Finish Dylan’s Chapter 4 lodge scene, then follow the lodge and radio-hut route in Chapter 5.

Chapter 6 · Chapter 7 follows

Walkway and bear trap

As Jacob, climb the wooden stairs and use the right fork. Finish the pickups before following the trail on the right at the bottom of the far stairs.

Optional evidence · Memory Card

Inspect the camera lying on the ground on the right branch of the raised walkway. Take it before descending the far stairs. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Justice

At the bottom of the far stairs, turn left and take the card at the camera change. If the prompt expires, walk away and return. The right trail starts the chase. Pickup video

Jacob · Pass the dodge, take a bite or die

  1. With Emma alive, choose Delighted → Apologetic and press Right to stay uninfected. Let Right expire for Caleb’s opening bite if Jacob took Throw Dirt? in Chapter 3.
  2. With Emma dead, press Down to dodge, or pass the climb mash if you miss Down. For the walkway death, let both the Down prompt and the climb mash expire. Walkway recap
  3. Run and pass the mash to reach the trap. If you need Caleb’s bite, choose Run and fail the mash. This works with or without Bobby’s blood and ends the chase before the bear trap. With Hide, pass Don’t Breathe to reach the trap. Failing it gives Jacob a bite only if Bobby left him unmarked. Chase variants
  4. At the trap choose Pry Open for the safe capture. For the death, choose Pull Free and let Up expire. The PC build records the bear-trap death even when a bitten Jacob is shown moving afterward. Bear-trap recap

Finish Chapter 6 from the pool house. Hit Nick as Abi to keep her available for the lodge silver. As Emma, let Run? expire at the hunters. A new bite there would replace her Chapter 4 infection and prevent the reunion. In Chapter 7, use the syringe escape so Travis is unharmed.

Chapter 8

The Belly of the Beast

Follow the quarry route. For the reunion, use Emma’s van steps to repel her alive with Regular Shot. At the basement, finish exploring before entering the red-lit tunnel.

Optional tarot · The Empress

Turn left from the basement entrance into the distillery and take the card at the camera change. The Rum Still clue is here too. Pickup video

Optional tarot · The Emperor

Return to the entrance and go straight along the other corridor. Take the card before entering the red-lit room. Pickup video

Ryan · Free Jacob and keep Nick alive

  1. Enter the red-lit room and press Down at the slippery floor. Let Open Cage? expire. Take Stop Laura? to save Nick.
  2. Choose Help Jacob, then breaker 1 → breaker 2. This opens Jacob’s cage. Choose Continue, then breaker 2 → breaker 3. The passage opens and Nick stays in his cage.

Ryan · Choose a cage death

Keep Jacob uninfected and take Stop Laura? so Nick is alive. Then choose the steps for the recap you want.

  • Open both cages. Choose Help Jacob and open Jacob’s door with 1 + 2. Choose Continue and open Nick’s door with 3 + 4. Nick kills Jacob immediately. Wrong cage recap
  • Leave Jacob. Choose Leave Jacob when he asks for help. You can also choose Help Jacob, open his door with 1 + 2 and choose Abandon before opening the passage. Nick kills him at the power cut. Refusal recap
  • Stall the rescue. Choose Help Jacob, then select 1 + 3 to shock Ryan. Choose Continue and select 1 + 3 again. Nick kills Jacob when the power goes out. Alternatively, open Nick’s door first with 3 + 4 while Jacob’s door and the exit are closed. Failed-rescue recap

For a later escape with Jacob still locked up, let Stop Laura? expire so Laura kills Nick, or leave an infected Jacob in the cage. He survives the Chapter 9 power cut in either case. Breaker recovery and power-cut outcomes

Choose Move On if Eliza offers a reading.

Chapter 9

Power cut and attic

  1. Use the Hackett house steps to reach Laura’s fight with Jedediah. Press Left three times and choose Run. Laura cuts the power after the fight. This decides the fate of Jacob if he was still in a cage.
  2. For Caleb to survive later, give Kaitlyn the crane bite. To kill Caleb later, take silver at the shelter fuse box as Abi and climb the ladder so she can pass it to Kaitlyn.
  3. For Chris’s chase in the woods, leave Chris alive. With Travis unharmed, aim far right and shoot Laura in the attic. Chris kills Ryan and Travis survives. Letting the shot expire also keeps Chris alive, but kills Travis.
  4. For the reunion or a cure, hit Chris directly ahead in the attic. To hunt Silas, choose Accept at Laura’s bite beforehand and let Pull Away? expire. Choose Suspicious after the shot. To keep Silas alive, choose Decline at Laura’s bite. After Chris dies, let the Up prompt expire when Laura reaches for Travis’s gun. Ryan dies and Travis captures Laura, skipping the hunt.
Optional tarot · The Hierophant

In the storm shelter, approach the stairs and take the card before climbing the separate ladder. If the prompt expires, back away and approach again. If you take it, press Left during Eliza’s flashback at the end of the chapter. Pickup video

Chapter 10

Woods, lodge and cures

As Max, choose Stay if the swim choice appears. As Kaitlyn, go upstairs in the lodge and choose Prepare for Attack at the portrait after getting your pickups.

Optional tarot · The High Priestess

Take the stairs next to the bunk room and approach the top-floor windows before starting the attack. Take the card at the camera change. Pickup video

Jacob · Meet Emma or walk alone

  • Uninfected Jacob, Chris dead, Emma elsewhere. Jacob survives after leaving the cages. This is the normal Rough Night outcome with an unbitten Emma. Survival recap
  • Uninfected Jacob meets Emma. Give Emma only Max’s bite, repel her with regular shells and shoot Chris. As Jacob choose Honest → Remorseful → Apologetic to tell her about the van and earn Nobody’s Fool. Reunion recap
  • Infected Jacob meets Emma. Give Emma Max’s island bite and let Run? expire at the Chapter 6 hunters. Give Jacob Caleb’s bite, drive Emma off at the van with Regular Shot, free Jacob and shoot Chris. Jacob turns. Emma uses her bracelet automatically if Jacob picked it up. Leave it in the grass in Chapter 1 for the branch where he kills her. Finish with both Caleb and Silas alive, Caleb dead or Silas dead to choose Jacob’s wording. Paired recap · Bracelet recap

Jacob · Escape Chris and the snare

Free an uninfected Jacob and leave Chris alive. Chris catches up to him in the woods. Use the branch for your recap.

  • Escape. Choose Run and pass the mash, or Hide and pass Don’t Breathe. Press Up at the snare to dodge it and trap Chris alive. Escape recap
  • Caught during the chase. Choose Run and let the mash expire, or choose Hide and fail Don’t Breathe. Chase death recap
  • Caught in the snare. Pass the Run mash or Don’t Breathe, then let Up expire at the snare. Snare death recap
  • Caught before the chase. Let the initial Hide or Run choice expire. Timeout death recap

Kaitlyn and Laura · Choose the cure

Give Jacob Caleb’s bite and free him from the cages. For a paired recap, set up the infected reunion without the bracelet. For Jacob’s standalone recap, keep Emma unbitten so they never meet in the woods.

  1. Keep Caleb and Silas alive. Decline Ryan’s bite, then shoot Chris and let Laura’s gun grab expire to skip the hunt. With Kaitlyn bitten, choose Run → Beam at the lodge and let both the balance mash and Up expire. Kaitlyn and Caleb live. Jacob’s infection remains. Infected Jacob recap · Paired infected recap
  2. Kill Caleb and keep Silas alive. Decline Ryan’s bite, then shoot Chris and let Laura’s gun grab expire. Keep Abi alive at the pool and bring her to the office with silver. At the lodge choose Run → Beam and pass the balance mash, or press Up if you miss it. Choose Run → Investigate and hit Caleb during the three-second base window. Caleb cure · Paired Caleb cure
  3. Kill Silas with Caleb alive. Leave Travis unharmed and accept Ryan’s bite, then shoot Chris and choose Suspicious. Use the bitten Kaitlyn beam fall above to save Caleb. Follow the car and nest steps, take Raise Gun? and shoot Silas. Silas cure · Paired Silas cure
