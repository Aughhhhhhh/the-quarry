Chapter 3

Ryan · Rescue Nick and get the vial

Finish Abi’s escape and Jacob’s lake scene. The vial is optional for the hunt and required for the protection recap.

  1. Choose Worried → Assertive → Take Shortcut. Press Up, Up, Left at the cliff and pass the climb mash.
  2. At the river choose Take Shortcut again. Press Up, Left, Left on the stones, then Up, Right at the stairs. The complete run earns Nick of Time.
  3. When Bobby is holding Nick, take Shout? and hit Bobby in the 7.5-second base window. Ryan picks up the vial and Bobby survives. If Ryan reaches only the bushes, hold fire for Nick to emerge safely. Missing Bobby or holding fire when he is there brings Nick back without the vial. Timing and missed prompts

Finish Jacob’s snare scene. Choose Move On if Eliza offers a reading.

Chapter 4

Ryan · Firepit and lodge

  1. Take Apply Pressure? and pass the mash. Choose Dismissive when Dylan suggests amputation. Take Call Out? when the bushes rustle.
  2. Hold fire in both aiming windows to spare Jacob. The second shot can kill him. Choose Questioning when he emerges and let Let Go? expire. Jacob’s shooting route · Manual combat achievement
  3. Finish Emma’s treehouse chase through her first zipline. Watch for Strength when the group carries Nick inside.

Chapter 9 · Before the attic

Ryan · Keep the knife, then decide whom to spare

  1. At Constance’s shotgun struggle, let the mash expire to spare her. Choose Run and press Right to dodge the shot. Bobby stabs Ryan either way.
  2. Let Pull Knife? expire. If you already took the interrupt, choose Stop Pulling. Take the two bedroom pickups before interacting with the left door’s keyhole or the three-minute base timer expires.
Optional evidence · Kaylee’s Letter

Open the jewelry box on the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Death

Cross toward the bed. Take the card when the camera cuts to it. Pickup video

  1. Inspect the left door’s keyhole, choose Block Door and pass both mashes. If you miss the first, press Up at the next door and pass the final mash to keep the knife. If Bobby takes it, Ryan loses the later Stab prompt. Hide route and door recovery
  2. As Laura, choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe in the bathtub. Skip the piano. Go through the blue room and upstairs. The Hunting Trophy Wall is in the blue room and the Hackett Family Tree is in the office.
Optional tarot · The Sun

Approach the windows beyond the upstairs office. Take the card before approaching the door. Step away and return if the prompt expires. Pickup video

  1. At the upstairs door press Left, Left, Left and choose Run to spare Jedediah.
  2. As Ryan, press Left at the wall gap, Right to catch the knife and pass Don’t Breathe. At Bobby, take Stab? for Ryan to survive the revenge shootout. Chris kills wounded Bobby and any surviving Constance or Jedediah in the attic. To leave Bobby alive, let Stab? expire.
  3. Choose Sympathetic when Laura reaches Ryan. Choose Accept to get Phlebotomy and stop the wound from killing him. Let Pull Away? expire. Taking that interrupt also gives him the bite. Choose Decline for the bleed-out routes.
  4. Complete the scrapyard and shelter scenes that your save reaches. If Abi or Emma is exploring the shelter, take the optional card before leaving.

Chapter 9 · Attic shootout

Choose whom Ryan shoots

As Laura, inspect the syringes if you want the extra interaction, then approach Chris. After they fall through the floor, Ryan has a 4.1-second base window. Aim directly ahead for Chris or farther right for transformed Laura. One miss allows a second shot if time remains. Attic path and exploration

  • Hold fire. Let the aiming window expire. Chris kills Ryan. Transformed Laura and Travis kill one another and Mutually Assured unlocks. Two misses also reach this branch. Ryan’s attic death recap · Travis’s fight recap
  • Hit Laura. Aim farther right and shoot transformed Laura. Chris kills Ryan and Travis escapes the fight. Travis’s escape recap
  • Hit Chris. If Travis was unharmed in Chapter 7 and Ryan accepted the bite, choose Suspicious or Inquisitive to earn Above the Law and reach the Silas hunt. Otherwise follow the struggle that matches your jail setup.

Travis unharmed in jail · Laura and Travis reach for the gun

Have Ryan Decline the bite and shoot Chris. Ryan bleeds out before the next prompt. Ryan’s bleed-out recap

  • Travis captures Laura. Let Laura’s Up expire. Grabbing the gun and missing or waiting out the shot also sends her to the basement. Travis’s house recap
  • Laura shoots Travis. Press Up to grab the gun and hit him in the 3.2-second base window. In Chapter 10 walk Max to the dock to reunite them and earn Should’ve Gone to the Motel. Travis’s house death recap

Travis wounded in jail · Ryan and Travis fight

Shoot Chris after wounding Travis in Chapter 7. Travis stabs Laura as she turns human. Choose Aggressive or Diplomatic. Travis lunges either way.

  • Travis shoots Ryan. Let Down expire. If you dodge successfully, a missed shot or timeout also lets Travis shoot Ryan. This works with or without Ryan’s bite. Ryan’s gunshot recap · Travis’s revenge recap
  • Ryan shoots Travis and survives. To survive, Ryan must have stabbed Bobby and accepted Laura’s bite. Press Down at the lunge and hit Travis in the 2.9-second base window. One miss ends the opportunity. Ryan’s survival recap · Travis’s shooting recap
  • Bobby kills Ryan after the shooting. Leave Bobby unstabbed and Accept the bite. Press Down and shoot Travis. Bobby returns and kills Ryan. Bobby’s attack recap
  • Ryan bleeds out after both shots. Choose Decline at the bite. Press Down and shoot Travis after shooting Chris. The scene checks the bite before Bobby’s survival, so Ryan bleeds out here even if Bobby is alive. Two-shot bleed-out recap

Chapter 10

Travis and Ryan · The car and Silas’s nest

For the hunt, keep Travis uninjured during the jail escape, have Ryan accept the bite, shoot Chris and agree to help. In Chapter 10, complete Max’s dock choice and Kaitlyn’s lodge scene if they play.

Optional tarot · The High Priestess

As Kaitlyn, take the stairs next to the bunk room and approach the top-floor windows before starting the attack. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Trail Cam Footage

As Kaitlyn, check the boxes near the upper-floor couch before choosing Prepare for Attack at the portrait. Pickup video

Car attack · Stop safely or crash

  1. Choose Inquisitive in the car. Press Right at the attack. Choose Grab Gun → Take Cover.
  2. Press Down to pull the handbrake. If you miss it, press the second Down to save Travis. Either success keeps all three alive. For the fatal crash, let both Down prompts expire. Silas kills Travis and Ryan dies in the crash. Laura’s next prompts determine whether Silas dies. Ryan’s crash recap · Travis’s car recap · Laura’s final shot

Nest · Raise the gun

  1. After stopping safely choose Determined. If Ryan has the vial, choose Give to Ryan to protect him or Take Vial to protect Laura. Continue with Probing → Reflective.
  2. Take Raise Gun? within six seconds for a clear shot at Silas. To struggle with Travis instead, let the interrupt expire.
  • Hit Silas. Aim into the cage and fire within about eight seconds. Ryan, Travis and Laura live. Silas dies and The White Wolf unlocks. Ryan’s hunt recap · Travis’s hunt recap
  • Save Ryan with the vial. Choose Give to Ryan after the car, take Raise Gun? and hold fire until the window ends. Silas kills Travis and Laura, then spares Ryan. Ryan’s protection recap
  • Silas kills Ryan and Travis. Give Laura the vial if it’s available. Take Raise Gun? and let the shot expire. Silas kills Travis and Ryan. Laura lives only if she has the vial’s protection. Ryan’s attack recap · Travis’s woods recap

Nest · Let Raise Gun expire

  • Travis is shot. Let Right expire when Travis lunges to fire accidentally. Then shoot Silas to save Ryan and Laura, or hold fire to let Silas attack. To shoot Travis deliberately, pass Right and aim at Travis on the ground. Hitting him immediately starts Silas’s attack. Both shots select the same Travis recap on the audited PC build. Travis’s shared shooting recap
  • Silas kills Travis after the struggle. Press Right when Travis lunges, then hold fire through the two-target window. Silas kills Travis on the ground, then attacks Ryan and Laura. The vial still protects its recipient. Travis’s ground attack recap
  • Save Travis after the struggle. Press Right at the lunge and aim at Silas in the cage. Hitting Silas keeps all three hunters alive. Compare all seven nest branches
