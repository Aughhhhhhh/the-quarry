Choose an outcome

Follow Rough Night for shared scenes, then use the changes here. Set Aim Assist to Off and use manual directional prompts, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe. Pass prompts unless the route calls for a miss. Continue past Death Rewind on planned deaths. Input settings

  • Die, get trapped or escape alone at the scrapyard · replay Chapter 8 to separate Kaitlyn from Dylan. Start in Chapter 4 if Emma needs the island bite.
  • Get Kaitlyn bitten while Dylan is at the crane · replay Chapter 9 with both alive and together.
  • Die at the first lodge shot · replay Chapter 10 with Kaitlyn alone and unbitten.
  • Survive or die upstairs · replay Chapter 10 with the bite status for the recap you want.
  • Shoot Caleb or miss with silver · replay Chapter 10 if Abi or Emma already reached the office with a shell. Otherwise replay the earliest missing setup.
  • Trap Caleb, hide alone, or be freed by Dylan · replay Chapter 10 if you already have the bites and rabbit you need. To add the rabbit, replay Chapter 1.

Keep the cure separate from the fight when planning a replay. Several cards use the fact that Silas died. Some also check Kaitlyn’s earlier bite, which the crash kill preserves. Chapter Select rules · Kaitlyn as sole survivor

Chapters 1 to 7

Set up the rabbit, the silver and Dylan’s bite

  1. In Chapter 1, choose Leave Bags as Abi to keep Dylan unbitten at the radio hut. For a rabbit route, choose Break In, inspect the toy on the shelf and choose Keep. In Chapter 5, remove Dylan’s bitten hand with Desperate → Chainsaw and pass the mash if you need him human at the lodge after the break-in. Radio-hut walkthrough
  2. In Chapter 2, choose Shut Trapdoor in Chris’s office so Abi can climb the shelter ladder without a broken-rung recovery. For the Chapter 10 Ticket Stub, use Kaitlyn’s table route with Bobby in Chapter 5 to break the frame.
  3. For a van fight in Chapter 8, give Emma Max’s Chapter 4 bite and let Run? expire at the Chapter 6 hunters. Otherwise follow the unbitten island route in Rough Night.
  4. In Chapter 6, hit Nick as Abi to keep her available for silver and the van ammo choice. If you want Abi dead at the van, withhold the shot and Nick kills her. Pool-house steps
  5. In Chapter 7, take and hide the syringe and press Left on the escape so Travis is unharmed. This leaves the hunt available for Kaitlyn’s cured outcomes. Station walkthrough

Chapter 8

Choose who goes to the scrapyard

Follow Laura and Ryan’s quarry walkthrough and take any pickups before leaving.

Optional tarot · The Lovers

After the railing breaks, keep left on the next ledge. Take the card before approaching the stairs ahead. Card and pickup video

Optional evidence · Scarred Flesh

Inspect the patch on the ground before the next walkway. Both outcomes of the stair prompt leave it accessible. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Wheel of Fortune

Follow the tunnel and take the card when the camera swings around. If it expires, back out of the trigger and approach again before leaving through the basement door. Card and pickup video

At the lodge, choose Honest if Abi is alive or Optimistic if she is dead.

  1. Kaitlyn and Dylan together, no van bite. If Emma is unbitten, choose Encouraging when they discuss the broken van. If Emma attacks and Abi is alive, choose Shoot, hit Emma and hit her again on the roof. Choose Regular Shot to keep Emma alive.
  2. Dylan takes a late bite. With Emma attacking, Abi alive and Dylan unbitten, choose Shoot and hit Emma. Let the roof shot expire. After Emma bites Dylan, hit her in the 3.9-second rescue window and choose Regular Shot. Use this bite when the freezer route needs both counselors infected and you want to skip the torch fight. Van bite and ammo choices
  3. Kaitlyn goes alone. With Abi alive and Dylan uninfected, hit Emma in the first window, then let both the roof shot and the rescue shot expire. Dylan dies and Abi remains available to pass silver. With Abi dead, choose Run at the van. Kaitlyn goes alone even if an active radio-hut bite saves Dylan inside the van. Van shot timers

In the basement, finish exploring before going into the red-lit room.

Optional tarot · The Empress

Turn left from where you gain control and walk around the distillery room until the camera reveals the card. The Rum Still clue is in the same room. Card and pickup video

Optional tarot · The Emperor

Return to the starting corridor and walk straight. Take the card before entering the tunnel lit in red. Card and pickup video

As Ryan, take Stop Laura? to spare Nick. Free Jacob with 1 + 2 → Continue → 2 + 3 if he is in the cage. Breaker and recovery steps

Chapter 9

The scrapyard and the route to the lodge

Use the house walkthrough up to Ryan’s bite decision. Accept Laura’s bite and let Pull Away? expire if you plan to hunt Silas. Choose Decline if you want the hunt skipped with Chris dead.

Optional evidence · Kaylee’s Letter

Open the small jewelry box on the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Death

Cross the bedroom toward the bed to trigger the camera change. Take the card before interacting with the left door or running out of time. Card and pickup video

Optional tarot · The Sun

Walk toward the windows in the room beyond the upstairs office. Take the card before you approach the door. Step away and approach again if the prompt expires. Card and pickup video

Explore the scrapyard before approaching the crane

Take the path to the right of the wrecks and enter the garage. Use the gate switch near the downstairs stairs. Go through the gate and up the metal stairs onto the containers.

Optional tarot · Judgement

Cross the container roofs until the camera cuts to the card. Take it before going down the far stairs and through the bus. Pickup video

Descend the far stairs, cross through the wrecked bus, then go left to the yellow crane. Approaching it starts the fight. Clues and exploration map

With Dylan · Choose whether Kaitlyn is bitten

  1. Choose Encouraging → Lift Car → Slam Car. If you choose Warn Kaitlyn instead of Lift Car, press Up before Slam Car.
  2. Pass the lever mash to keep Kaitlyn unbitten. Let it expire for Silas’s ankle bite. Dylan still drops the car. An untreated radio-hut bite makes him turn before the lodge. A van bite leaves him human and with Kaitlyn.
  3. To give Dylan a fresh bite here too, start with him uninfected and with both hands. Choose Sound Horn, pass the torch mash, miss Left, then pass the recovery mash. Pass the lever mash for a Dylan-only bite or fail it to infect both. Torch recovery and earlier bite variants

Alone · Gun, car and falling scrap

  1. Gun. Hit Silas as he approaches along the wall. The base aiming window is 5.1 seconds. Letting Gun or Hide expire, missing both shots on the Gun route, or failing the later support-bar mash kills Kaitlyn. Scrapyard attack recap
  2. Hide. Kaitlyn gets into the car. Pass Don’t Breathe to stay unbitten or fail it to get Silas’s bite. On either branch, hit Silas in the 2.3-second base window to continue. Missing both shots or letting the window expire kills Kaitlyn. Car death recap
  3. After a hit, pass the mash to pull the bar. Press Left to dodge the falling scrap and reach the lodge. Let Left expire to get pinned. A bitten Kaitlyn survives under the pile and skips the lodge. Her scrapyard card can still say she is infected after Silas dies. An unbitten Kaitlyn dies under the scrap. Trapped alive · Crushed

Abi or Emma · Carry silver upstairs

With Abi alive, inspect the lit fuse box if her Chapter 8 silver was spent. Use the ladder into Chris’s office. An uninfected Emma can bring fuse-box silver alone if Abi died. If Emma turns with Abi present, choose Ladder and pass the recovery mash if the rung breaks. Shelter walkthrough

Optional tarot · The Hierophant

Approach the stairs and take the card at the camera change. Do it before using the separate ladder. If it expires, back away and approach again. At Eliza’s flashback, press Left at the flying stake. Pickup video

Ryan · Choose the cure setup in the attic

  • Set up the hunt. With Ryan bitten and Travis unharmed, shoot Chris directly ahead and choose Suspicious. Later shoot Silas at the nest unless the recap steps specify the crash shot. The crash kills Laura, Ryan and Travis.
  • Keep Silas alive. With Ryan unbitten and Travis unharmed, shoot Chris and let Laura’s Up grab expire. Travis captures Laura and the hunt is skipped. To keep Chris alive too, shoot Laura in the attic instead. This leaves the hunt unavailable.

Chapter 10

Prepare for Caleb’s attack

If Max gets the swim choice, choose Stay. As Kaitlyn, go upstairs in the lodge. Check the chimney portrait and choose Keep Looking if you still want pickups. Return and choose Prepare for Attack once you’re ready. If Chris survived, handle Jacob’s chase if it plays.

Optional tarot · The High Priestess

Take the stairs next to the bunk room and approach the top-floor windows. Take the card at the camera change before preparing for the attack. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Trail Cam Footage

Inspect the boxes by the couch on the upper floor before preparing for the attack. Pickup video

The rabbit’s Keep choice in Chapter 1 is enough to unlock the decoy. You can inspect Abi’s bag in the kitchen to see the rabbit again. Lodge map and optional interactions

Kaitlyn · Run or take the first shot

  1. Choose Run to continue upstairs and keep Dylan with you if present. Choose Wait and hit Caleb within 1.6 seconds to stun him and reach the same route.
  2. If you let the shot expire with Dylan present, Caleb kills an unbitten Dylan or spares a bitten one. Kaitlyn continues alone. Use this to remove unbitten Dylan before the failed-decoy route where Kaitlyn freezes with Caleb.
  3. If Kaitlyn starts alone, choose Wait and let the shot expire for her opening death when unbitten. An active bite lets Caleb spare her and ends her chase early. Opening death recap

Kaitlyn · Beam, window and upstairs hiding

Choose Beam and pass the balance mash or the Up recovery to continue. Choose Window and pass Down to rejoin the same route. For the epilogue outcomes, change the input below.

  • Beam or Window timeout. Let the choice expire. Unbitten Kaitlyn dies in the attic. A bitten Kaitlyn is spared. For the cured version, complete the hunt and kill Silas. Attic death · Cured attic recap
  • Window failure. Choose Window and let Down expire. Without a bite Kaitlyn dies. With a bite she is spared. Keep Silas alive for her infected card, or kill him for the cured attic wording. Window death · Infected survival
  • Beam fall. Choose Beam and fail the mash and the Up recovery. Unbitten Kaitlyn dies. A bitten Kaitlyn survives and can stay infected or be cured by Silas’s death. Beam death · Infected survival · Cured survival
  • Upstairs choice timeout. Pass the beam crossing and let Run or Hide expire. With no bite, Caleb kills Kaitlyn before she hides. An active bite saves her. Upstairs timeout death
  • Discovered while hiding. Pass the beam crossing, choose Hide and fail Don’t Breathe. An active bite keeps her alive. Kill Silas for the cured dormitory card. Dormitory death · Infected survival · Cured survival

Kaitlyn · Use or miss the silver shot

Cross the beam and choose Run downstairs. If a counselor is in the office with silver, they knock. Choose Investigate to get one shot in a three-second base window. Silver handoff requirements

  • Caught before taking silver. Let Investigate or Run expire. Caleb kills Kaitlyn if unbitten and spares her if bitten. Office choice death
  • Hit Caleb. With no Kaitlyn bite, she gets the ordinary silver-shot card. If she is bitten, spare Silas for the infected card or shoot Silas after the crash for the card that credits both shots. Unbitten · Still infected · Cured
  • Miss or hold fire. With no bite, Caleb kills her. A bitten Kaitlyn survives. Kill Silas for the cured version. Silver-shot death · Infected survival · Cured survival

Kaitlyn · Choose how to use the freezer

Cross the beam, choose Run downstairs and choose Run again if the office choice appears. Keep Dylan with you for a rescue. At the counter, use the branch below.

  • Let Caleb catch Kaitlyn. Fail Don’t Breathe, or pass it and choose Run. Letting either kitchen choice expire leads to the same attack. Without a bite she dies. If bitten she survives. Use the crash shot to preserve her bite flag for the cured attack card. Kitchen death · Infected survival · Cured survival
  • Hide inside without a rescuer. Pass Don’t Breathe and choose Freezer → Hide. If Abi never kept the rabbit, choosing Freezer hides Kaitlyn immediately. With Dylan unavailable, an unbitten Kaitlyn freezes. A bitten Kaitlyn can survive the cold with Silas alive. Freezer death · Infected survival
  • Hide and have Dylan open the door. Pass Don’t Breathe, choose Freezer and choose Hide if offered. Dylan needs a van or crane bite. For unbitten Kaitlyn, give Dylan the crane bite and leave Silas alive. For both bitten, give Dylan the van bite and Kaitlyn the lever bite. Keep Chris and Silas alive for the infected pair or shoot Chris and use the crash shot for the cured pair. Unbitten Kaitlyn · Both still infected · Both cured
  • Trap Caleb alone. With the rabbit kept, pass Don’t Breathe, then choose Freezer → Use Decoy and pass the mash. Kaitlyn stays outside. With Silas alive Caleb stays alive. Killing Silas removes Caleb’s protection from the cold. Decoy recap
  • Get pulled in with Caleb. With the rabbit kept, pass Don’t Breathe, choose Freezer → Use Decoy and fail the mash. Caleb kills unbitten Kaitlyn. If Kaitlyn has a bite and Dylan is still outside, he can free both of them even without his own bite. Leave Silas alive for the cursed rescue or kill him for the cured rescue. Decoy death · Cursed rescue · Cured rescue
  • Get pulled in while bitten and alone. Bring Dylan to the lodge unbitten and let the opening Wait shot expire so Caleb kills him. With Kaitlyn bitten, pass the beam and reach the kitchen. Pass Don’t Breathe, choose Freezer → Use Decoy and fail the mash. Keep Silas alive for her survival with Caleb. For the card where both freeze after losing the curse, kill Silas after the crash. Survives with Caleb · Both freeze
