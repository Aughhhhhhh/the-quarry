Before you start

  1. Pick the opening you want. With all ten evidence items, the closing article follows that opening, with one extra choice for No Soul Left Alive. With fewer than ten items, use the partial-evidence routes.
  2. Use Chapter Select only if your save already has the earlier evidence and choices the route requires. Replay from the earliest change, then collect every later item again. To change only the final pickup, replay Chapter 10 if Kaitlyn reaches the lodge.
  3. Keep Aim Assist off and use manual directional prompts, button mashes and interrupts. For a planned death, continue past Death Rewind. Let the podcast finish to see the closing article.

Opening routes use Rough Night for unchanged scenes unless a step names another walkthrough. Each route states what to change and where to begin. The routes use the Steam PC selectors, which count some deaths differently from the fate-card labels. How deaths are counted

Collect ten evidence items on the same route

For the full-evidence closings, keep Emma alive through the photo in Chapter 4, Jacob alive through the boardwalk pickup in Chapter 6, and Kaitlyn alive through lodge exploration in Chapter 10. Drowning Jacob in Chapter 3 prevents the boardwalk pickup.

Find a pickup on this route

The evidence steps below cover all ten items. For tarot cards, use Rough Night’s pickup finder or follow the base route for your headline. Full collectible checklist

Chapter 1 · Empty Vial

As Abi, inspect the big tree next to the central swing. As Jacob earlier in the chapter, choose Steal Rotor Arm to allow the lake dive in Chapter 3. Empty Vial video

Chapter 2 · Torn Bags

At the rear store door choose Call Jacob. Inspect the torn bags in the corner near the shotgun before going back to the safe. Torn Bags video

Chapter 3 · Bloated Corpse

After stealing the rotor arm, choose Dive In → Grab and press Up. When Jacob gets caught in the wire, choose Detangle to save him for the Memory Card. Bloated Corpse video

Chapter 4 · Attack Photo

In the treehouse choose Search Bag → Use Taser, then take Take Photo before the chase. Do this before any planned death for Emma. Attack Photo video

Chapter 5 · Claw Marks

As Dylan, inspect the wall beside the fan left of the radio hut entrance before talking to Ryan at the steps. Claw Marks video

Chapter 6 · Memory Card

As Jacob, climb the stairs and take the right fork on the boardwalk. Inspect the camera on the ground before descending the stairs. If you want Justice, take the left trail at the bottom before following the right trail into the chase. Memory Card video

Chapter 7 · Cease & Desist Letter

As Laura in the station, go up the short steps in the lobby corner. Inspect the letter across from the wooden door before going to sleep. Cease & Desist Letter video

Chapter 8 · Scarred Flesh

As Laura in the quarry, climb the stairs, then inspect the patch on the ground before crossing the next metal walkway. Scarred Flesh video

Chapter 9 · Kaylee’s Letter

As Ryan, open the jewelry box on the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Take the letter before checking the left door’s keyhole or running out the three-minute bedroom timer. Kaylee’s Letter video

Chapter 10 · Trail Cam Footage

As Kaitlyn, inspect the boxes by the couch on the upper floor. Pick up the footage before choosing Prepare for Attack at the portrait. You can still take it before a planned death at the lodge. Trail Cam Footage video

For tarot along the way, open the card steps in Rough Night or the base route named below. A character who dies early can miss later pickups, and a run that skips the lodge also misses The High Priestess.

All nine counselors survive

Use the full-evidence acquittal walkthrough through Chapter 9. Give Kaitlyn the crane bite, let Abi take the silver to the office and keep Travis, Bobby, Constance and Jedediah alive. With the first nine evidence items, you can replay Chapter 10 for both pairs.

The teens are charged after a local girl is shot

Choose Stay as Max and take the footage as Kaitlyn. At the lodge choose Run → Beam, fail the mash and let the Up recovery expire. Kaitlyn’s bite saves her and Caleb leaves. Kill Silas at the nest. Opening article · Self-defense acquittal

The teens are charged after a local family is killed

Replay Chapter 10. Keep Max ashore and take the footage again. Choose Run → Beam, pass the mash, then choose Run → Investigate and hit Caleb with silver. Kill Silas as before. Opening article · Case reopened and teens acquitted

Massacres with and without survivors

Start in Chapter 4 with the first three evidence items and Jacob alive, or start earlier if a pickup is missing. The existing all-evidence death route keeps Emma and Jacob alive for their pickups. Use the exact changes below for each headline.

No Soul Left Alive

Follow the No Soul Left Alive walkthrough with its evidence changes. All nine counselors and Bobby, Chris, Constance, Jedediah and Travis die. The rabbit keeps Caleb trapped while the final Up after the car crash decides whether he freezes. Opening article

With all ten items, press Up after winning Laura’s crawl mash for self-defense, or let Up expire for Caleb’s arrest. Full Chapter 10 replay steps

All nine die while members of the family survive

  1. Follow the evidence death route through Chapter 8. Let Emma fall from the zipline after her photo and let Jacob die at the bear trap after his Memory Card. Let Nick kill Abi, sedate Travis and let Laura shoot Nick in the cage.
  2. In Chapter 9 fail Constance’s gun mash, choose Run from Jedediah and skip Stab against Bobby. They survive the attic. Have Ryan accept the bite. Kill Dylan with the failed blowtorch mash, shoot Chris and choose Suspicious.
  3. As Max choose Swim to Shore. Take the footage. As Kaitlyn choose Prepare for Attack → Wait and let the shot expire. With Dylan dead and Kaitlyn unbitten, Caleb kills her. Use the fatal car inputs to kill Laura and Ryan.

This route leaves Bobby, Constance and Jedediah alive. Emma’s fall, Jacob’s bear trap and the crash keep the werewolf-death count below six. Opening article · Family arrested for killing a group of teens

Bear attack bloodbath

  1. Start in Chapter 3 if your save has the blood vial. Keep Jacob alive through the dive. As Ryan, let the shot at Bobby expire so he leaves without picking up the vial. Start in Chapter 4 if Jacob survived the dive and the vial is already absent.
  2. Use the all-nine-die route above, with a different death for Emma. After the photo and first chase in Chapter 4, choose Reel Quickly → Reel In at the final zipline and miss Right. Max kills her.
  3. In Chapter 6 take Jacob’s Memory Card and any optional tarot. On the path to the right, miss Down at the opening attack, then fail the climb mash. Caleb kills Jacob at the walkway. Let Nick kill Abi and have Laura shoot Nick in the cage as before.
  4. Sedate Travis in Chapter 7. In Chapter 9 spare the three Hacketts at the house, accept Laura’s bite, kill Dylan at the crane, then shoot Chris and choose Suspicious. Swim as Max and let the lodge shot expire as Kaitlyn. Take the footage before starting the lodge attack.
  5. Survive the car attack with Inquisitive → Right → Grab Gun → Take Cover → Down. At the nest take Raise Gun and hold fire. Without the vial, Silas kills both counselors and Travis.

All nine die. Bobby, Constance and Jedediah survive, and the werewolf deaths pass the six-death threshold. Opening article · Evidence casts doubt on the bear attack

Massacre with a survivor and the five Hacketts dead

Finish the all-evidence No Soul Left Alive route, then replay Chapter 10. Choose Stay to save Max. Collect the footage again and repeat Kaitlyn’s failed decoy and the fatal car crash. Max survives alone and the five Hacketts are dead. Opening article · Deceased Hacketts blamed for the massacre

Massacre with a survivor and Hacketts alive

Finish the all-nine-die route that spares Bobby’s group. Replay Chapter 10, choose Stay, pick up the footage again, and repeat Kaitlyn’s failed opening shot and the fatal car crash. Max, Bobby, Constance and Jedediah survive. Opening article · Hacketts arrested for killing several teens

Gunfire and the freak bear attack

Replay Chapter 8 on a survival save with the first seven evidence items, Jacob captured and Abi alive. Pick up Scarred Flesh. At the cages let Open Cage? expire, then let Stop Laura expire. Laura shoots Nick. Jacob gets out when Laura cuts the power in Chapter 9. Cage outcome rules

  1. In Chapter 9 follow Rough Night, including Kaitlyn’s lever bite and the alliance with Travis. Take Kaylee’s Letter.
  2. In Chapter 10, use the dock choice below as Max. Then collect Trail Cam Footage, keep Kaitlyn and Dylan alive using the beam fall with Kaitlyn bitten, and shoot Silas.

Teens turn on one another

Choose Stay. Nick is the only counselor who dies. The gunfire count is one and the werewolf-death count is zero. Opening article · Self-defense closing

Freak bear attack leaves several dead

Replay Chapter 10 and choose Swim to Shore as Max. Caleb kills him, tying the gunfire count at one. Recollect the footage and finish the same lodge and hunt steps. Opening article · Foul play closing

The sheriff’s car crashes

Start in Chapter 10 with everyone alive, nine evidence items and the hunt set up. Choose Stay as Max. Take the footage and save Kaitlyn and Dylan with the beam fall if she took the crane bite. With silver in the office, the silver-shot route also saves them.

Fatal car inputs

  1. Choose Inquisitive, miss Right when Silas attacks, then choose Grab Gun when paired with Take Cover.
  2. Miss Down when Laura can save Travis. Travis dies and the car crashes, killing Ryan.
  3. Win the crawl mash, then press Up to shoot Silas before Laura dies. With the other counselors alive, the crash report takes priority over the remaining rules.

Accidents and drowning

Tragedy at Hackett’s Quarry leaves teens dead

  1. Replay Chapter 6 with the first five evidence items and all counselors alive. Take Jacob’s Memory Card before leaving the boardwalk. Continue toward Emma.
  2. Choose Delighted → Apologetic, press Right, choose Run and pass the mash. At the trap choose Pull Free and miss Up. The second trap kills Jacob.
  3. Keep every other counselor alive using Rough Night. Take Stop Laura at the cage to save Nick. There is no Jacob puzzle. Collect the last four evidence items and finish the hunt.

Jacob’s bear-trap death triggers this opening. Emma’s fall from the zipline can also trigger it if no earlier rule wins. Opening article · Animals caused the deaths and the family is suspected

A teen’s body washes up on the lake shore

  1. Replay Chapter 3 with the rotor arm stolen in Chapter 1. At the lake choose Dive In → Grab and press Up.
  2. When Jacob gets caught, choose Pull twice. He drowns after collecting the Bloated Corpse evidence.
  3. Keep the other eight counselors alive using Rough Night and pass the final hunt. Save Nick at the cage with Stop Laura.

Jacob’s Chapter 6 scene never plays, so there is no Memory Card. The all-evidence closing image exists in the files, but this pairing is unavailable in a standard playthrough. Use a partial-evidence closing with the drowning opening.

Murder mysteries after Ryan bleeds out

Replay Chapter 9 with all nine counselors alive, Emma uninfected and Travis unharmed. For the full-evidence closings, have the first eight pickups before you start. For the hiker closings, you only need the three hiker items. In Chapter 9 take the letter, give Kaitlyn the crane bite and get Abi to the office with silver. Choose Decline when Laura offers the bite, then shoot Chris. Only Ryan dies among the counselors.

Police question the teens and a local family

Spare Constance, Jedediah and Bobby. When Travis reaches for the shotgun after Ryan bleeds out, miss Up as Laura. He locks her up alive. Keep Max on the island, take the footage and use the infected beam fall to save Kaitlyn and Caleb. Laura’s capture walkthrough · Opening article · Hacketts arrested for killing one teen

Police question the teens after the five Hacketts die

Replay Chapter 9. Win the mash to kill Constance, choose Attack and win the mash against Jedediah, and Stab Bobby so Chris kills him. After declining the bite and shooting Chris, press Up as Laura and shoot Travis. Walk Max to the dock for the reunion. Take the footage and use Run → Beam → pass the mash → Run → Investigate to shoot Caleb with silver. House and attic inputs · Opening article · Deceased Hacketts blamed for the murders

Closings with partial evidence

Recover the hikers and change how many Hacketts are charged

  1. Take Torn Bags, Bloated Corpse and Memory Card. Follow the survival route through Chapter 8. Leave at least one other item behind, such as Trail Cam Footage, on every replay.
  2. For multiple Hacketts charged, use the murder mystery that spares Bobby’s group and skip the footage. Several Hacketts live. Closing article
  3. For one Hackett charged, use the house kills and island reunion. At the lodge, with Kaitlyn bitten, choose Run → Beam, fail the mash and miss Up. Caleb survives alone among the six Hacketts checked by this closing. Closing article
  4. For the deceased Hacketts blamed, replay Chapter 10 from that branch and shoot Caleb with the office silver. Skip the footage again. Closing article

Unidentified animals are loose

Start a survival run without the complete hiker set. One simple route skips all evidence through Chapter 4 and takes Claw Marks at the Chapter 5 radio hut. Keep the hiker set incomplete through the end. Attack Photo or Scarred Flesh can replace Claw Marks. Closing article

Kaylee’s private struggles

Use a survival run and leave the hiker set incomplete. Skip the Attack Photo, Claw Marks and Scarred Flesh. Take Kaylee’s Letter as Ryan in Chapter 9, then finish normally. Closing article

The summer camp closes for good

Use the same setup and leave Kaylee’s Letter behind as well. With the hiker set incomplete and all three animal items missing, the closing reports the camp’s closure. Empty Vial, Cease & Desist Letter and Trail Cam Footage can stay in your journal. Closing article
