Before you start

On a fresh save, follow Rough Night from the prologue for the story between pickups. It keeps everyone available for the evidence scenes. Use Steal Rotor Arm as Jacob, keep him alive through Chapter 6, and get Kaitlyn out of the scrapyard for the final pickup. Either Chapter 2 trail works for evidence. Choose Rocky Road if you also want the full tarot deck.

Open Pause → Evidence after each numbered chapter. You should have the same number of entries as the chapter you finished. The first pickup awards The Truth is Out There. Ten on the same save award Conspiracy Theorist. Achievement details · Collect all 22 tarot cards alongside this route

Find a pickup in this walkthrough

Choose an item to open its steps. The links inside each step take you to its journal photo, video and other walkthroughs.

Recover missed evidence

If you can still explore the scene, return to the item and inspect it. Once the scene ends, finish the story and use Chapter Select. The save carries forward the evidence held at the start of the chapter you select. Pick up all remaining evidence again on that branch.

Empty Vial · Chapter 1
Replay Abi’s cabin exploration. Dylan visits the same tree in Chapter 5 and sees a scene there. The evidence entry comes from Abi’s Chapter 1 inspection.
Bloated Corpse · Chapter 3 or Chapter 1
Replay Chapter 3 if Jacob already stole the rotor arm. If you chose Break Fuel Line, go back to Chapter 1 and change the sabotage.
Attack Photo · Chapter 4
Use Search Bag → Use Taser and take the photo interrupt. If you want Emma to die after taking the photo, use the massacre’s alternate treehouse steps.
Memory Card · Chapter 6, 4 or 3
Replay Chapter 6 if Jacob was alive. If Ryan shot him, replay Chapter 4 and hold fire in both windows. If he drowned, replay Chapter 3 and choose Detangle to save him and keep the corpse evidence. For a planned Jacob death after the pickup, use the later death branch.
Trail Cam Footage · Chapter 10 or Chapter 9
Use Chapter 10 if Kaitlyn explored the lodge. If she died at the scrapyard or was trapped there, start in Chapter 9 and get her out. With Dylan, choose Lift Car → Slam Car and pass the lever mash. Alone, hit Silas, pass the support-bar mash and press Left to dodge the falling scrap. Solo scrapyard choices

For the other pickups, replay the chapter listed below. If more than one is missing, use the earliest chapter that restores access to all of them.

Chapter 1 · 1 of 10

Hackett’s Quarry Forever!

Evidence · Empty Vial
  1. Earlier in the chapter, choose Steal Rotor Arm when Jacob sabotages the van. This opens the Chapter 3 dive.
  2. As Abi, press Up at Emma’s high-five prompt. Both Leave Bags and Break In let you explore the cabin clearing.
  3. Find the large tree next to the central swing and inspect it as Abi. Take the vial before talking to Emma at the buggy to leave the cabins. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 2 · 2 of 10

Truth or Dare

Evidence · Torn Bags
  1. As Emma, use Kick Door? to enter the store, or let Jacob open it. Go through the aisles to the locked storeroom at the back.
  2. Choose Call Jacob at the inner door. Wait for him to open it and get the beer. Inspect the torn bags in the right-hand corner near the shotgun.
  3. With the evidence collected, inspect the shotgun for the safe code. Talk to Jacob to leave the room, then return to the safe by the entrance. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 3 · 3 of 10

Trouble in Paradise

Evidence · Bloated Corpse
  1. As Jacob, leave the dock for the boathouse and check the empty towel cabinet upstairs. Return to Emma. Choose Enthusiastic, let Splash Emma expire, then choose Flirt.
  2. When Abi screams, choose Dive In. With the rotor arm stolen, you have one base second to choose Grab underwater. Then press Up to record the corpse. Help Abi, Surface or a missed Up sends Jacob ashore without the evidence.
  3. When Jacob gets caught in the wire, choose Detangle to get him out. Each wire choice has a one-second base timer. If you chose Pull once, the two options swap positions. Read the labels and choose Detangle at the second prompt. Two Pull choices or a wire timeout drown him after the evidence pickup and cost you the Chapter 6 Memory Card. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 4 · 4 of 10

Don’t Panic

Evidence · Attack Photo
  1. At the firepit, let both aiming windows expire so Jacob lives for Chapter 6.
  2. As Emma, go inland from the island dock and take the stairs to the treehouse door. Inside choose Search Bag → Use Taser. Opening the trapdoor first kills Emma. Using bear spray first starts the chase without the photo.
  3. Take Take Photo? immediately after Emma uses the taser. This is the photo window. The chase continues if the prompt expires, and the photo is missed. Use the escape steps to keep Emma alive after the pickup. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 5 · 5 of 10

White Noise

Evidence · Claw Marks

As Dylan, cross the bridge and head through the cabins toward the radio tower. Check the wall beside the fan to the left of the hut entrance. Inspect the marks before talking to Ryan and choosing Confident to go inside. Both cabin choices from Chapter 1 allow the pickup. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 6 · 6 of 10

Prayers by Night

Evidence · Memory Card

As Jacob, climb the first wooden stairs and keep right at the fork on the walkway. Inspect the camera on the ground before going down the stairs at the far end. This exploration can play with Emma already dead. It is skipped if Jacob died earlier. For Justice, turn left after descending. The path to the right ends exploration. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 7 · 7 of 10

The Past Behind Us

Evidence · Cease & Desist Letter

Wait until Laura can explore the police station outside her cell. Go to the lobby and climb the short steps at the corner. Inspect the letter across from the wooden door. Get it before returning to the cell and choosing Go to Sleep. The pickup works with either later escape plan. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 8 · 8 of 10

The Belly of the Beast

Map · Scarred Flesh after the stair collapse

Evidence · Scarred Flesh

As Laura, follow the walkways up from the quarry pool. After the railing breaks, cross the ledge and approach the metal stairs. Press Right when the stairs collapse. Passing or missing that dodge still lets you reach the pickup. Inspect the patch on the ground at the top before crossing the next metal walkway. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Chapter 9 · 9 of 10

The Matriarch

Evidence · Kaylee’s Letter

After Bobby stabs Ryan, let Pull Knife? expire for the survival route. In the bedroom, go to the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Open the small jewelry box and take the letter before checking the left door’s keyhole. The bedroom has a three-minute base timer. Get the letter and Death before spending time on optional interactions. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

Get Kaitlyn out of the scrapyard before the chapter ends. See the lodge recovery steps if she died or got trapped under the scrap on an earlier run.

Chapter 10 · 10 of 10

Bricks & Mortar

Evidence · Trail Cam Footage
  1. As Kaitlyn, head upstairs from the main hall. If you inspect the portrait on the chimney wall on the way, choose Keep Looking.
  2. Go to the upper floor. Inspect the boxes by the couch for the footage. Lodge pickup map
  3. Confirm all ten entries in Evidence before choosing Prepare for Attack. You can collect the footage before a planned lodge death. With the other nine in this save, the pickup unlocks Conspiracy Theorist. Journal photo and pickup video · Rough Night step

What the evidence changes

Finish the story and let the podcast play to see the closing headline. The full set uses the ending’s opening headline to select its closing article. For fewer than ten pieces, the podcast checks the groups below in order.

  1. Missing hikers. Collect Torn Bags, Bloated Corpse and Memory Card. The number of surviving Hacketts determines which hiker headline plays.
  2. Unidentified animals. With the hiker set incomplete, take at least one of Attack Photo, Claw Marks or Scarred Flesh.
  3. Kaylee’s private struggles. Take Kaylee’s Letter with an incomplete hiker set and all three animal items left behind.
  4. The camp closes. Leave the hiker set incomplete and skip the animal items and Kaylee’s Letter. Empty Vial, Cease & Desist Letter and Trail Cam Footage can still change the conversation.
