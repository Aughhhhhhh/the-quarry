Before you start

For all 40 clues with everyone alive, follow the Rough Night story steps between pickups. Use Break In in Chapter 1, Shady Glade in Chapter 2, and Bobby’s table encounter in Chapter 5. Amputate Dylan’s bitten hand to keep him with Kaitlyn. If you also want all nine infections, use the separate Blood Pact and all-clues route.

Open Pause → Clues to check the named pickups in each collection. Meddling Kids! counts the 40 original pickups on one save. The later boxes attached to a clue are journal updates. All ten evidence items fit this route. Take Rocky Road on a separate run for the full tarot deck.

Recover missed clues

Take the same-run recoveries below if they are still ahead. After finishing the story, Chapter Select restores the items from the beginning of the chapter you pick. Start at the earliest missing clue or setup choice and collect the later clues again.

Chapter 1 cabins
Use Dylan’s Chapter 5 exploration for the newspaper, camp song and signpost. The newspaper needs the cabin already open. If it stayed locked, replay Chapter 1 and choose Break In.
Ranger Box and Trail Camera
Replay Chapter 2 on Shady Glade for the box. For the camera alone, Abi can collect it in Chapter 3 after either Chapter 2 trail.
Kid’s Letter Home
Replay Chapter 6 if Emma reached the firepit. If she died on the island, replay her Chapter 4 escape and keep her alive.
Scrawled Limerick
Read it when Laura can re-enter her cell during the same chapter’s station exploration. Finish before choosing Go to Sleep. Cell location
Ticket Stub and the other lodge clues
Replay Chapter 5 if the portrait’s nearby frame is intact. If Kaitlyn never explored the lodge, replay Chapter 9 and get her out of the scrapyard. If she explored and the frame was already down, replay Chapter 10.

Prologue · 4 clues

Into the Woods

Laura · Three woods clues
  1. Harum Scarum Poster. Follow the path past the large rock. Inspect the tree on the right. This first clue earns What’s This? Journal and video
  2. Escapology Trunk. At the next fork, follow the left side path to the trunk and inspect it. Journal and video
  3. Broken Cage. Backtrack from the trunk. Take the right side path to the broken cage before advancing along the main trail. Journal and video
Laura · The storm shelter

Bloodied Collar. At the camp, go down the steps outside the lodge. Choose Take Wrenches at the shelter. Enter and walk forward. Laura collects the collar automatically. Journal and video

Story inputs and optional tarot

Chapter 1 · 5 new clues

Hackett’s Quarry Forever!

Jacob · Lodge grounds
  1. Hunting Notice. From the van, walk between the parked cars. Inspect the sign at the edge of the grounds. Journal and video
  2. Camp Plaque. Go up the lodge steps. Inspect the plaque left of the main door before going inside for the bags. Journal and video
Abi · Locked cabin and clearing
  1. Newspaper Headline Scrap. Choose Break In with Emma. Inside, inspect the floor between the first two rows of bunks on the left. You can keep the rabbit nearby if you want the freezer decoy later. Journal and video
  2. Camp Song Recording. Go outside and find Room 08. Inspect the recorder at the foot of its steps. Journal and video
  3. Information Signpost. Inspect the sign beyond the central tree. Finish the cabin pickups before speaking to Emma at the buggy. Journal and video

Opening the cabin changes the Chapter 5 radio-hut attack. Story changes for Rough Night · Cabin map and interactions

Chapter 2 · 6 new clues

Truth or Dare

Emma · General store
  1. Damaged Memorabilia. In the main storage room, go behind the first shelf on the left and inspect the flyers. If you took the Harum Scarum Poster in the prologue, this gives an extra entry for It’s All Coming Together. Journal and video
  2. Lodge Renovation Plans. At the far end, inspect the shelf beside the large wooden table before returning to the safe. Journal and video
Dylan · Chris’s office
  1. Family Photo. Check the framed photo on the desk to the left. Journal and video
  2. Counselor’s Ledger. Inspect the hatch and choose Suspicious → Shut Trapdoor. Choose Playful when Dylan sits at the desk. He opens the drawer and collects the ledger. Do this before choosing Head to Party. Journal and video
Nick · Shady Glade
  1. Trail Camera. Choose Shady Glade at the fork. Follow the right edge and inspect the camera strapped to a tree. Journal and both pickup videos
  2. Ranger Box. From the camera tree, take the path left and inspect the metal box. If birds startle Abi first, choose Reassuring and turn left after the scene. Collect the box before leaving the glade. Journal and video

Chapter 3 · 3 new clues

Trouble in Paradise

Optional recovery · Trail Camera

Trail Camera. As Abi, take the side path along the right edge of the clearing. Inspect the camera on the tree before meeting Nick. This works after either Chapter 2 trail. The Ranger Box needs Nick’s Chapter 2 visit. Both pickup videos

Jacob · Boathouse deck
  1. No Swimming Sign. Leave the dock and take the left path around the boathouse to the railing facing the lake. Journal and video
  2. North Kill Gazette. Inspect the newspaper on the table across from the sign. Journal and video
  3. Old Camp Photo. Go to the far end of the deck. Look behind the seats for the photo on the wooden beam. Finish these pickups before returning to Emma after the towel cabinet. Journal and video

For the clues alone, Jacob’s later choices can vary after this scene. Keep him alive through Chapter 6 if you also want Memory Card or Justice.

Chapter 4 · 2 new clues

Don’t Panic

Emma · Both island paths
  1. Box of Matches. From the fork at the stairs, stay on the ground path and inspect the matches at the fire ring. Leave the nearby ladder alone so you can backtrack. Journal and video
  2. Police Car Keys. Return to the fork and go up the stairs. Inspect the window left of the treehouse door before going inside. Journal and video

Keep Emma alive for her Chapter 6 clue. Use Search Bag → Use Taser and pass the treehouse chase inputs. At the later crank choose Reel Slowly and let Speed Up? expire.

Chapter 5 · 2 new clues

White Noise

Kaitlyn · Set up the Ticket Stub

  1. Press Right at Bobby’s flashlight. Let Left at the banister expire. If you passed that dodge, fail the following Don’t Breathe to reach the same encounter.
  2. When Bobby throws the vial, you can press Left to dodge it. Either result leads to him lifting the table. He knocks the frame down before the shot.
  3. Hit the table in the 4.4-second base window, or press Up at the stairs if the shot fails. The frame stays down either way. All table branches · Chapter 10 pickup
Dylan · Cabin clearing
  1. Triggered Bear Trap. Inspect the ground by the fence at the far left of the clearing. Journal and video
  2. Letter to Camp Nurse. Check the paper outside Room 03. Finish the clearing pickups before going into the radio hut. Journal and video
  3. Camp Song Recording. If you missed it as Abi, inspect the recorder at the foot of Room 08’s steps.
  4. Information Signpost. If missing, check the signpost by the central tree.
  5. Newspaper Headline Scrap. If Abi chose Break In, go into the cabin she opened. Check the floor between the first two rows of bunks on the left.

The last three locations recover Chapter 1 clues before you enter the hut. Recovery conditions

If Abi opened the cabin, use Desperate → Chainsaw after Dylan’s bite and pass the mash to continue the survival route. Amputation and later dialogue

Chapter 6 · 3 new clues

Prayers by Night

Abi · Pool house
  1. Campers Photo. Check the board in the corner of the main room. Journal and video
  2. Spooky Drawing. Inspect the locker at the far end of the changing-room corridor. Finish both clues and The Moon if wanted before checking Nick. Journal and video
Emma · Firepit and tents

Kid’s Letter Home. Turn right from the firepit. Take the letter from the ground by the yellow tent before walking uphill toward the hunters. Journal and video

Chapter 7 · 3 new clues

The Past Behind Us

Laura · Cell and sheriff’s offices
  1. Scrawled Limerick. In the cell, inspect the wall opposite the loose brick before using the spoon. If missed, return during the later station exploration. Journal and video
  2. Charred Sheriff’s Badge. During station exploration, go upstairs to the second office on the left. Inspect the right side of the room. Journal and video
  3. Bizarre Yet Bonafide Podcast. Read the birthday card in the first upstairs office and the calendar in the third. Use the computer in the downstairs corner office. Laura enters the password and plays the recording. If you used the computer too early, return with both clues before sleeping. Journal and video · Computer and phone scene

Chapter 8 · 3 new clues

The Belly of the Beast

Quarry pickup map · Basement pickup map

Laura · Flooded quarry
  1. Old Quarry Tools. After the fall, inspect the ground to the left of the bottom of the first metal staircase. Journal and video
  2. Miner’s Lunchbox. Turn back before climbing. Circle the pool to the far end and inspect the metal lunchbox. Journal and video
Ryan · Hackett basement

Rum Still. From the starting corridor, turn left into the side room and inspect the distilling equipment. Get it before entering the red-lit passage to the cages. Journal and video

Chapter 9 · 4 new clues

The Matriarch

Pickup maps · Hackett house · Scrapyard

Laura · Hackett house
  1. Hunting Trophy Wall. In the blue room, inspect the display by the stairs. Leave the piano alone so its ambush doesn’t end exploration early. Journal and video
  2. Hackett Family Tree. Go upstairs into the office and inspect the framed chart by the armchair. Get it before approaching the next room’s exit and triggering the fight. Journal and video
Dylan or Kaitlyn · Scrapyard
  1. Scrapyard Note. Take the path right of the wrecks into the garage. Go upstairs and inspect the receipts at the end of the upper walkway. Journal and video
  2. Discarded Signage. Use the downstairs switch and go through the gate. Cross the container roofs and go down the far stairs. Inspect the green sign between the staircases before approaching the crane. Journal and video

Kaitlyn can collect both scrapyard clues on her own. Get her clear of the scrapyard so she can also explore the lodge. With Dylan · Kaitlyn alone

Chapter 10 · Final 5 clues

Bricks & Mortar

Kaitlyn · Lodge exploration
  1. Camp Letter. Go up the first stairs and turn left into the bunk room. Inspect the note on the floor. Journal and video
  2. Ancestral Portrait. Inspect the portrait on the chimney wall midway along the landing. Choose Keep Looking. Journal and video
  3. Ticket Stub. Inspect the fallen frame on the floor beside the portrait. If the frame is intact, you need the Chapter 5 table encounter. Journal and video
  4. Chalkboard Doodle. Go past the portrait to the classroom and inspect the board. Journal and video
  5. Inscribed Jewelry. Leave the classroom, turn left up the stairs and inspect the couch. Journal and video
