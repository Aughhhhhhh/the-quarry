Achievements & trophies

Steam has 40 achievements. Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S each have 40 worth 1,000G. PS4 and PS5 each have 41 trophies, including one platinum. Each entry shows its PlayStation grade and Xbox Gamerscore.

Use interactive play for ending achievements. Movie Mode awards Creature Feature when you start it. For Rough Night and Hackett’s Quarry Massacre, follow the interactive routes.

    Open the chapter-by-chapter achievement walkthrough

    Two short starts

    Start Movie Mode for Creature Feature. Then use a separate interactive save for Forewarned is Forearmed. Pick up The Fool and choose See More at the first Eliza interlude. Once the trophy unlocks, start the main run on its own save.

    First story run with all nine counselors alive

    Use the Auto combat walkthrough for this survival run. It uses the cabin break-in, the amputation and the silver shot. Keep Emma unbitten and save her treehouse bite for the reunion replay. Keep directional prompts, interrupts and Don’t Breathe on manual settings so you can take or skip them as the route calls for.

    1. Prologue through Chapter 2. Pick up all the Prologue and Chapter 1 clues to carry into the later Chapter 2 replay. They include the first pickup for What’s This? and the poster that matches the Chapter 2 flyers for It’s All Coming Together. As Jacob, steal the rotor arm for the Chapter 3 corpse evidence. As Abi, break into the cabin and keep the stuffed rabbit. Take Rocky Road for The Hanged Man.
    2. Collectibles throughout. Use the checklist to collect all 22 tarot cards for Decked Out and all ten evidence items for Conspiracy Theorist. Empty Vial in Chapter 1 is the first evidence pickup for The Truth is Out There. Choose Move On at each optional interlude and take The Hierophant in Chapter 9 for Hard Pass.
    3. Chapters 2–6. Use both shortcuts and pass all prompts for Nick of Time. Follow the early Reactionist route and pass Abi’s breathing check after climbing the tree. For You’re Breathtaking!, also pass the boar check in Chapter 2, Nick’s hiding check in Chapter 5 and Jacob’s Hide check in Chapter 6. Save the fifth pass for Ryan in Chapter 9 so you can take Kaitlyn’s table shot in Chapter 5. Amputate Dylan’s hand at the radio hut for Just a Flesh Wound.
    4. Chapters 7–9. Play the station computer recording for Bizzare Yet Bonafide and use the syringe on Travis. As Ryan, take both bedroom pickups, then check the left door’s keyhole and choose Hide before the three-minute timer runs out. Pass the fifth Don’t Breathe check. Accept Laura’s bite for Phlebotomy, shoot Chris and agree to hunt Silas for Above the Law.
    5. Chapter 10 and credits. Keep Max on the island, use the silver shot at the lodge, and kill Silas for The White Wolf. Finish with all nine alive for Rough Night. Let the credits finish and return to the main menu for Epilogue and the rest of the story progression awards.

    For the newspaper variants, use the completed No Soul Left Alive save to replay Chapter 10 for four closing headlines.

    ReactionistDuring the story · input · Bronze · 10G

    Activate 15 timed interrupts by pressing the displayed input while the prompt is active. The counter carries over between saves on Steam PC. Under Settings → Accessibility → Interrupt Speed, choose a timer setting if you want time to decide which prompts to take.

    Open the interrupt sequence · Follow the full achievement run

    A route through Chapter 4

    Take the numbered interrupts in order. From zero, the award should appear at number 15. The next prompt covers one miss. Between these inputs, use the survival walkthrough. Keep Aim Assist off when you call out at the Chapter 4 bushes.

    1. Prologue · Snoop. As Laura, open the trunk and press the prompt while it’s on screen. Trunk location and retry
    2. Prologue · Recoil. Miss Down at the branch during the run back to the car. Press Recoil later when Travis reaches to wipe Laura’s face. Traffic stop setup
    3. Chapter 1 · Squash? As Jacob, inspect the litter in the grass by the van. Take the prompt when he sees the spider. Spider location
    4. Chapter 1 · Nudge? Choose Break In as Abi. As Dylan back at the lodge, take Nudge? when she and Emma return with the bags. Use the radio-hut amputation in Chapter 5 to keep Dylan available for the lodge later.
    5. Chapter 2 · Kick Door? As Emma, press it while Jacob works on the camp store lock.
    6. Chapter 2 · Save Abi. Follow either trail to Abi’s sketch. Choose Sincere and press the interrupt when the boar charges.
    7. Chapter 2 · Interrupt? At Truth or Dare, have Ryan step in when Dylan questions Abi.
    8. Chapter 3 · Call Back? Follow the woods trail as Abi until Nick calls. Take the interrupt to answer.
    9. Chapter 3 · Kiss? Choose Honest → Cautious and take the first kiss prompt.
    10. Chapter 3 · Climb Tree? After Nick is attacked, choose Help Nick, press Up, choose Run and pass the mash. Take the climb interrupt, press Right, pass the climb mash and pass Don’t Breathe. Climb recoveries
    11. Chapter 3 · Splash Emma. As Jacob, check the boathouse towel cabinet and return to Emma. Choose Enthusiastic and take the splash prompt. Optional evidence dive afterward
    12. Chapter 3 · Shout? As Ryan, take both shortcuts and pass the directions. Use the interrupt when you see Bobby carrying Nick.
    13. Chapter 3 · Call Out? Use the prompt as Jacob before he is caught in the snare. Pass the struggle mash and let Throw Dirt? expire so Bobby marks him with blood. Snare sequence
    14. Chapter 4 · Apply Pressure? As Ryan at the firepit, take the interrupt while treating Nick and pass the mash.
    15. Chapter 4 · Call Out? Choose Dismissive and use the interrupt when the bushes rustle. Let both aiming windows expire to save Jacob. Firepit choices
    16. Chapter 4 · Take Photo. As Emma, choose Search Bag → Use Taser in the treehouse, then press the photo interrupt. This also collects the Attack Photo. Follow the unbitten escape afterward.

    For later chances, take Speak Up? as Nick in Chapter 5, Intervene as Kaitlyn at the pool in Chapter 6, or Stop Laura in Chapter 8. Replay starting points

    You’re Breathtaking!During the story · input · Bronze · 10G

    Pass five Don’t Breathe events with Settings → Accessibility → Don’t Breathe → Default. Hold the prompted input and release in the safe window when the red danger effect clears. Releasing while it’s red or holding until the breath meter runs out fails the event. Player tests report that Auto clears the scene without adding to this award. On Steam PC, the counter carries over between saves.

    Open the five-pass route · Follow the full achievement run

    Where to get the five passes

    With zero prior passes, these five checks can earn the award by Chapter 6. If you need the Ticket Stub, skip step 3 and replace it with Ryan’s Chapter 9 bedroom check below.

    1. Chapter 2 · Nick and the boar. Follow either trail, choose Sincere at Abi’s sketch and take Save Abi when the boar charges. Hold through the red danger effect and release once it clears. Boar outcomes
    2. Chapter 3 · Abi behind the rocks. Choose Help Nick, press Up, choose Run and pass the mash. Let Climb Tree? expire and pass the breathing check. If you’re taking the climb interrupt for Reactionist, press Right, pass the climb mash and pass the check that follows.
    3. Chapter 5 · Kaitlyn in the lodge. Press Right at Bobby’s flashlight, then Left at the banister. Pass Don’t Breathe and press Down at the stairs. This bypasses the table scene that exposes the Ticket Stub.
    4. Chapter 5 · Nick with the group. Choose Confident → Intrigued → Hide when Bobby approaches. Pass the breathing check. Group escape choices
    5. Chapter 6 · Jacob at the walkway. Start with Jacob alive. Pass Right with Emma or Down when alone. Choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe. Choose Pry Open at the bear trap. Chase and trap choices

    Chapter 9
    In Ryan’s bedroom, keep the knife in and check the left door before the timer expires. Choose Hide and pass the breathing check. Later press Left → Right at the wall gap for Ryan’s next breathing check. Laura gets another by choosing Hide in the bathroom after avoiding the opening headshot. Solo Kaitlyn can choose Hide in the scrapyard car.
    Chapter 10
    Free uninfected Jacob and spare Chris to get Jacob’s woods chase. Choose Hide, pass Don’t Breathe and press Up at the snare. At the lodge choose Run → Beam and cross safely. Choose Hide upstairs and pass Don’t Breathe. If the office choice appears, choose Run to reach the kitchen breathing check. Check the freezer outcomes before choosing what to do next.

    Peanut Butter Butterpops!Full run · combat · Silver · 20G

    On Steam PC, the game checks for zero recorded combat misses on the current save before the first epilogue fate card. Successful Auto runs let both Chapter 4 bushes timers and the Chapter 5 ceiling timer expire.

    Automatic combat with all nine counselors alive

    Open the full Auto combat walkthrough

    Set Settings → Accessibility → Aim Assist to Auto before starting a new game. Leave it on through the epilogue. Use a timer for Interrupt Speed so you can skip optional prompts. Use the Rough Night walkthrough between the changes below.

    1. Chapters 1–3. Break into the cabin as Abi. Let Auto handle Nick’s four shots. As Ryan, take both shortcuts and pass the prompts so Auto can hit Bobby.
    2. Chapter 4. Let both bushes timers expire to keep Jacob alive. Follow Emma’s unbitten route to skip the van fight.
    3. Chapter 5. Reach Bobby’s table shot by passing Right at the flashlight and missing both Left prompts. Use a manual Simple QTE setting for those misses and let Aim Assist handle the shot. At the radio hut, let the ceiling timer expire. When Dylan is bitten, use Desperate and amputate to keep him with Kaitlyn at the scrapyard.
    4. Chapters 6–8. Let Auto shoot Nick at the pool to save Abi. Let Emma’s Run interrupt expire at the hunters to keep her unbitten. Sedate Travis in Chapter 7. In the basement, use Stop Laura to prevent the scripted shot at Nick. Help Jacob and solve the breaker puzzle.
    5. Chapter 9. At the crane, choose Lift Car, then Slam Car and pass the lever mash to keep Kaitlyn unbitten. Have Abi pick up the silver shells at the shelter fuse box. Accept Laura’s bite as Ryan, let Auto hit Chris, then agree to hunt Silas.
    6. Chapter 10. Keep Max on the island. In the lodge, choose Wait for Auto to hit Caleb. Choose Beam, pass the mash, then choose Run and Investigate so Auto can shoot Caleb with silver. Survive the car attack and choose Raise Gun at Silas’s nest. Continue through the start of the epilogue.

    The audited Steam PC build requires ten refusal credits on one save. From the prologue through Chapter 8, pick up at least one card in each chapter and choose Move On when Eliza offers a vision. Each of these interludes checks for its own chapter’s cards. Extra pickups in one chapter still give you one refusal opportunity.

    Forewarned is ForearmedTarot · one reading · Bronze · 10G

    Pick up The Fool in the prologue, choose See More at Eliza’s first interlude and watch the vision. If you’re also working on Hard Pass, take the reading on a separate save so the main run can use Move On. Short unlock walkthrough

    The Steam PC award uses the See More counter. Chapter 6 offers visions for Justice and The Moon, but its See More branch lacks the stat increment found in the other optional interludes on this build. Use a different chapter for this unlock.

    It’s All Coming TogetherClues · connected discoveries · Bronze · 20G

    Story updates can also trigger the award. If you skip the matching pickups, the mandatory Bloodied Collar gets another entry when Chris falls through the attic floor in Chapter 9.
