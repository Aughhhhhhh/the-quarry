Before you start

In a fresh interactive save, follow the chapter steps below at each checkpoint. Use the achievement walkthrough for the scenes between them, or start at the earliest missing tape with Chapter Select after finishing the story. Check Pause → Paths to see which tapes have opened. Later entries on a tape depend on your choices.

For this route, choose Break In at the cabin, Shut Trapdoor in Chris’s office and Chainsaw after Dylan’s bite. Keep Jacob alive through the cages. These choices let all nine counselors reach the end and open the tapes without having to see opposing outcomes.

Use manual directional prompts, mashes and interrupts. The achievement walkthrough uses Aim Assist → Auto. If you play with manual aim, hit Bobby in Chapter 3, Nick in Chapter 6, Chris in Chapter 9, and Caleb and Silas in Chapter 10 as called out in the walkthrough. Input settings

Optional tarot, evidence and clues

Pickups between tape choices follow the achievement walkthrough. Open its pickup finder to jump directly to an item. For The Hanged Man, choose Rocky Road in Chapter 2. For the Ranger Box, choose Shady Glade. Either trail works for all 15 tapes. Find a pickup on this route · Chapter totals

Prologue

Into the Woods

02 · Above the Law

After Laura returns from the woods, pass the escape prompts so she reaches Max at the car. When Travis asks about the crash, choose Honest → Honest. Continue with Friendly → Compliant, take Recoil when he reaches for Laura, then choose Relieved. Other tape entries

01 · Laura & Max

At the camp, go down to the shelter by the lodge. Choose Take Wrenches → Confident and enter. When Max is attacked, choose Help Max. Max is bitten on every route. Other tape entries

Chapter 1

Hackett’s Quarry Forever!

15 · Keepsakes

Choose Reflective as Jacob. Before going into the lodge for the bags, turn left from where you gain control and inspect Emma’s silver bracelet on the ground by the van. The bracelet is a separate interaction from the litter. Later bracelet entries

03 · A Fool’s Errand

Try the lodge door, then use the open library window to the left. Get the bags by the stairs and return to Kaitlyn. Choose Playful → Mischievous → Brave → Steal Rotor Arm. Press Up to catch the phone and choose Friendly. The rotor arm also opens the Chapter 3 evidence dive. Other tape entries

04 · Down the Rabbit Hole

As Abi, press Up for Emma’s high five, then choose Break In. Inside, inspect the rabbit on the shelf and choose Keep. The break-in sets up Dylan’s bite in Chapter 5. Keeping the rabbit adds an entry and unlocks a freezer decoy later. Other tape entries

Chapter 2

Truth or Dare

05 · Playing with Fire

As Emma, choose Kick Door at the store. Find the locked storage room in the back and choose Call Jacob. Inside, inspect the shotgun to get the safe code. Talk to Jacob, return to the safe by the entrance and choose Leave Fireworks after Emma opens it. Firework updates and alternatives

06 · Watch Your Step

As Dylan, choose Friendly. During office exploration, inspect the floor hatch near Chris’s desk. Choose Suspicious → Shut Trapdoor. This opens the tape and keeps the storm shelter ladder intact. For the Counselor’s Ledger, choose Playful at the desk afterward. Climb Down and the later rung check

Chapter 3

Trouble in Paradise

07 · Pieces of Silver

Find Nick as Abi. Take Call Back?, choose Honest → Cautious and take Kiss?. When he’s attacked, choose Help Nick. Press Up, choose Run and pass the mash. Take Climb Tree?, press Right, pass the climb mash and pass Don’t Breathe. Later silver entries

08 · Finders Keepers

As Ryan, choose Worried → Assertive → Take Shortcut. Press Up → Up → Left and pass the climb mash. Choose Take Shortcut again and press Up → Left → Left → Up → Right. Take Shout? and hit Bobby to add his blood vial to the tape. If you arrive late, the tape still opens but the vial is unavailable. Other tape entries

09 · Distraction

As Jacob, take Call Out? when offered. Pass the struggle mash and choose Pleading. After Bobby cuts Jacob down, let Throw Dirt expire so he marks Jacob with blood. Chapter 4 updates

Chapter 4

Keep Jacob and Emma alive

  1. As Ryan at the firepit, let both aiming windows expire. Jacob is in the bushes. The second window updates Distraction.
  2. As Emma at the treehouse, choose Search Bag, then follow the full escape and pickup steps. Pass the chase prompts. At the crank choose Reel Slowly and let Speed Up? expire. Keeping Emma unbitten avoids the van attack in Chapter 8.

Chapter 5

White Noise

10 · Helping Hand

  1. As Dylan, cross the cabin clearing and talk to Ryan at the radio hut steps. Choose Confident to enter. Choose Interested → Concerned → Apologetic and let the ceiling shot expire.
  2. Choose Aggressive → Confident → Calm. With the cabin opened in Chapter 1, Silas bites Dylan. Choose Desperate → Chainsaw and pass the mash. The amputation stops the bite from progressing. Other tape entries

Chapter 6

Get Jacob, Abi and Nick to Chapter 8

  1. As Jacob, choose Delighted → Apologetic, press Right at the attack, choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe. Choose Pry Open at the bear trap.
  2. At the pool house, hit Nick as Abi during the aiming window. He survives and Abi gets away. The shot updates Pieces of Silver.
  3. As Emma, go uphill from the firepit and let Run? expire when the hunters catch the werewolf. This keeps the van route simple.

Chapter 7

The Past Behind Us

11 · The White Whale

  1. During the first cell exploration, inspect the loose brick by the bed, then use the spoon from the bed. During station exploration, go upstairs to the third office and take the syringe from the locker.
  2. Return to the cell and hide the syringe behind the loose brick. Choose Go to Sleep at the bed. Let Take Gun expire during the poem.
  3. Press Left when Travis comes to check on Laura. She sedates him and opens the tape. If you miss the syringe prompt, use the gun escape and lock him in unharmed. Later tape entries

Chapter 8

The Belly of the Beast

12 · Teambuilding

At the van as Kaitlyn, choose Honest → Encouraging on the unbitten Emma route. Kaitlyn and Dylan leave for the scrapyard. If Emma transforms here, use the van rescue branches to keep Dylan alive. The tape also has a solo scrapyard entry. Later tape entries

13 · Love’s Labours Lost

  1. As Ryan, enter the red-lit passage and press Down at the slippery floor. Let Open Cage? expire. Take Stop Laura when she aims at werewolf Nick, then choose Help Jacob.
  2. Pick breaker 1 → breaker 2 → Continue → breaker 2 → breaker 3. Jacob gets out and Nick stays in the cage. Recover from a wrong pair · Later tape entries

Chapter 9

The Matriarch

14 · A Pound of Flesh

As Laura, let the shotgun mash expire to spare Constance. Choose Run and press Right at her shot. After Bobby stabs Ryan, let Pull Knife? expire. The tape opens before the bedroom exploration. Keep the knife in Ryan’s side and let the later Stab? interrupt expire to spare Bobby. Choose Sympathetic → Accept when Laura offers to bite Ryan and let Pull Away? expire. Other tape entries

Check Pause → Paths for all 15 tapes. Continue with the scrapyard, shelter and attic steps. Hit Chris directly ahead in the attic and choose Suspicious when Travis proposes the hunt.

Chapter 10

Finish the night

Choose Stay as Max. At the lodge and the nest, use the full Chapter 10 walkthrough to save the nine counselors and pick up the last tarot card and evidence. The lodge and the hunt add entries to Teambuilding and The White Whale. For specific later entries, use the tape reference and replay from the earliest choice the entry depends on.
