Before you start

  1. Finish the first achievement run through the credits. From the main menu choose Chapter Select for that completed save. On Steam you can make a copy of the completed save before replaying.
  2. Set Aim Assist to Off and keep directions, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe manual. Pass inputs unless a step calls for a failure. Continue past Death Rewind on planned deaths. Input settings
  3. Each replay replaces the save from the start of the chapter you select. Use the latest completed branch for the next stage. Finish through the credits and return to the main menu to reopen Chapter Select, even when the award appeared earlier. How save snapshots work · When ending awards are checked

The first achievement run sets up the rabbit for the shortest Kaitlyn replay and carries early clues into the Chapter 2 replay. A save from Rough Night with Leave Bags still works for the first two stages. Use the no-rabbit branch for Kaitlyn and the earlier start for all clues.

Use the pickup finder in each full walkthrough to add tarot, evidence or clues. For an award that requires a complete set, pick up every remaining item on the same branch after the chapter you select. Pickup recovery rules

Stage 1 · Start in Chapter 4

Both reunions and Family Matters

Start from the completed survival save. Jacob, Emma and Abi should enter Chapter 4 alive. Follow the full reunion walkthrough between these choices. Optional tarot and evidence

  1. Chapters 4–6. Hold fire at both bushes windows. As Emma, choose Search Bag → Use Taser, press Down, pass the zipline mash, choose Spray and press Up twice. Choose Use Trapdoor and miss Down at the cabinet for the arm bite. Press Right and pass the ladder mash. Choose Reel Slowly and let Speed Up? expire later. In Chapter 6 pass Jacob’s escape prompts and choose Pry Open. Hit Nick to save Abi, and let Emma’s Run? interrupt expire at the hunters so she keeps only the island bite.
  2. Chapters 7–8. Hide the syringe and press Left to sedate Travis. At the van choose Shoot, hit Emma and then hit her on the roof. Choose Regular Shot. Take Stop Laura in the basement and choose Help Jacob. Use breakers 1 → 2 → Continue → 2 → 3. Van rescue and cage steps
  3. Chapter 9. Win Constance’s shotgun mash. Keep Ryan’s knife and stab Bobby so Chris can kill him. Choose Attack and win the mash against Jedediah. As Ryan, Decline Laura’s bite. As Abi, take silver from the shelter fuse box and climb the ladder. After Ryan shoots Chris in the attic, press Up as Laura and hit Travis in the 3.2-second window. Ryan bleeds out. House, crane, silver and attic steps
  4. Chapter 10. Walk Max to the dock for Should’ve Gone to the Motel. As Jacob choose Honest → Remorseful → Apologetic for Nobody’s Fool. At the lodge use Run → Beam, pass the mash or recovery, then Run → Investigate and hit Caleb with silver. The epilogue checks Family Matters before the fate cards. Both reunions and the lodge

For Family Matters without the reunions, start in Chapter 9 and use the hunt branch if Travis is unharmed and someone can take silver to the office. If Max gets the swim choice on the reunion route, Laura missed the dock branch. Use the attic replay after the credits.

Stage 2 · Start in Chapter 4 again

Hackett’s Quarry Massacre and Lovers’ Quarrel

Chapter Select restores the same start of Chapter 4, before the reunion replay’s island bite and station escape. Use the full massacre walkthrough. If you already earned the massacre on a different branch, check these choices before using that save for the sole-survivor replays. Optional pickups on the massacre route

  1. Chapters 4–5. At the firepit, hold fire at the first bushes window and hit Jacob in the second. As Emma, Open Trapdoor at the treehouse. If Dylan is bitten at the hut, choose Desperate → Chainsaw and finish the amputation so the bite stops progressing.
  2. Chapter 6. As Abi, choose Calm → Compassionate and let the pool house shot expire. Nick kills her and Lovers’ Quarrel appears. Pool house and pickup steps
  3. Chapters 7–8. Leave the syringe in the locker and let the early Take Gun interrupt expire during the poem. When Travis comes into Laura’s cell during the escape, press Right → Right and choose Demanding → Shoot Travis. Check Paths → The White Whale for the entry that she shot him. In the cages, let Stop Laura expire so Laura shoots Nick. Full station steps and recoveries
  4. Chapter 9. Accept Laura’s bite as Ryan. At the crane use Warn Kaitlyn, press Up, then choose Sound Horn and fail the first torch mash. Dylan dies and Kaitlyn leaves unbitten. Shoot Chris in the attic. Travis stabs Laura. Choose Aggressive and fail Down so Travis kills Ryan. Crane and attic steps
  5. Chapter 10. As Max choose Swim to Shore. At the lodge choose Prepare for Attack → Wait and let the shot expire. Caleb kills Kaitlyn. Check for Hackett’s Quarry Massacre at the start of the epilogue.

On this branch, Max and Kaitlyn are the only two alive at the start of Chapter 10. Keep this branch through the credits so you can change just the lodge outcome for The Final Girl.

Stage 3a · Start in Chapter 10 with the rabbit

The Final Girl

  1. Select Chapter 10 on the completed massacre branch. The quick route needs Max on the island, Kaitlyn alone at the lodge and Abi’s rabbit from Chapter 1. Check Paths → Down the Rabbit Hole for Abi putting the toy in her bag.
  2. Choose Swim to Shore as Max. At the lodge choose Prepare for Attack → Run → Beam. Pass the mash or Up recovery, then choose Run downstairs.
  3. Pass Don’t Breathe. Choose Freezer → Use Decoy and win the door mash. Kaitlyn locks Caleb in and stays outside. The Final Girl checks before the fate cards. Full rabbit walkthrough · Optional lodge pickups

Without the rabbit, start in Chapter 9. Use the ankle-bite route with Dylan alive and uninfected at the start of the chapter. Fail the crane lever mash after Slam Car to infect Kaitlyn. Repeat Laura, Ryan and Max’s deaths. At the lodge choose Wait and let the shot expire so Caleb kills Dylan. Choose Beam and fail the mash and Up recovery. The bite saves Kaitlyn.

Stage 3b · Start in Chapter 9

Last Man Standing

Start Chapter 9 after either the massacre or The Final Girl. Laura must have shot Travis in Chapter 7. Jacob, Emma, Abi and Nick should already be dead. Dylan should enter the chapter alive and uninfected. Full Ryan-only walkthrough

  1. Keep Ryan’s knife in the bedroom. Check the left door’s keyhole, choose Block Door and pass both mashes. At the wall gap press Left → Right and pass the breathing check. Take Stab when Bobby catches up so he cannot kill Ryan later. Knife recoveries
  2. Choose Sympathetic → Accept and let Pull Away? expire. At the crane use Warn Kaitlyn → Up → Sound Horn and fail the first torch mash again. The Chapter 9 restart restores Dylan even if you killed him later on the Kaitlyn replay.
  3. Shoot Chris in the attic. After Travis stabs Laura, choose Aggressive, pass Down and hit Travis in the 2.9-second window. Chris kills wounded Bobby. Ryan survives.
  4. In Chapter 10, choose Swim to Shore as Max. As Kaitlyn choose Prepare for Attack → Wait and let the shot expire. Continue until Last Man Standing appears at the start of the epilogue.

Stage 4 · Start in Chapter 2 with early clues

Blood Pact, Mutually Assured and Meddling Kids!

Start Chapter 2 on the same completed save if it already has all four prologue clues, Hunting Notice, Camp Plaque and the cabin break-in. Dylan can recover the other three Chapter 1 clues at the cabins. Start in Chapter 1 if you left the cabin locked or missed a lodge clue, or in the prologue if an opening clue is missing. Full combined route · Find a pickup · Clue totals by chapter

  1. Chapters 2–4. Choose Shady Glade and collect Ranger Box before leaving. As Abi, take the bite by failing Don’t Breathe behind the rocks. As Jacob, take Throw Dirt so Bobby’s blood won’t protect him in Chapter 6. Give Emma Max’s island bite. Abi and Jacob · Emma’s island bite
  2. Chapters 5–6. Reach Bobby’s table scene to expose the Ticket Stub. Leave Dylan’s radio-hut bite untreated with Terrified. Miss Jacob’s first Right dodge, then choose Run, pass the mash and choose Pry Open. Shoot Nick to save Abi. As Emma, leave the firework at the firepit and take Run? at the hunters to get her second bite. That moves her transformation to the shelter.
  3. Chapters 7–8. Use the syringe on Travis. Take the station clues and later quarry and distillery clues. At the cages take Stop Laura and free Jacob. Station steps · Quarry and cages
  4. Chapter 9. Accept Laura’s bite as Ryan. Choose Lift Car → Slam Car at the crane and fail the lever mash for Kaitlyn’s bite. With all other infection flags recorded, Blood Pact appears here. At the attic, hold fire as Ryan so Chris kills him and Laura and Travis kill one another. This awards Mutually Assured. Bite, shelter and attic steps
  5. Chapter 10. As Kaitlyn, use Keep Looking at the portrait. Collect the five lodge clues, including the Ticket Stub in the fallen frame. The 40th original clue awards Meddling Kids!. With the ankle bite, use Run → Beam and fail the mash and Up recovery to finish alive.

Shady Glade skips The Hanged Man on this branch. For the full deck, use Rocky Road on the separate tarot route. For Blood Pact alone with a locked cabin, give Dylan and Kaitlyn their bites at the crane and start at the earliest missing bite.
