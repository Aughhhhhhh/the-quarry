Choose an outcome

Use Rough Night for the prologue and shared scenes. Keep Aim Assist Off and play directional prompts, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe manually. Pass inputs unless the steps call for a miss. Continue past Death Rewind on a planned death. Input settings

  • Keep, lose or leave the bitten hand untreated · replay Chapter 5 if Abi already made the cabin choice you need. Otherwise replay Chapter 1.
  • Take Emma’s bite or die at the van · replay Chapter 8 if Emma has only the island bite and Abi has the status you need. Replay Chapter 4 to change the island bite or Chapter 6 to change Abi’s fate.
  • Survive, turn or die at the crane · replay Chapter 9 with Dylan alive. Check his hand and infection before choosing the torch route.
  • Survive or die at the lodge · replay Chapter 10 with Dylan still human after the scrapyard.
  • Reach the kitchen or free Kaitlyn · bring Dylan to the lodge and keep him beside Kaitlyn until they reach the kitchen. For a rescue, give Dylan a van or crane bite.
  • See the cure wording that checks Dylan’s bite · set up the hunt in Chapter 9, then take the crash branch instead of reaching the nest.

If Dylan survives the scrapyard with an untreated Chapter 5 bite, he transforms before the lodge. Bites from the van and crane leave him human through the lodge fight. Chapter Select rules · Dylan as sole survivor

Chapter 1 · Setup through Chapter 3

Choose the cabin

  1. As Abi, press Up when Emma offers a high five. Choose Leave Bags to keep the cabin locked and avoid Dylan’s first bite. Choose Break In for the radio-hut bite, amputation or freezer decoy. Inside, inspect the rabbit on the shelf and choose Keep if you want the decoy.
  2. If Abi broke in, take Nudge? when she returns with her bag and choose Friendly → Supportive → Understanding. Continue through Chapter 2. In Chris’s office choose Suspicious → Shut Trapdoor to keep the shelter ladder intact.
  3. In Chapter 3 use both shortcuts to reach Bobby and shoot him for the blood vial if you want the vial variation at the nest. For Dylan’s routes that skip the hunt entirely, you can proceed without the vial.

Chapter 5

White Noise

Follow Nick and Kaitlyn’s opening scenes. Once you control Dylan, cross the cabin clearing to the radio tower. Collect anything you want before talking to Ryan at the steps.

Optional tarot · The Devil

Walk between the central swing and the picnic tables. Press Interact when the camera shows the side of the table. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Claw Marks

Check the wall beside the fan to the left of the radio-hut entrance. Pickup video

Optional tarot · The Hermit

Go around the corner past the claw marks to the base of the radio tower. Take the card at the camera change. Step away and approach again if it expires. Pickup video

Ryan · Set up the feedback loop

  1. Talk to Ryan and choose Confident to enter. Inside, choose Interested → Concerned → Apologetic. Let the first roof shot expire.
  2. Choose Aggressive → Confident, then Calm at the broken microphone. The cabin choice now determines what happens.
  3. Cabin locked. Ryan goes outside. Let the second aiming window expire. Press Left → Down at the lunges. Dylan keeps both hands and stays uninfected.
  4. Cabin broken into. Silas bites Dylan’s hand. Choose Terrified for an untreated bite. This registers for Blood Pact and sends Dylan toward a transformation at the crane.
  5. Remove the bitten hand. Choose Desperate → Chainsaw and pass the mash. The amputation still happens if the mash fails. To use the shotgun, choose Shotgun and fire once in the six-second base window. Either completed amputation stops this bite and earns Just a Flesh Wound.
  6. Choose Reassuring when the group reunites. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered. Radio conversation and recovery branches

Chapter 8 · Setup in Chapters 6 and 7

Pool house, station and van

  1. In Chapter 6, hit Nick to save Abi if you want to choose ammo at the van. For Dylan’s death inside the van, let Abi’s aiming window expire and Nick kills her.
  2. Let Run? expire when Emma sees the hunters. This preserves her island bite so she transforms at the van. A Chapter 6 bite would move her transformation to the storm shelter.
  3. In Chapter 7, hide the syringe and press Left to sedate Travis. In Chapter 8, use the quarry route. After the van, follow the basement steps to save Nick and free Jacob.

Kaitlyn · Decide what happens to Dylan at the van

  1. With Abi alive, choose Honest and then Shoot when transformed Emma attacks. Hit her during the 2.2-second base window to save Abi and reach the roof shot.
  2. To give Dylan a bite, bring him in uninfected, let the roof shot expire, then hit Emma during the 3.9-second rescue window. Choose Regular Shot to preserve his new bite. Silver Shot or an ammo timeout kills Emma and clears it.
  3. For Dylan’s death outside the van, let the roof shot expire, then withhold fire again after Emma bites him. Outside-the-van recap
  4. For Dylan’s death inside the van, arrive with Abi dead and Dylan uninfected. Choose Optimistic at the lodge, then Run when Emma attacks Dylan. To save him on that setup, choose Help, hit Emma within four seconds and hit her again on the roof. Inside-the-van recap
  5. To keep Dylan unbitten when Abi is alive, hit Emma in the first window and again on the roof. Choose Regular Shot to spare her. If Emma has no island infection active, there is no fight. Choose Encouraging when they discuss the van. All shot timers and outcomes

Chapter 9

Scrapyard, shelter and attic

Use the Hackett house steps through Laura’s fight with Jedediah. For the hunt, accept Laura’s bite as Ryan and let Pull Away? expire. To skip the hunt with Chris dead, decline her bite.

Dylan · Control the crane

  1. Walk right of the wrecks into the garage. Use the gate switch near the downstairs stairs. Go through the gate and climb the metal steps onto the containers.
Optional tarot · Judgement

Cross the container roofs and press Interact at the camera change before descending. Pickup video

  1. Go down the far stairs, through the wrecked bus, then left to the yellow crane. Choose Encouraging → Lift Car.
  2. Keep Dylan’s current state. Choose Slam Car. Pass the lever mash to keep Kaitlyn unbitten, or fail it for her ankle bite. A Dylan with a van bite still goes to the lodge. With an untreated radio-hut bite, he transforms before the lodge. Infected woods recap · Cured woods recap
  3. Take the crane bite. Start with Dylan uninfected and both hands intact. Choose Sound Horn, pass the torch mash, then let Left expire so Silas bites Dylan. Pass the recovery mash so he gets the torch back. Pass the lever mash to keep Kaitlyn unbitten or fail it to give her a bite too.
  4. Die at the crane. Start with Dylan uninfected. Choose Sound Horn and let the first torch mash expire. This kills him with either two hands or an earlier amputation. An existing bite protects him at this prompt. Two-hand recap · Amputation recap

Abi · Bring silver to the office

Inspect the lit fuse box if you want a silver shot in Chapter 10. Use the ladder to take it to Chris’s office. For an unbitten Emma alone or a shelter transformation, follow the shelter walkthrough.

Optional tarot · The Hierophant

Approach the stairs to reveal the card before using the separate ladder. If the prompt expires, back away and return. It leads to Eliza’s flashback. Press Left at the flying stake. Pickup video

Ryan · Set up the hunt or leave Silas alive

  • To kill Silas later, hit Chris directly ahead with Ryan bitten and Travis unharmed. Choose Suspicious. For the radio-hut cure and the hall or kitchen attack cure, the nest shot works. Use the crash shot for the recaps that need Dylan’s bite flag.
  • To keep Chris and Silas alive, aim right and shoot Laura. Chris kills Ryan, Travis escapes and the hunt is skipped. This is the setup for the infected rabbit-trap and paired freezer rescue recaps with a van bite.
  • To keep Silas alive with Chris dead, decline Ryan’s bite before the attic, then shoot Chris. Let Up expire during Laura’s shotgun grab. Travis captures Laura and the hunt is skipped.

Chapter 10

Lodge, kitchen and the cure

Choose Stay as Max if the swim choice appears. As Kaitlyn, climb to the lodge’s upper floor. At the portrait, choose Keep Looking if you still want pickups, then Prepare for Attack when ready. With Chris alive, an uninfected Jacob can escape the woods chase.

Optional tarot · The High Priestess

Use the stairs by the bunk room and approach the top-floor windows. Take the card at the camera change before preparing for the attack. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Trail Cam Footage

Inspect the boxes by the couch on the upper floor before preparing for the attack. Pickup video

If Dylan already transformed or died, Kaitlyn fights alone. Use the silver-shot route to save her, or use the beam fall if she took the lever bite. Continue to the hunt if you set it up in the attic.

Kaitlyn · Keep Dylan with you or let Caleb catch him

  1. Choose Run to keep Dylan with Kaitlyn. If you choose Wait, hit Caleb within 1.6 seconds to stun him and continue together.
  2. With uninfected Dylan present, choose Wait and let the shot expire to get him killed. Opening death recap
  3. With Dylan bitten at the crane and Kaitlyn unbitten, choose Wait and let the shot expire. Caleb spares Dylan and Kaitlyn goes upstairs alone. Choose Beam, then let the mash and Up expire. Dylan’s hall recap depends on Silas’s fate. Choosing Window and missing Down can skip Dylan’s card. Still infected · Cured

Kaitlyn · Send Dylan into the classroom

Use Run → Beam and pass the balance mash or the Up recovery. Choose Hide upstairs and fail Don’t Breathe. Caleb goes after Kaitlyn while Dylan gets away. Use an uninfected Dylan, a crane bite with Silas alive, or a crane bite with the crash cure for the three cards. Uninfected · Still infected · Cured

Kaitlyn · Investigate the office for silver

  1. With Abi ready to pass silver, use Run → Beam and pass the mash or the Up recovery. Choose Run downstairs and Investigate when Abi knocks. There are three base seconds to shoot Caleb. If the choice expires before the handoff, Caleb catches Kaitlyn and Dylan survives.
  2. With Dylan uninfected, hit Caleb for Dylan’s refuge card. Missing can produce the same Dylan card while putting Kaitlyn at risk. Uninfected refuge recap
  3. With Dylan carrying the crane bite, hold fire for the infected refuge and keep Silas alive. For the cured refuge, hit Caleb with the silver and take the crash cure. Still infected · Cured

Kaitlyn · Reach the kitchen together

Keep Dylan beside Kaitlyn with Run → Beam and a successful mash or Up recovery. Choose Run downstairs. If you get the office choice, choose Run again to continue to the kitchen.

  1. Let Caleb catch them. Fail Don’t Breathe at the counter. Dylan dies if uninfected. With the crane bite he survives. Kaitlyn also needs her own bite to survive. Leave Silas alive for Dylan’s infected kitchen card or kill Silas for the cured version. Kitchen death · Still infected · Cured
  2. Trap Caleb with the rabbit. Pass Don’t Breathe and choose Freezer → Use Decoy. Pass the door mash. With Dylan uninfected, the recap credits the trap for his survival. For the infected and cured variations, take the van bite. Leave Chris and Silas alive for the infected variant, or shoot Chris and use the crash cure for the cured variant. Caleb freezes if Silas dies. Uninfected · Still infected · Cured
  3. Let Dylan free Kaitlyn. Bring Dylan with a late bite and pass Don’t Breathe. Choose Freezer. With the rabbit, choose Hide. Without it, Kaitlyn hides immediately. Caleb spares Dylan, who opens the door. For the paired recap with uninfected Kaitlyn, take the crane bite on Dylan alone and keep Silas alive. For the paired recaps with both bitten, use Dylan’s van bite and Kaitlyn’s lever bite. Keep Chris and Silas alive for the cursed pair, or shoot Chris and use the crash cure for the cured pair. Kaitlyn rescued · Both cured · Both still infected

Laura · Kill Silas after the crash

  1. Set up the hunt by shooting Chris with Ryan bitten and Travis unharmed. Choose Suspicious when Travis offers to work together.
  2. In the car choose Inquisitive, press Right and choose Grab Gun → Take Cover. Let the first Down expire at the handbrake. When Silas reaches Travis, let the second Down expire too.
  3. After the crash, pass the mash to reach the gun, then press Up to shoot Silas. Travis, Ryan and Laura die on this branch. The epilogue records Silas’s death while the earlier Dylan bite flag is still available. Crash shot recap and footage · Why the two cures differ in the credits
