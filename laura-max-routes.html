Choose an outcome

Set Aim Assist to Off and keep directional prompts, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe on manual. Pass prompts unless a step calls for a miss. Continue past Death Rewind on planned deaths. Replay forward from the earliest chapter where your save needs a different choice. Input settings · Chapter Select rules

Laura

  • Kills Silas at the nest · reach Chapter 10 with the hunt set up, then shoot Silas.
  • Reunites with Max or is locked in the basement · start at Chapter 9 with Travis unharmed and have Ryan decline the bite.
  • Survives with the vial · start at Chapter 3 if Ryan lacks the vial. Otherwise start at Chapter 10 with the hunt set up.
  • Kills Silas after the crash and dies or dies before firing · start at Chapter 10 with the hunt set up.
  • Travis stabs Laura in revenge · shoot Travis in Chapter 7, then Chris in Chapter 9.
  • Dies fighting Travis or is shot by Ryan · replay Chapter 9 and leave Chris alive.
  • Killed by Silas at the nest · reach Chapter 10 with the hunt set up and leave Laura unprotected.

Max

  • Cured and stays on the island, waits for Laura or dies swimming · start at Chapter 10 with Chris dead and the dock choice available.
  • Reunites with Laura · start at Chapter 9 with Travis unharmed and follow Laura’s reunion route.
  • Remains infected · replay Chapter 9 and leave Chris alive.

For the hunt, leave Travis uninjured during the Chapter 7 escape. Have Ryan accept Laura’s bite, shoot Chris and agree to the hunt in the Chapter 9 attic scene. Laura as sole survivor · Max as sole survivor

Pick the escape branch

  1. Choose Honest → Confident when Travis returns. Let Take Gun expire during the poem. Choose Confused → Encouraging → Resigned → Empathetic, then press Up to listen to Travis and Chris.
  2. Syringe, Travis unharmed. With the syringe hidden, press Left as Travis comes in to check Laura.

Pick the attic target and follow through

As Laura, approach Chris. After the fall, you control Ryan’s shotgun. The base aiming window is 4.1 seconds. Chris is directly ahead and transformed Laura is farther right. Target comparison

  • Laura and Travis kill each other. Let the shot expire. Chris kills Ryan and the fight between Travis and transformed Laura earns Mutually Assured. Max stays infected. Laura’s fight recap
  • Ryan shoots Laura. Aim farther right and hit transformed Laura. Chris kills Ryan and Max stays infected. Laura’s shooting recap
  • Travis stabs Laura. After shooting Travis during the escape, hit Chris as Ryan. Travis stabs Laura when she turns human. Ryan’s next scene depends on the bite and Bobby. Ryan’s follow-up · Laura’s revenge recap
  • Travis and Laura fight for the gun. With Travis unharmed and Ryan unbitten, hit Chris. Ryan bleeds out and you control Laura’s next prompt. Follow the reunion or capture steps below.
  • Laura and Travis work together. With Travis unharmed and Ryan bitten, hit Chris. Choose Suspicious when Travis asks for help. Inquisitive also works. This earns Above the Law and opens the Silas hunt.

Reunite Laura and Max

  1. After Ryan bleeds out, press Up as Laura to grab the gun. Hit Travis within the 3.2-second base aiming window.
  2. In Chapter 10, walk Max down the island trail to the dock. Laura meets him there automatically in place of the dock choice. This earns Should’ve Gone to the Motel. Laura’s reunion recap · Max’s reunion recap

Leave Laura locked in the basement

After Ryan bleeds out, let Laura’s Up prompt expire. If she grabs the gun, a miss or timeout also gives Travis the upper hand. He locks Laura in the basement alive. When you reach Max’s dock choice, choose Stay to keep him safe. Laura’s capture recap

Chapter 10

Max · Island and dock

If Ryan shot Chris, Max wakes up human. Follow the trail from the treehouse to the dock. You can inspect the old firepit and Laura’s clothes along the way. If Max killed Emma in Chapter 4, follow the trail past her body to see her phone. Max sees the attack photo if Emma took it and a video otherwise.

  • Stay on the island. Choose Stay. For the standard recap, keep Laura alive through the rest of the game. Max’s island recap
  • Wait for Laura. With the hunt set up, choose Stay, then take the fatal crash route. The game checks Laura’s death when the epilogue starts. Max’s waiting recap and version notes
  • Swim to shore. Choose Swim to Shore to leave the island. Caleb kills Max as he climbs out. Max’s swimming recap
  • Stay infected. At the Chapter 9 attic shot, let time run out or shoot Laura so Chris survives. Max’s playable island scene is skipped. The audited PC build selects his infected recap even if Kaitlyn later kills Caleb. Max’s infected recap · Caleb route

If Kaitlyn reaches the lodge alive, use her lodge walkthrough between the island and the hunt. Collect before choosing Prepare for Attack at the portrait.

Chapter 10 · Silas hunt

Laura · Car attack and Silas’s nest

Reach the handbrake

  1. In the car choose Inquisitive. Press Right when Silas attacks. Choose Grab Gun → Take Cover.
  2. Press Down to pull the handbrake and stop safely. If you miss it, another Down saves Travis and still gets everyone out. For the crash, let both prompts expire. Car attack alternatives
  • Kill Silas after the crash. After failing both car prompts, pass the crawl mash and press Up to shoot Silas. Laura dies from her injuries and the curse ends. Laura’s final-shot recap
  • Die before firing. After failing both car prompts, let the crawl mash expire. Missing the final Up after a successful mash also works. Silas kills Laura and the curse persists. Laura’s crash death recap

Choose what happens at the nest

  1. After stopping safely choose Determined. If Ryan collected the vial, choose Take Vial for Laura’s protection or Give to Ryan for his.
  2. Choose Probing → Reflective on the way to the nest. At the cage take Raise Gun? in the six-second base window.
  • Shoot Silas. Hit him in the cage within the aiming window, about eight seconds on base timing. Laura, Ryan and Travis survive, the curse ends and you earn The White Wolf. Laura’s nest shot recap
  • Save Laura with the vial. Choose Take Vial after the car and hold fire through the entire aiming window at the nest. Silas kills Ryan and Travis and spares Laura. Laura’s vial recap
  • Let Silas kill Laura. Give the vial to Ryan if he offers it, then hold fire through the entire aiming window. Silas kills Laura and Travis. Ryan survives if protected. Laura’s nest death recap · Travis struggle variants
