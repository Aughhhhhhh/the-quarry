Choose an outcome

Use Rough Night for the prologue and shared scenes. Set Aim Assist to Off and keep directional prompts, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe on manual. Pass prompts unless a step calls for a miss. Continue past Death Rewind on planned deaths. Input settings

  • Take Abi’s bite for Blood Pact · replay Chapter 3. Nick is bitten on every branch.
  • Save Abi or earn Lovers’ Quarrel · replay Chapter 6 for the shotgun decision.
  • Save Abi from Emma at the van, or let Emma kill her · replay Chapter 8 with Abi alive and Emma carrying only Max’s bite. Replay Chapter 4 to take that bite or Chapter 6 to avoid replacing it.
  • Get Abi to the office or lose her in the shelter · replay Chapter 9 with Abi alive. Replay Chapter 6 to set up Emma’s shelter bite, or Chapter 2 to change the ladder rung.
  • Spare Nick or let Laura shoot him · replay Chapter 8.
  • Cure Nick or leave him infected · replay Chapter 10 if Nick survived the cage and your lodge or hunt setup is ready. Replay Chapter 9 to change the hunt.

Chapter 3

Trouble in Paradise

Abi · Find Nick and choose whether she is bitten

Follow the trail after taking control of Abi. If you missed the Trail Camera in Chapter 2, look for its Chapter 3 pickup before moving on.

Optional tarot · The Tower

Watch for the camera pointing up into the canopy on the main trail. Take the card before meeting Nick. Pickup video

  1. Take Call Back? when Nick calls. Choose Honest → Cautious and take Kiss? for the kiss. The attack happens either way.
  2. After Nick is bitten, choose Help Nick, press Up and choose Run. Pass the chase mash. Let Climb Tree? expire.
  3. Behind the rocks, pass Don’t Breathe to keep Abi unbitten. Release early or run out of breath to register Kaylee’s bite for Blood Pact. Missing the log dodge on the Hide route also registers a bite. Failing the tree recovery can show a shoulder bite and an infected map marker while leaving the infection state and Blood Pact counter unchanged. Chase inputs and infection audit

Ryan · Reach Nick and get the vial

  1. Finish Jacob’s lake scene, then choose Worried → Assertive as Ryan. Choose Take Shortcut at the cliff. Press Up, Up, Left and pass the climb mash.
  2. At the river, choose Take Shortcut again. Press Up, Left, Left at the stones, then Up, Right at the stairs. The full sequence earns Nick of Time.
  3. When you see Bobby holding Nick, take Shout? and hit Bobby in the 7.5-second base window. He survives and Ryan picks up the blood vial. A miss or timeout still brings Nick back. If you arrive after Bobby leaves and see only bushes, hold fire for Nick to emerge safely. Slower arrivals and recovery inputs
  4. Finish Jacob’s snare scene and choose Move On if Eliza offers a reading.

Chapter 6 · Chapter 7 follows

Abi · The pool house

  1. Finish Jacob’s walkway scene if he is alive. As Kaitlyn outside the pool, choose Compassionate, take Intervene and choose Help Nick to pull him out.
  2. Once you control Abi, get any pickups before checking Nick. Campers Photo is on the main-room board and Spooky Drawing is in a locker at the end of the changing-room corridor. The two clues and checking Nick each count toward ending exploration. Get The Moon before finishing those interactions.
Optional tarot · The Moon

Enter the third changing stall on the left and walk toward its back wall. The prompt has a 2.5-second base timer. If you miss it, leave the stall and re-enter. Pickup video

Abi · Hit Nick or let the shot expire

  1. Check Nick, then choose Calm → Compassionate. Nick throws Abi and she reaches the shotgun.
  2. Hit Nick in the 6.5-second base window. Abi survives. Nick transforms and escapes, then turns up in the Chapter 8 cage.
  3. For Lovers’ Quarrel, let the shot expire. Missing also works. Nick kills Abi even if she took the Chapter 3 bite. Pool-house death recap

Emma · Set the next encounter

At the hunters, let Run? expire for the van death route or a human Emma. To make Emma turn in the shelter, take Run? without the firework or miss Up if you picked it up. The later bite takes priority over any island bite. Firework and forest steps

Finish as Ryan with Reassuring → Suspicious → Interested. In Chapter 7 use the syringe escape to keep Travis unharmed for the hunt. The station’s tarot and evidence steps are unchanged.

Chapter 8

The van and Nick’s cage

Follow Laura and Ryan’s pool-house and quarry steps until Kaitlyn checks the van. Laura makes a silver shell from Abi’s bracelet if Abi is alive.

Kaitlyn · Save Abi from Emma

  1. With Abi alive, choose Honest. If Emma has only the Chapter 4 bite, she bursts out of the van and charges Abi.
  2. To save Abi, choose Shoot and hit Emma within 2.2 seconds. Hit Emma again on the roof, then choose Regular Shot to drive her off. To get the van death, choose Run at Emma’s first charge. Abi dies if the first shot misses or times out too. Van death recap · All shot windows
  3. If Emma is human at the van, choose Encouraging when the group discusses the van. Abi and Emma head to the shelter if both are alive.

Ryan · Decide whether Nick lives

At the basement, get the pickups before going through the red-lit doorway.

Optional tarot · The Empress

Turn left into the distillery and take the card at the camera change. The Rum Still clue is here too. Pickup video

Optional tarot · The Emperor

Return to the entrance and go straight along the other corridor. Take the card before entering the red-lit room. Pickup video

  1. Press Down on the slippery floor. Let Open Cage? expire if offered.
  2. Take Stop Laura? within the six-second base window to save Nick. Let it expire for Laura to kill him and skip the breaker puzzle. The interrupt is available even if Jacob died earlier. Cage death recap
  3. If Nick is alive and Jacob is in the next cage, choose Help Jacob and select 1 + 2 → Continue → 2 + 3. This frees Jacob and keeps Nick’s cage shut. Breaker outcomes and recoveries

Chapter 9

Storm shelter and attic

Follow the house steps. At the crane, use Kaitlyn’s lever bite to save her and Caleb by the beam route, or keep the lever mash successful to leave Kaitlyn unbitten for the silver shot.

Abi · Get to Chris’s office

  1. In the earlier shelter conversation, choose Curious → Friendly if Emma is there. As Abi, inspect the lit fuse box for silver if the Chapter 8 shell was spent or you want a spare. Finish your pickups before taking the ladder.
Optional tarot · The Hierophant

Approach the stairs for the camera change and take the card. Step away and return if the prompt expires. The separate ladder ends exploration. Pickup video

  1. With Emma human and unbitten, use the ladder to reach Chris’s office with her. If Emma died or turned at the van, Abi can climb alone. A loosened rung does not require an escape input on these branches. Office survival recap
  2. With Emma’s Chapter 6 bite, choose Concerned when she starts turning. Choose Ladder. If Dylan chose Climb Down in Chapter 2, pass the mash when the rung breaks. If he chose Shut Trapdoor, Abi climbs out without it. Shelter escape recap
  3. For the shelter death, choose Stairs while Emma turns. If the rung was loosened, choosing Ladder and failing the recovery mash also kills Abi. This removes her from the silver handoff. Shelter death recap

Ryan · Decide whether to hunt Silas

For Nick’s Silas cure, accept Laura’s bite earlier in the house, let Pull Away? expire, and shoot Chris in the attic. Choose Suspicious with Travis unharmed. To keep Silas alive, decline Laura’s bite and shoot Chris, then let Laura’s Up grab expire. Travis captures her and the hunt is skipped. Attic shot and recovery details

If you took The Hierophant, press Left at the flying stake in Eliza’s flashback. Otherwise choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 10

Nick · Cure the infection or let it persist

Choose Stay as Max if the swim choice appears. As Kaitlyn, finish exploring the lodge before choosing Prepare for Attack at the upstairs chimney portrait.

Optional tarot · The High Priestess

Use the stairs by the bunk room and approach the top-floor windows before preparing for the attack. Take the card at the camera change. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Trail Cam Footage

Inspect the boxes by the couch on the upper floor before starting the attack. Pickup video

  1. Caleb cure. Keep Silas alive. With Abi or unbitten Emma in the office with silver, choose Run → Beam, pass the mash or Up recovery, then choose Run → Investigate. Hit Caleb in the three-second base aiming window. Nick’s cure works even if Chris was spared. Caleb cure recap
  2. Silas cure. With Kaitlyn bitten at the crane, choose Run → Beam and let both the mash and Up expire. Kaitlyn and Caleb survive. Complete the car and hunt steps, take Raise Gun? and shoot Silas. Silas cure recap
  3. Nick remains infected. Skip the hunt and use the same bitten Kaitlyn beam fall to spare Caleb. Nick lives as long as you stopped Laura at the cage. Infected recap
