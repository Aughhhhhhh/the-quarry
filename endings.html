Silas’s car attack and crashHandbrake, rescue and roadside endings

Inquisitive and Accusatory change Laura’s conversation with Travis. Both lead into the same car attack.

Opening dodge
Pass the first prompt (Right) to see Grab Gun or Take Wheel. If you miss it, the scene goes straight to Take Cover or Grab Gun. You can still save everyone from there.
Reach the handbrake
Take Wheel and win the mash, or choose Take Cover when it appears. Both routes lead to the handbrake prompt (Down). Pass it to stop the car safely. Letting the first choice expire selects Take Wheel. Letting the second expire selects Take Cover.
Save Travis after Silas breaks through the roof
Fail the wheel mash, miss the handbrake, or choose Grab Gun when Take Cover is offered to reach this prompt (Down). Pass it to save Travis and continue the hunt. Fail it and Silas kills Travis, the car crashes and Ryan dies.
After the crash
Win the mash to reach the gun, then pass the directional prompt (Up) for Laura to shoot Silas before dying from her injuries. Failing the mash or the final prompt lets Silas kill Laura and leaves the curse active. Laura’s crash recaps · Crash headline

How the ending changesFate cards, the curse and the podcast
  1. Creative director Will Byles described 186 variations across the characters’ stories. A single playthrough can combine several character outcomes, scene variants and news headlines. What the epilogue catalog counts · Will Byles on the character variations
  2. Shooting Chris cures Max and Laura. Other infections can persist until their own source dies. Killing Silas ends the remaining curse, although the trapped Kaitlyn recap can still show her as infected. See who infected each counselor
  3. A failed car escape can end the hunt before the nest. At the nest, holding fire can let Silas kill members of the party. Blood collected from Bobby in Chapter 3 can protect one person. The vial and the later gun struggle change who makes it out.
  4. The lodge has a separate outcome from the hunt. Shooting Caleb with silver kills him. Trapping him can leave him alive if the curse persists, or cause him to freeze if he is cured while still locked in. Infected counselors may be ignored during an attack.
  5. Eliza’s final speech depends on Silas, Caleb and whether the hunt reaches the nest. The epilogue shows who survived, died or remained infected. The survivors and evidence affect the podcast and headlines. Eliza’s four speeches · Find your closing newspaper · Newspaper rules and images
