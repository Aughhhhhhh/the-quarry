Before you start

The first story run targets 28 awards, including Rough Night, Peanut Butter Butterpops!, Hard Pass, all 22 tarot cards and all ten evidence items. Two short unlocks and four Chapter Select stages cover the other 12 shared awards.

  1. In the main menu, set Settings → Accessibility → Aim Assist → Auto before starting a fresh interactive game. Keep it on through the first epilogue. Auto handles the intended gunshots. The route calls out when to let an aiming window expire.
  2. Keep directional prompts, button mashes and interrupts on manual timer settings. Use Don’t Breathe → Default to record the five passes. Hold the prompt and release when the danger clears. Pass each input unless the steps call for a miss. Accessibility settings
  3. Follow the tarot and evidence steps for their awards. Pick up at least one card before each Eliza visit through Chapter 9. Choose Move On at every vision offer. Take The Hierophant for the Chapter 9 flashback and its refusal credit.

Choose Break In and keep Abi’s rabbit in Chapter 1. Later, amputate Dylan’s bitten hand and use silver at the lodge. These steps set up the later trophy replays and leave all nine counselors alive on the first run.

Choose between the full tarot deck and all 40 clues
  • Decked Out and Hard Pass. Follow the main route and take The Hanged Man on Rocky Road. Take the Trail Camera as Abi in Chapter 3. You can collect 39 clues if you open every clue step. Get the Ranger Box on the all-clues replay.
  • Meddling Kids! with all nine alive. Use Shady Glade in Chapter 2 and take the Ranger Box and Trail Camera. Open every clue step through the lodge. The Chapter 5 table scene still exposes the Ticket Stub, and the rest of the survival route stays the same. On the audited PC build, this route has 21 tarot cards and skips the Chapter 2 refusal credit. Replay from Chapter 2 on Rocky Road for Decked Out and Hard Pass, taking the later cards and refusal credits again.

All ten evidence items are available on either trail. Use the chapter totals to spot a missing pickup before your next replay.
