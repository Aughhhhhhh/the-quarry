Before using a life

  1. Finish the story once or use the Deluxe Bonus Content Pack for immediate access. Turn on Settings → Accessibility → Death Rewind. An interactive single-player run gives you three lives. Wolf Pack and Couch Co-op exclude this setting. 2K · unlock conditions
  2. When Retry? appears, read the counselor’s name. Choose Use a life to retry. For a longer rewind, check the destination named in the warning before confirming.
  3. Replay the choices and pickups from the restored checkpoint onward. If you want to keep the death for a route, cancel the retry. If there is no usable retry, finish the run and use Chapter Select from the chapter where the outcome can still change.

The entries below trace the Steam PC checkpoint writes and the death requests that use them. Several deaths can use different points within the same scene. Use the destination shown by your game before committing to a long rewind.

Chapter 3 · The lake

Jacob drowns. The save is in the underwater sequence before the wire choice, including a later checkpoint after an initial pull. Choose Detangle as soon as it appears. Dive choices and timers

Chapter 4 · The bushes and the island

Ryan shoots Jacob
The firepit saves when Ryan takes aim. Hold fire through both windows and let Jacob step out of the bushes. Which shot can kill him
Emma dies opening the trapdoor
The treehouse saves on entry. Choose Search Bag → Use Taser. For a death later in the chase, expect a closer checkpoint such as the walkway or Emma’s return to the treehouse. Use the recovery input for the prompt on screen. Treehouse recovery inputs
Emma falls from the second zipline
The late zipline has separate saves for the jump and the reel-in branch. If it returns to Jump, press Up to catch the handle. If it returns to reeling in, press Right when Max attacks. Zipline choices

Chapter 6 · Jacob and Abigail

Jacob falls from the walkway while alone
The death requests a Jacob rewind. The audited walkway scene does not establish a nearby Jacob checkpoint for this branch. Check the destination before accepting. On a Chapter 6 replay press Down at the attack. If you miss it, pass the mash to pull him up. Both opening dodges
Jacob dies after choosing Pull Free
The trap sequence saves as Jacob takes Pull Free. If you retry that branch, press Up to avoid the second trap. If you’re replaying the whole chapter, Pry Open avoids that extra prompt. Trap outcomes
Nick kills Abi at the pool house
The save is in the confrontation before the shot. As Abi, hit Nick before the timer expires. He survives the blast and Abi escapes. Shotgun outcomes

Chapters 8–9 · The van and the cages

Emma kills Abi at the van
The scene saves when Emma is revealed. Choose Shoot and hit Emma before she reaches Abi. Opening van choices
Kaitlyn kills Emma with silver
The save is at the ammunition sequence. Choose Regular Shot before the choice expires. Silver kills Emma. Ammo choice and timer
Emma kills Dylan at the van
With Abi already dead, the van scene saves when Emma bursts out. Choose Help and hit her. If Dylan took a bite after the roof shot, the save is at that bite. Hit Emma in the rescue window. Van recovery inputs
Laura shoots Nick
The basement saves before the shot, with or without Jacob. Take Stop Laura? as she raises the gun. Cage prompt and timer
Nick kills Jacob in the cages
Jacob’s saves are in the Chapter 8 basement, including after Ryan stops Laura. Choose Help Jacob and use 1 + 2 → Continue → 2 + 3. This also fixes the later death after the Chapter 9 power cut. Breaker solution and one-shock recovery

Chapter 9 · Scrapyard, shelter and attic

Dylan dies when his missing hand stops him reaching the torch
This death requests the crane retry saved after Sound Horn. Light the torch and pass Left to fend Silas off. On a full Chapter 9 replay, Lift Car → Slam Car skips that fight. Torch and hand branches
Kaitlyn dies at the scrapyard while alone
The solo fight writes checkpoints before its opening choice, during the car sequence and after Kaitlyn pulls the support bar. Choose Gun and hit Silas, or hit him from the car if you’re already hiding. Pass the bar mash and press Left at the falling scrap. Solo inputs and shot windows
Emma kills Abi in the shelter
After a stairs death, retry the escape and choose Ladder. After a broken-rung death, the scene has a closer checkpoint at the rung. Pass the mash as Abi climbs. Shelter and rung conditions
Chris kills Ryan, or Ryan hits Laura in the attic
The fight saves as Ryan spots the gun and on the later crawl branch. Aim directly ahead and hit Chris. Laura is farther right. Attic targets and recovery
Ryan shoots Travis, then bleeds out
The revenge branch requests a named checkpoint at Laura’s bite offer. Choose Accept. If Bobby is still alive, he can kill Ryan after Ryan shoots Travis, so check the earlier knife and Stab setup before trying to win the revenge branch.
Travis kills Ryan after stabbing Laura
The fight saves at Travis’s lunge. Press Down and hit Travis. Ryan needs Laura’s bite and Bobby must already be mortally wounded for Ryan to survive this branch. Laura’s death precedes this checkpoint. Ryan’s fight and earlier requirements
Travis stabs Laura
An Xbox Series X playthrough guide reports a rewind to Chapter 7. The audited PC death requests a named checkpoint whose matching save was not found in the inspected assets. If the retry is unavailable, finish and use Chapter Select from Chapter 7 to sedate Travis or lock him up unharmed. Xbox replay notes

Chapter 10 · Island, woods and lodge

Caleb kills Max on the mainland
The save is at Max’s walk to the pier. Choose Stay when you reach the swim decision. Island walkthrough
Jacob dies while Chris chases him
The scene saves Jacob as he runs toward the snare. If the retry returns to that approach, press Up. If the earlier chase is still ahead, choose Run and pass the mash, or choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe. Woods chase inputs
Infected Jacob kills Emma in the woods
The scene requests an Emma rewind. The reunion checkpoint itself is for Jacob, so check the destination before accepting. To preserve the infected reunion and save Emma on a replay, have Jacob pick up her bracelet in Chapter 1. Bracelet and reunion requirements
Caleb kills Kaitlyn or Dylan at the first lodge shot
Choosing Wait writes a checkpoint for both. If the retry returns to the shot, hit Caleb in the 1.6-second base window. If the choice is still available, Run skips the shot. Opening shot and outcomes
Unbitten Kaitlyn falls from the beam
Missing the beam mash writes a checkpoint as Kaitlyn slips. If she falls and the retry returns to that point, press Up to catch the beam. Lodge recovery options
Caleb catches Kaitlyn upstairs
The chase saves after Kaitlyn crosses the attic and again when she chooses to hide. If the choice is available, choose Run to head downstairs. If she’s already hiding, pass Don’t Breathe. On the window route, the save is at the traversal and the roof dodge is Down. Attic and bunk-room branches
Caleb kills Kaitlyn after she takes silver
The office scene saves at the shell handoff. Aim at Caleb and hit him within the three-second base window. Silver-shot walkthrough

Kitchen and freezer deaths

Caleb catches a counselor, or Kaitlyn hides and a counselor dies
With the rabbit available, the corresponding retry uses the kitchen-entry checkpoint. Without the rabbit, some deaths request the Chapter 1 cabin checkpoint. If the warning names Chapter 1 and you accept it, choose Break In, inspect the stuffed rabbit and choose Keep. Exact rabbit pickup
Kaitlyn dies after using the decoy
Going for the toy writes a closer checkpoint. On the retry, pass the freezer-door mash so Kaitlyn stays outside. Freezer choices and survival conditions

Car crash and Silas’s nest

Ryan or Laura dies after the crash
The car sequence writes a checkpoint for both after a failed handbrake input. If the retry returns to the struggle over Travis, press Down to save him and stop the crash. Recover from other car inputs
Silas kills Laura or Ryan at the nest
The hunt saves as Silas appears. Take Raise Gun? if it’s still available and shoot Silas. If Travis is already lunging, pass Right and aim at Silas in the cage. All nest branches
