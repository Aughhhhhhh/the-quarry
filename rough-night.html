Before you start

Start an interactive single-player game. Use manual directional prompts, button mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe. Set Aim Assist to Off. Pass each prompt unless the steps call for a miss or a timeout. During Don’t Breathe, release when the threat has turned away and the red danger effect clears.

Dialogue in these steps gives you one consistent path. Directions such as Up refer to the on-screen quick-time prompt. Let it expire means wait without pressing anything.

This route uses Leave Bags in Chapter 1 and deliberately gets Kaitlyn bitten in Chapter 9. You can collect all 22 tarot cards and all 10 evidence items along the way. The full clue set needs a different Chapter 2 path and the cabin break-in. Clue achievement requirements

  1. Press Up at the opening prompt. Choose Leave Bags. This keeps the cabin locked and lets Dylan avoid his radio-hut bite in Chapter 5.

Inspect the big tree next to the central swing while playing as Abi. Dylan’s visit in Chapter 5 is too late for the evidence. Pickup video

  1. Talk to Emma and choose Enthusiastic once you’re ready. Press Right at the buggy prompt, choose Scenic Route, then Supportive.

Back at the lodge · Chris and the keys

  1. Choose Questioning when the group asks about the missing bags. Then choose Friendly → Supportive → Understanding. Leaving the bags skips the bag-carrying Nudge? interrupt.
