Before you start

Start an interactive single-player game. Use manual directional prompts, button mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe. Set Aim Assist to Off. Pass each prompt unless the steps call for a miss or a timeout. During Don’t Breathe, release when the threat has turned away and the red danger effect clears.

Dialogue in these steps gives you one consistent path. Directions such as Up refer to the on-screen quick-time prompt. Let it expire means wait without pressing anything. If a scene changes after a missed prompt, check the recovery steps.

Choose Rocky Road in Chapter 2 for all 22 tarot cards. The Ranger Box needs Shady Glade instead. All ten evidence items work on either trail. Both versions deliberately get Kaitlyn bitten in Chapter 9 to save her and Caleb.

Alternate route · Collect all 40 clues and save everyone
  1. In Chapter 1 choose Break In and collect the newspaper. Replay Chapter 1 if the cabin stayed locked. Start at Chapter 2 if the cabin was opened and you have the earlier clues. Dylan can recover the newspaper and the two outdoor cabin clues in Chapter 5. Start earlier if you missed other pickups.
  2. In Chapter 2 choose Shady Glade for the Ranger Box. You can get 21 tarot cards on this branch. For the full deck, replay Chapter 2 on Rocky Road and collect every later card again.
  3. In Chapter 5 use Kaitlyn’s table encounter to expose the Ticket Stub. When Dylan gets bitten at the radio hut, choose Desperate → Chainsaw so he stays available for the crane and lodge.
  4. Open the clue steps as you play and collect all five lodge clues before preparing for the attack. The 40th clue awards Meddling Kids! Follow the same beam and Silas steps to finish Rough Night.
