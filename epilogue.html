02 / AFTER DAWN

Eliza and the headlines

Eliza’s final speechFour documented branches

Use the first condition that matches your ending.

  1. Silas dies. Eliza threatens the player after losing her son. Killing him after the crash also counts. Kill Silas
  2. Laura reaches the nest and Silas lives. Eliza gloats that the attempt failed. Spare Silas at the nest
  3. Caleb dies and the hunt never reaches the nest. Eliza thanks the player for leaving Silas alive.
  4. The other branches. Eliza is disappointed and warns the player about returning. This includes the early ending that skips Chapter 10. Skip Chapter 10

The podcast’s opening and closing headlinesSurvivors, deaths and evidence

The first newspaper reflects the immediate aftermath. Stay through the podcast to see whether the report changes. These pairs come from recorded runs.

All counselors survive with ten evidence items
“Psycho Teens Charged After Summer Camp Massacre” gives way to an acquittal on self-defense grounds. The run also spares Caleb and kills Silas. Route · Opening at 49:30 · Closing at 50:50
The surviving Hacketts are suspected
A summer camp tragedy report attributes the deaths to a bear attack. The last headline says hiker remains were recovered and the Hackett family is suspected. In this run Chris and Silas survive. Hackett survival setup · Opening at 30:20 · Closing at 33:10
No Soul Left Alive with ten evidence items
“Summer Camp Massacre, No Soul Left Alive” changes to “New Evidence Shows Teens Were Trying to Defend Themselves.” The footage shows the all-deaths route, including the crash and Silas’s death. All-deaths evidence route · Opening at 26:40 · Closing at 27:10

Other reported headlines

Players report openings about bodies piling up, bodies being recovered, and a car crash. Other reported closings include unidentified animals in North Kill Forest, the case reopening over suspected foul play, and the camp closing for good. An Everyone Dies Movie Mode run with no evidence was reported to end with the closure headline.

All ten items can lead to different closing headlines. Exact selection rules, including the minimum conditions for “No Soul Left Alive,” remain unverified.

03 / SOURCES

How the cards were checked

  • The 120 records correspond to rows in the community epilogue catalog. The labels here describe the cards in plain language. A single playthrough can produce several of these records, including separate cards about the same event. The Quarry Wiki · epilogue catalog
  • Chapter walkthroughs and character death and infection lists were used to check prerequisites. “Exact card conditions unverified” means the event is documented but the particular card’s trigger is disputed or unclear. GameFAQs · choice and consequence guide · Chapter 9 · Chapter 10
  • Scene links go to timestamps supplied by the creator who captured the gameplay. They show the branch playing out. They may end before the recap card appears. Gaming With Abyss · footage index
  • Links labeled as recorded recaps or headlines jump directly to the on-screen result. The same paired freezer recap appears under Kaitlyn and Caleb so it can be found under either character. Recorded headline pairs
  • Creative director Will Byles reported 186 endings. The 120 card records are a separate count from that figure. The research found no official list that maps the 186 endings to exact inputs. Will Byles interview
