Before you start

  1. Finish the Hackett’s Quarry Massacre walkthrough through the credits. These routes start from its completed save. Check the opening state listed for the counselor you picked.
  2. Use Chapter Select from the main menu. If you plan to try several survivors, make a backup of the completed massacre on Steam. Each replay replaces later chapter snapshots. The one you choose carries forward the deaths and pickups recorded before that chapter.
  3. Set Aim Assist to Off and keep directions, mashes, breathing and interrupts manual. Pass prompts unless the route calls for a miss. If Retry? appears after a planned death, continue without rewinding. Input settings

The epilogue cards confirm who made it. A living, infected counselor still counts as a survivor. Kaitlyn and Ryan have dedicated sole-survivor achievements. When the game checks awards

Each replay links to optional pickups in the scenes it changes. Use the massacre pickup finder for shared scenes and check its access notes before choosing a branch.

Start in Chapter 6

Only Abigail survives

Find pickups on Abigail’s replay · Full deck and evidence from Chapter 1

Start with Abi alive and Emma dead on the island. Jacob can already be dead or take the Chapter 6 walkway death.

Chapter 6 · Shoot Nick

  1. As Kaitlyn choose Compassionate, take Intervene and choose Help Nick when he falls in.
  2. As Abi, take The Moon before finishing the two clue interactions and checking Nick. Choose Calm → Compassionate when talking to him.
  3. When Abi raises the shotgun, hit Nick within the 6.5-second base window. Abi survives. Nick transforms and escapes alive. Choose Reassuring as Ryan when the group checks on her. Shot and aiming outcomes

Chapters 7–8 · Shoot Travis in the escape, then let Laura kill Nick

  1. Use the massacre station escape so Laura shoots Travis. Confirm it in Paths → The White Whale.
  2. At the pool house choose Uncertain → Curious → Suspicious → Guarded → Compassionate → Friendly. Follow the quarry steps after leaving. With Emma dead there is no fight at the van. In the basement let Stop Laura expire. Laura shoots Nick.

Chapters 9–10 · Leave Abi in the office

  1. After the crane death, explore the storm shelter as Abi. Take The Hierophant by the stairs if you want it, then use the separate ladder to reach Chris’s office. Abi climbs out even if Dylan loosened the rung in Chapter 2.
  2. Use the attic deaths and the island and lodge deaths. Kaitlyn’s opening shot expires before any office handoff.

Abi’s card shows her safe in the surveillance room. Epilogue recap · Recorded Abi ending

Start in Chapter 5 or Chapter 1

Only Dylan survives

Find pickups from Dylan’s Chapter 5 replay

Use Chapter 5 if Abi chose Break In and Emma and Jacob already died. If the cabin stayed locked, replay Chapter 1 and break in, then take the Chapter 4 deaths again.

Chapter 5 · Leave the bite active

  1. Follow the radio-hut route through the feedback-loop setup. At the cabin clearing, The Devil, The Hermit and Claw Marks are optional.
  2. Silas bites Dylan while he works on the feedback loop. Choose Terrified to leave both hands intact and keep the bite active. What the radio-hut choices do

Chapters 6–8 · Keep the massacre setup

  1. Let Nick kill Abi in the pool house. In Chapter 7, have Laura shoot Travis during the escape.
  2. In the basement, let Stop Laura expire so Nick dies.

Chapters 9–10 · Let Dylan transform and keep Kaitlyn unbitten

  1. Use the Chapter 9 house and scrapyard directions. At the crane, choose Encouraging → Lift Car → Slam Car to skip the torch fight. Pass the lever mash to protect Kaitlyn. Dylan transforms before the lodge and Kaitlyn leaves alone. Transformation branch
  2. Use the attic deaths and the island and lodge deaths. Dylan stays alive with his infection.

If Dylan reaches the lodge with Kaitlyn, check for an amputation or a later bite. This route needs his active Chapter 5 bite. Infected recap · Recorded Dylan ending

Start in Chapter 4

Only Emma survives

Find pickups on Emma’s replay

Start before Ryan aims into the bushes. Hold fire at the first window and hit Jacob at the second. For Justice or the Memory Card, hold fire twice and use the later death.

Chapter 4 · Escape the island without a bite

  1. At the dock turn around for The Magician if wanted. Follow the upper path to the treehouse after any island clues.
  2. Inside choose Search Bag → Use Taser. Take Take Photo? if you want the Attack Photo.
  3. Press Down, pass the first zipline mash, choose Spray and press Up twice. Back inside, choose Use Trapdoor, press Down and pass the ladder mash.
  4. After Dylan’s lodge call, choose Reel Slowly at the crank and let Speed Up? expire. Emma leaves without a bite. If she was bitten earlier in the chase, the van will need a different rescue.

Chapters 5–8 · Keep Emma human

Optional pickups · Keep Jacob alive until Chapter 6
  1. Hold fire in both Chapter 4 aiming windows. Escape the island as Emma. Use the unbitten route above or take the island bite if you want the woods cure.
  2. In Chapter 6, take Memory Card and Justice as Jacob before taking the right trail.
  3. With Emma present, choose Delighted → Apologetic and press Right at the attack. Choose Run and pass the mash so Jacob reaches the bear trap unbitten. Choose Pull Free and let the Up recovery expire. Jacob dies and Emma gets away. Trap outcomes
  1. Keep Dylan uninfected at the radio hut. In Chapter 6 let the pool house shot expire so Nick kills Abi.
  2. At the firepit, turn right for Kid’s Letter Home near the yellow tent if wanted. Walk uphill. When the hunters catch the werewolf, let Run expire to keep Emma unbitten.
  3. Use the station escape that wounds Travis. In Chapter 8 the van fight is skipped. Let Laura shoot Nick in the basement.

Chapters 9–10 · Leave Emma in the office

  1. After the crane death, explore the shelter as Emma. Take The Hierophant by the stairs if wanted. Use the ladder to reach Chris’s office.
  2. Use the attic deaths and the island and lodge deaths. Emma stays safe in the office.

  2. As Emma, choose Search Bag → Use Taser. Press Down, pass the zipline mash, choose Spray and press Up twice. Back inside choose Use Trapdoor and let Down expire at the cabinet for Max’s bite. Press Right and pass the ladder mash. Choose Reel Slowly and let Speed Up? expire at the final crank.
  3. Keep Dylan uninfected at the radio hut. At the pool house let Abi’s aiming window expire so Nick kills her. As Emma, let Run? expire at the hunters to preserve her island bite. Pool house inputs
  4. Have Laura shoot Travis during the escape. At the van choose Optimistic → Help, hit Emma within four seconds, then hit her on the roof within about three seconds. With Abi dead there’s no silver choice. Emma flees alive and Dylan stays uninfected. Van aiming windows

Alternate · Let Emma turn in the shelter

  2. For an infected ending, shoot Travis in jail. For a cured ending, sedate him so the hunt can start. At the van Emma comes out human. In the basement, let Stop Laura? expire so Nick dies.

Start in Chapter 4

Only Jacob survives

Find pickups on Jacob’s replay

Jacob must enter Chapter 4 alive. If he drowned, replay Chapter 3 and choose Help Abi instead of the dive.

Chapters 4–6 · Keep Jacob alive through the walkway

  1. At the firepit, take Apply Pressure?, pass the mash, choose Dismissive and take Call Out?. Hold fire in both aiming windows. Jacob walks out of the bushes.
  2. As Emma, choose Open Trapdoor before searching the bag at the treehouse. For Attack Photo, take the picture first and use the massacre’s second-zipline death. Keep Dylan uninfected at the radio hut.
  3. In Chapter 6, Jacob explores the walkway alone. Pick up Memory Card and Justice before taking the right path. Press Down at the attack. If you miss it, pass the mash to pull him up.
  4. Choose Run, pass the mash and choose Pry Open at the trap. Jedediah captures Jacob alive. At the pool house, let Nick kill Abi.

Chapters 7–10 · Let the power cut free Jacob

  1. Have Laura shoot Travis during the escape. In the basement press Down at the slippery floor, let Open Cage? expire, then let Stop Laura expire. Laura kills Nick and the puzzle is skipped.
  2. In Chapter 9, Laura cuts the power after fighting Jedediah. Jacob leaves his cage alive because Nick is dead. Power-cut outcomes
  3. Use the crane death, attic deaths and island and lodge deaths. Chris’s death removes Jacob’s last woods chase.

Jacob’s card shows him freed from the cages. Epilogue recap · Jacob recap footage from an alternate route

Alternate · Keep Jacob infected or cure him

  1. Start Chapter 6 with Jacob alive, Emma dead and Dylan uninfected, or use the Chapter 4 setup above. Collect Justice and the Memory Card if wanted. Press Down at the walkway and choose Run. Let the mash expire so Caleb bites Jacob. This skips the bear trap. Let Nick kill Abi at the pool house.
  2. In Chapter 7, shoot Travis during the escape to keep Jacob infected, or use the syringe to set up his cure later.
  3. Let Stop Laura? expire at the cage. Nick dies, and Jacob gets out at the Chapter 9 power cut. Use the crane death and keep Kaitlyn unbitten.
  4. For Jacob still infected, use the attic deaths and island and lodge deaths. For Jacob cured, shoot Chris with Ryan bitten and Travis unharmed, then choose Suspicious. Use the same island and lodge deaths, then shoot Silas after the crash.

Start in Chapter 7 or Chapter 3

Only Laura survives

Find pickups from Laura’s Chapter 7 replay

Check Paths → Finders Keepers for “Ryan picked up the vial dropped by the hunter.” With that entry and the earlier massacre deaths, start in Chapter 7. If you already set up the hunt with the vial and only Laura, Ryan, Max and Kaitlyn are alive among the counselors, start in Chapter 10.

Chapter 3 · Get the vial if it’s missing

  1. Replay Chapter 3. As Ryan choose Worried → Assertive, then Take Shortcut. Press Up, Up, Left and pass the climb mash.
  2. Choose Take Shortcut again. Press Up, Left, Left at the stones, then Up, Right at the stairs.
  3. Aim at Bobby holding Nick and hit Bobby on the first shot. Ryan picks up the vial automatically. If Ryan aims at empty bushes instead, he reached Nick too late to get it. Vial pickup and timing
  4. Redo the massacre in Chapter 4, Chapter 5 and Chapter 6. Jacob, Emma and Abi must die, and Dylan must reach the crane uninfected.

Chapters 7–9 · Start the hunt

  1. Use the unharmed Travis escape. Loosen the brick with the spoon, take the syringe from the upstairs locker, hide it behind the brick before sleeping and press Left to inject Travis during the escape.
  2. In Chapter 8, let Stop Laura expire so Laura kills Nick. Use the crane death in Chapter 9 to kill Dylan and leave Kaitlyn unbitten.
  3. Choose Sympathetic → Accept when Laura offers Ryan her bite. Let Pull Away? expire. As Ryan, shoot Chris directly ahead in the attic. With Travis unharmed, choose Suspicious to join the hunt.

Chapter 10 · Give Laura the protection

  1. Use the island and lodge deaths, then follow the car and nest sequence. At the roadside choose Take Vial to protect Laura.
  2. At the nest take Raise Gun? and let the aiming timer expire. Silas kills Travis and Ryan. Laura survives.

If Ryan never offers the vial after the car stops safely, replay Chapter 3 for the pickup. Laura’s vial recap · Recorded vial protection on another nest branch

Start in Chapter 10

Only Max survives

Find pickups on Max’s replay

The massacre branch should enter Chapter 10 with Max cured on the island and unbitten Kaitlyn alone at the lodge. If Chris is alive, replay Chapter 9 and hit him to get Max’s island scene.

  1. As Max, walk from the treehouse to the dock and choose Stay. Caleb waits across the water if Max swims.
  2. As Kaitlyn, take any lodge pickups first. Use the portrait on the landing to Prepare for Attack.
  3. Choose Wait and let the opening aim timer expire. Caleb kills Kaitlyn. Continue to the epilogue to confirm Max is the only living counselor.

Start in Chapter 8

Only Nick survives

Find pickups on Nick’s replay

Jacob, Emma and Abi should already be dead, Dylan should be uninfected, and Laura should have shot Travis during the Chapter 7 escape.

Chapter 8 · Stop the cage shot

  1. Follow the quarry walkthrough. As Ryan in the basement, take The Empress and The Emperor before entering the red-lit passage if wanted.
  2. Enter the passage and press Down at the slippery floor. As Laura aims at the caged werewolf, take Stop Laura? within the six-second base window. The prompt appears with Jacob already dead. With Jacob gone the breaker puzzle is skipped.

Chapters 9–10 · Leave Nick’s infection active

  1. Use the crane death and the attic deaths. Chris dies, which cures Max. Nick’s infection stays active.
  2. Use the island and lodge deaths. Caleb and Silas stay alive. The epilogue shows Nick surviving the full moon infected.

If Laura shoots Nick, restart Chapter 8 and take the interrupt. Epilogue recap · Recorded Nick ending

Alternate · Cure Nick or Dylan

  1. Start from Chapter 7 for Nick, or for Dylan if his radio-hut bite is already active. Otherwise replay to get Dylan’s bite. In Chapter 7, change the escape and sedate Travis with the syringe.
  2. For Nick, take Stop Laura? at the cage and kill Dylan at the crane. For Dylan, let Stop Laura expire so Nick dies, then let Dylan transform at the crane. Keep Kaitlyn unbitten on either route.
  3. As Ryan, accept Laura’s bite and hit Chris in the attic. Choose Suspicious to start the hunt. Use the island and lodge deaths in Chapter 10.
  4. Use the fatal car branch and shoot Silas. Laura, Ryan and Travis die. Your chosen counselor survives cured. Cured Nick recap · Cured Dylan recap

Shared finale inputs

Chapter 9 · Kill Dylan and leave Kaitlyn unbitten

  1. Use the Hackett house steps through Ryan’s bite. When Dylan explores the scrapyard, pass through the garage and use the switch to open the gate. Take any scrapyard pickups before approaching the crane.
  2. As uninfected Dylan, choose Encouraging → Warn Kaitlyn, press Up and choose Sound Horn. Fail the first blowtorch mash. Dylan dies before the lever prompt. Kaitlyn goes to the lodge unbitten.

Chapter 9 · Kill Chris and lose to Travis

  1. Ryan should already have accepted Laura’s bite. As Laura in the attic, approach Chris to start the fight.
  2. As Ryan, hit Chris in the 4.1-second base aiming window. Chris is directly ahead. Laura is farther right. Chris’s death cures Max.
  3. If Laura shot Travis in Chapter 7, he stabs her. Choose Aggressive and let Down expire to let him kill Ryan. If Travis instead agrees to the hunt, the save carried an unharmed Travis into the attic. Replay the gun escape in Chapter 7 for this version.

Chapter 10 · Kill Max and Kaitlyn

  1. As Max, walk to the dock and choose Swim to Shore. Caleb kills him on the mainland.
  2. As Kaitlyn, collect any lodge clues, evidence or The High Priestess before choosing Prepare for Attack at the portrait.
  3. With Kaitlyn unbitten and Dylan absent, choose Wait and let the first aiming timer expire. Caleb kills Kaitlyn before she reaches the office or kitchen.

Chapter 10 · Survive the car and let Silas attack

  1. Choose Inquisitive as Laura. Press Right at the attack, choose Grab Gun → Take Cover and press Down to pull the handbrake. How to recover from a missed dodge
  2. Choose Determined after the car stops. Choose Take Vial for Laura or Give to Ryan for Ryan.
  3. Choose Probing → Reflective in the woods. At the nest take Raise Gun? and let the aiming timer expire. Silas spares the vial’s recipient. Vial replay conditions

Chapter 10 · Kill Silas while the hunters die

  1. Set up the hunt in Chapter 9. Keep Travis unharmed in Chapter 7, have Ryan Accept the bite, shoot Chris and choose Suspicious. Use the island and lodge deaths before the hunt.
  2. In the car choose Inquisitive, press Right and choose Grab Gun → Take Cover. Let Down expire twice, first at the handbrake and again when Silas reaches Travis. Travis dies and Ryan is killed in the crash.
  3. Pass the crawl mash to reach the gun, then press Up to shoot Silas. Laura dies of her injuries after the shot. The curse lifts. Car outcomes · Crash recap and footage
