Choose who survives

Max, Emma and Nick survive infected on these branches. Jacob, Laura and Ryan die. Your choices at the pool, van, scrapyard and attic decide who joins the survivors.

Keep Abigail alive
Keep Dylan uninfected in Chapter 5. Shoot Nick in Chapter 6 to save Abi. At the van, save Abi but let Emma kill Dylan. Abi can collect The Hierophant in Chapter 9.
Keep Dylan alive
Break into the locked cabin in Chapter 1 and keep Dylan’s radio-hut bite. Let Nick kill Abi, then choose Run at the van. Emma spares infected Dylan.
Lose Abigail and Dylan
Keep Dylan uninfected in Chapter 5. Let Nick kill Abi, then choose Run at the van. Emma kills uninfected Dylan.

Each van route sends Kaitlyn to the scrapyard alone. You can leave her alive under the scrap or kill her. At the attic, shoot transformed Laura to save Travis or hold fire to let her kill him.

On a completed save, replay Chapter 4 with Emma and Dylan alive. If you want Dylan to survive and Abi left the cabin locked, replay from Chapter 1 and choose Break In. For a new game, follow Rough Night through Chapter 3 with that change if needed. Chapter Select and save rules

Use manual directions, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe, with Aim Assist Off. Pass prompts unless the guide calls for a miss or timeout. Decline Death Rewind for the deaths you planned. Input settings

Chapter 4

Don’t Panic

Ryan · The firepit and the bushes

  1. Take Apply Pressure? and pass the mash. Choose Dismissive when Dylan suggests amputation. Take Call Out? when the bushes rustle.
  2. Hold fire during the first aiming window. On the second rustle, hit the movement in the bushes within the six-second base window. Ryan gets the choice to leave a note if Jacob died. Choose Dismissive. If Jacob drowned in Chapter 3, there is no second shot.
Alternate route · Keep Jacob alive for Chapter 6 pickups

Let both shots expire. When Jacob comes out, choose Questioning and let Let Go? expire. Later, collect Justice and Memory Card and take the fatal bear-trap branch. This still leaves the Chapter 10 woods scene unavailable.

Emma · The island and the treehouse

  1. When Emma takes over at the dock, turn around for the card if you want it. Then head inland to the fork.
Optional tarot · The Magician

Walk back to the tip of the dock and press Interact at the camera change. Step away and approach again to retry. Card and pickup video

  1. Explore both paths before opening the treehouse door if you want the island clues. Both vlog choices are optional.
Optional clues · Box of Matches and Police Car Keys
  1. Box of Matches. At the stairs, take the ground path to the fire ring. Inspect the matches and leave the ladder alone. Journal and video
  2. Police Car Keys. Backtrack and take the stairs. Inspect the window just left of the treehouse door before going in. Journal and video
  1. Open the treehouse door, then choose Search Bag → Use Taser. Opening the trapdoor before searching the bag kills Emma.
Optional evidence · Attack Photo

Take Take Photo immediately after Emma uses the taser. Missing the interrupt loses the evidence and the chase still continues. Pickup video

  2. On the second visit inside, choose Use Trapdoor as soon as it appears. Miss Down at the cabinet so Max bites Emma’s arm. Press Right to fend him off, then pass the ladder mash. Check that Emma is clutching her arm. Alternate bite and recovery inputs

Dylan · Call for help and give Kaitlyn a gun

Optional tarot · Strength

  2. When the phone cuts out, choose Suspicious → Calm. After the power goes out, choose Defensive → Give Gun. Kaitlyn takes a shotgun and Dylan and Ryan leave for the radio hut. Other lodge dialogue

Emma · The second zipline

  1. Choose Reel Slowly at the crank. Let Speed Up? expire. Emma leaves the island with Max’s bite. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 5

White Noise

Nick and Kaitlyn · Escape the lodge

  1. As Nick, choose Compassionate, let Speak Up? expire, then choose Reassuring.
  2. As Kaitlyn, press Right at the flashlight and Left at the banister, pass Don’t Breathe and press Down at the stairs.
  1. Back as Nick, choose Confident → Intrigued → Hide. Pass Don’t Breathe to get out with Abi.

Dylan · Cabins and radio hut exterior

  1. Cross the cabin clearing toward the building with the radio tower. Finish your pickups before talking to Ryan at the steps.
Optional clues · Two new pickups and three possible recoveries
  1. Triggered Bear Trap. Check the ground by the fence at the far left of the clearing. Journal and video
  2. Letter to Camp Nurse. Inspect the paper on the ground outside Room 03. Journal and video

If you missed a cabin clue in Chapter 1, check the three possible recovery pickups before entering the hut.

Optional tarot · The Devil

Walk between the central swing and the picnic tables. Press Interact when the camera shows the side of the table. Card and pickup video

Optional evidence · Claw Marks

At the hut, look at the wall by the fan left of the entrance. Pickup video

Optional tarot · The Hermit

Round the corner past the claw marks and approach the foot of the tower. Step away and return if the prompt expires. Card and pickup video

Ryan · The feedback loop

  1. Choose Interested → Concerned → Apologetic. Hold fire when Ryan aims at the ceiling.

  • Keep Dylan alive. Choose Terrified when he is bitten. Ryan leaves the hand intact and Dylan stays infected. You must also let Nick kill Abi in Chapter 6.
  • Keep Abi alive or lose both. Choose Desperate → Chainsaw and pass the mash. This removes Dylan’s hand and stops the infection. If the cabin is locked, the steps above already keep him uninfected.

Chapter 6

Prayers by Night

Jacob · Walkway and bear trap

Skip this scene if Jacob is already dead. If you spared him for pickups, get both before the attack.

  1. Climb the wooden stairs and take the right side at the fork. Check the walkway for the camera before you go down the stairs at the far end.
Optional evidence · Memory Card

Inspect the camera lying on the ground on the right branch of the wooden walkway. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Justice

After leaving the walkway, turn left and take the card at the camera change. The path to the right starts the scene with Emma. Card and pickup video

  1. Take the path to the right. Choose Delighted → Apologetic and press Right when the werewolf attacks.
  2. Choose Run and pass the mash. At the bear trap choose Pull Free and let the next Up expire. The second trap kills Jacob. On the audited PC build, the game records his death even if an earlier bite leaves him moving in the cutscene. Bites and trap outcomes

Kaitlyn and Abi · The pool house

  1. As Kaitlyn, choose Compassionate, take Intervene, then choose Help Nick when he falls in.
  2. As Abi, collect anything you want before starting the conversation with Nick. The two clue interactions and checking Nick count toward ending exploration, so grab The Moon first if you want it.
Optional tarot · The Moon

Enter the third changing stall on the left and walk toward the back wall. If you miss the card prompt, leave the stall and re-enter. Card and pickup video

Optional clues · Campers Photo and Spooky Drawing
  1. Campers Photo. Check the board in the corner of the main room. Journal and video
  2. Spooky Drawing. Inspect the locker at the far end of the changing-room corridor. Journal and video
  1. Check Nick. Choose Calm → Compassionate. He throws Abi across the room.
  2. To save Abi, hit Nick within the 6.5-second base window. To lose Abi, hold fire until Nick transforms and kills her. Nick reaches the cages either way.

Emma and Ryan · Hunters and a visitor

Optional clue · Kid’s Letter Home

Turn right from the firepit and pick up Kid’s Letter Home on the ground by the yellow tent before going uphill. Journal and video

  1. As Emma, check the clearing before going uphill if you want the clue. Then follow either trail uphill. When the hunters catch a werewolf, let Run expire to keep Max’s bite active. Taking another bite here delays Emma’s transformation until the shelter and prevents the van fight.
  2. As Ryan at the pool house, choose Reassuring → Suspicious → Interested if Abi lives or Hopeful → Suspicious → Interested if she died. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 7

The Past Behind Us

Laura · Questioning and the first cell visit

  1. Let Call Out? expire. Choose Compliant → Compliant → Intrigued → Pleading. Let Lash Out expire on the way back to the cell.
  2. With Max, choose Concerned → Disappointed. Choose Compliant when Travis returns and Worried when Max struggles.
Optional tarot · The World

Walk toward the bed, then back toward the bars. Take the card beneath the bed before using the spoon. If the camera prompt expires, repeat the walk. Card and pickup video

Optional clue · Scrawled Limerick

Read the Scrawled Limerick on the wall opposite the loose brick. You can return here during station exploration if you missed it. Journal and video

  1. During the first cell exploration, inspect the loose brick to the left of the bed, then interact with the bed to use the spoon. Making the space ends exploration.
  2. Choose Fearful → Serious → Reflective → Encouraging → Empathetic. Back at the pool house choose Calm, then continue the flashback with Curious → Optimistic → Skeptical.
  3. Let Call for Help? expire while Laura is cuffed. At sunset choose Compassionate. Max transforms and injures Laura’s eye on this branch.

Laura · Search the station and hide the syringe

  1. When the station opens, use the main room and lobby for the downstairs pickups before heading upstairs.
Optional tarot · The Chariot

Walk between the tables in the main room. Step away and return to the gap if the prompt expires. Card and pickup video

Optional evidence · Cease & Desist Letter

Go up the short steps at the lobby corner and inspect the paper across from the wooden door. Pickup video

  1. Go upstairs. In the third office, open the locker and take the syringe. Pickups and the computer are still available before you go to sleep.
Optional clues · Charred Sheriff’s Badge and the podcast
  1. Charred Sheriff’s Badge. Go upstairs and enter the second office. Look on the right side of the room. Journal and video
  2. Bizarre Yet Bonafide Podcast. Read the birthday card in the first upstairs office and the calendar in the third. Use the computer in the downstairs corner office. Laura works out the password and plays the recording for the clue and the podcast achievement. Journal and video
  1. Return to the cell and interact with the space behind the brick to hide the syringe. Interact with the bed and choose Go to Sleep after the syringe is hidden.

Laura · Get out without shooting Travis

  1. Choose Honest when Travis returns, then Confident during his explanation. Let Take Gun expire.
  2. Choose Confused → Encouraging → Resigned → Empathetic. Press Up to hear more of Travis and Chris’s conversation.
  3. When Travis comes into the cell, press Left to inject him. If you miss it, use the gun escape without shooting him.
  4. Choose Anxious after the escape. If the group asks about Jacob, choose Reassuring. After the island flashback choose Show Bite. If Jacob’s cage scene plays, choose Pleading. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 8

Ryan and Laura · Walk to the Hackett house

  1. At the pool house, choose Uncertain → Curious. Choose Suspicious if Abi lives or Honest if she died. Continue with Guarded → Compassionate → Friendly.

Laura · The flooded quarry

Optional clues · Old Quarry Tools and Miner’s Lunchbox

  2. Miner’s Lunchbox. Turn back and circle the pool. Inspect the metal lunchbox at the far end before climbing. Journal and video
  1. Climb up and follow the walkways.
Optional tarot · The Lovers

After the railing breaks, keep left on the next ledge. Take the card before approaching the stairs ahead. Card and pickup video

  1. Press Right when the stairs collapse. Search at the top before crossing the next metal walkway.
Optional evidence · Scarred Flesh

Inspect the patch on the ground before the next walkway. Both outcomes of the stair prompt leave it accessible. Pickup video

  1. Past the Cave In sign, choose Patient if you want to hear the full conversation. Pass the mash at the planks to enter the tunnel.
Optional tarot · Wheel of Fortune

Follow the tunnel and take the card when the camera swings around. If it expires, back out of the trigger and approach again before leaving through the basement door. Card and pickup video

Kaitlyn · Leave the van without Dylan

Emma transforms here if she has Max’s island bite and avoided the Chapter 6 bite. Follow the branch that matches the pool-house outcome.

Abi alive · Save Abi and lose Dylan

  2. On the roof, hold fire. Emma bites Dylan as he tries to help. Hold fire again for the 3.9-second rescue window. Emma kills Dylan outside the van. Kaitlyn goes to the scrapyard alone and Abi reaches the shelter.
Abi dead · Dylan’s bite decides whether he survives

Choose Optimistic in the lodge, then Run at the van. Emma spares Dylan if he kept the Chapter 5 bite. If he was unbitten or lost his hand, Emma kills him inside the van. Kaitlyn goes to the scrapyard alone in both cases.

Chapter 9

The Matriarch

Laura and Ryan · Constance and the bedroom

  1. Fail the shotgun mash to spare Constance. Choose Run, then press Right to dodge her shot. Bobby stabs Ryan regardless.
  2. As Ryan, let Pull Knife? expire. The bedroom has a three-minute base timer on PC. Take the pickups and inspect the cabinet before using the left door.
Optional evidence · Kaylee’s Letter

Open the small jewelry box on the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Death

Cross the bedroom toward the bed to trigger the camera change. Take the card before interacting with the left door or running out of time. Card and pickup video

  1. Inspect the tall cabinet. Check the left door’s keyhole and choose Hide → In Cabinet. Pass Don’t Breathe and pass the barricade mash to keep the knife. Bedroom timeout and recovery

Laura · Bathroom, house pickups and Jedediah

  1. Choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe in the bathtub. Skip the piano in the blue room to avoid an ambush.
  2. Go upstairs and through the office. Check the windows in the next room before approaching the door.
Optional clues · Hunting Trophy Wall and Hackett Family Tree
  1. Hunting Trophy Wall. Inspect the display next to the stairs in the blue room. Journal and video
  2. Hackett Family Tree. Go upstairs into the office. Inspect the framed chart beside the armchair. Journal and video
Optional tarot · The Sun

Walk toward the windows in the room beyond the upstairs office. Take the card before you approach the door. Step away and approach again if the prompt expires. Card and pickup video

  1. Approach the door when you’re done. Press Left, Left, Left against Jedediah, then choose Run to spare him. Laura cuts the power after the fight.

Ryan · Spare Bobby

  1. Press Left to squeeze through the gap and Right to catch the knife. Pass Don’t Breathe.
  2. Let Stab? expire when Bobby catches up. He needs to be able to fight off Chris to protect himself, Constance and Jedediah.
  3. When Laura finds Ryan, choose Sympathetic → Accept and let Pull Away? expire. Ryan still dies when Chris survives the attic fight.

Kaitlyn · Stay out of the lodge

  1. Follow the path right of the cars into the garage.
Optional clue · Scrapyard Note

Go upstairs in the garage and inspect the receipts at the far end of the walkway for the Scrapyard Note. Journal and video

  1. Use the switch on the table by the downstairs stairs to open the gate. Go through and take the metal steps onto the containers.
Optional tarot · Judgement

Walk across the container roofs after opening the gate. Take the card at the camera change. Card and pickup video

Optional clue · Discarded Signage

Go down the far steps and inspect the green sign between the staircases for Discarded Signage. Journal and video

  1. Descend on the far side and cross through the wrecked bus. Go left toward the yellow crane to start the fight.
Keep Kaitlyn alive
Choose Hide and fail Don’t Breathe in the car to take the leg bite. Hit Silas within the 2.3-second base window. Pass the mash to pull the bar, then let Left expire. The falling scrap traps her and the bite keeps her alive.
Kill Kaitlyn
Choose Gun and let the 5.1-second base aiming window expire. Silas kills her before she reaches the lodge.

Abi · The storm shelter

  1. Abi explores alone if she survived the van. If she died, the game skips this scene because Emma already transformed. Get any pickups before climbing the ladder.
Optional tarot · The Hierophant (Abi alive)

Walk toward the staircase on the right to reveal the card. The staircase and the ladder are separate. Take the card before leaving by the ladder. Card and pickup video

  1. You can inspect the lit fuse box across from the stairs for silver. Use the ladder to reach Chris’s office.

Ryan · Keep Chris alive

As Laura, approach Chris after inspecting anything you want in the attic. She transforms and the two fall through the floor. Bobby fends off Chris if you spared him.

Save Travis
Aim at transformed Laura, the target farther to the right, and hit her within the 4.1-second base window. Chris kills Ryan. Travis escapes, and Chris stays alive.
Lose Travis
Let the aiming timer expire. Chris kills Ryan, and transformed Laura and Travis kill each other. This earns Mutually Assured. Target and timing reference

If you took The Hierophant, press Left when the stake flies toward Eliza in the flashback. Otherwise choose Move On if offered.
