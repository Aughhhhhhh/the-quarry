Choose your ending

Nobody’s Fool · Jacob and Emma
Start in Chapter 4 with Jacob, Abi and Emma alive. Follow the island bite, van and cage steps. Shoot Chris in Chapter 9, then finish Jacob’s confession. Either attic branch works for this reunion.
Should’ve Gone to the Motel · Laura and Max
Start in Chapter 9 if you left Travis unharmed in the station escape. Choose Decline at the bite. Follow the shotgun steps and walk Max to the dock. Ryan dies on this branch.
Family Matters · All seven Hacketts die
Start in Chapter 9 with Travis unharmed and either Abi or a human Emma available to carry silver. Follow all three house kills and shoot Caleb with silver. Kill Travis in the attic to get the island reunion, or at Silas’s nest to keep Ryan alive. If the hunt is already set up and only Caleb and Travis are left, start in Chapter 10 with silver or the rabbit. Kaylee’s death is scripted.
Above the Law and The White Wolf · Hunt Silas
Start in Chapter 9 if Travis is unharmed. Accept the bite, shoot Chris and agree to the hunt for Above the Law. Replay Chapter 10 if the hunt is already set up. Hit Silas for The White Wolf.

For both reunions and Family Matters on one replay, start in Chapter 4, take the house kills in Chapter 9, decline the bite, shoot Travis in the attic and use silver at the lodge. For an ending with every counselor alive, use the Rough Night walkthrough.

Set Aim Assist to Off. Keep directional prompts, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe on manual settings. Pass prompts unless a step calls for a miss. For planned deaths, continue past Death Rewind. On a fresh game, follow the survival walkthrough through Chapter 3. Chapter Select and save rules · Manual input settings

Chapter 5

White Noise

Kaitlyn and Nick · Leave the lodge

  1. As Nick choose Grateful if Abi helped him in the woods. Compassionate is available either way. Let Speak Up? expire, then choose Reassuring.
  2. As Kaitlyn, press Right at the flashlight and Left at the banister. Pass Don’t Breathe and press Down at the stairs. If you want the Ticket Stub, expose the frame using the table encounter.
  3. Back as Nick, choose Confident → Intrigued → Hide and pass Don’t Breathe. If Abi kept the rabbit in Chapter 1, the toy stays available in the lodge kitchen.

Dylan and Ryan · Radio hut

  1. Cross the bridge and walk through the cabins toward the radio tower. Collect anything you want before talking to Ryan on the steps.
Optional tarot · The Devil

In the cabin clearing, walk between the swing and picnic tables. Take the card at the camera change. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Claw Marks

Inspect the wall by the fan left of the hut door. Pickup video

Optional tarot · The Hermit

Round the corner past the claw marks and walk to the base of the tower. Pickup video

  1. Talk to Ryan and choose Confident. In the hut choose Interested → Concerned → Apologetic, then hold fire at the ceiling.
  2. Choose Aggressive → Confident → Calm as they set up the feedback loop.
  3. If Abi broke in, Silas bites Dylan. Choose Desperate → Chainsaw and pass the mash to remove the hand and stop the infection. If Abi left the cabin locked, Ryan goes outside instead. Hold fire again, then press Left → Down when Silas lunges. Dylan stays unbitten.
  4. Choose Reassuring when Kaitlyn asks if it’s safe. Choose Move On if Eliza offers a reading.

Chapter 6

Prayers by Night

Jacob · Walkway and bear trap

  1. Climb the wooden stairs and take the right side at the fork. Check the walkway for the camera before you go down the stairs at the far end.
Optional evidence · Memory Card

Inspect the camera lying on the ground on the right branch of the wooden walkway. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Justice

After leaving the walkway, turn left and take the card at the camera change. The path to the right starts the scene with Emma. Card and pickup video

  1. Take the path to the right. Choose Delighted → Apologetic and press Right when the werewolf attacks.
  2. Choose Run and pass the chase mash. Choose Pry Open at the trap. Jacob reaches the cages alive and uninfected. Chase and trap variations

Kaitlyn and Abi · The pool house

  1. As Kaitlyn, choose Compassionate, take Intervene, then choose Help Nick when he falls in.
  2. As Abi, collect anything you want before starting the conversation with Nick. The two clue interactions and checking Nick count toward ending exploration, so grab The Moon first if you want it.
Optional tarot · The Moon

Enter the third changing stall on the left and walk toward the back wall. If you miss the card prompt, leave the stall and re-enter. Card and pickup video

  2. Hit Nick with the shotgun. The base window is 6.5 seconds. The blast saves Abi. Nick survives, transforms and escapes.

Emma and Ryan · Hunters and a visitor

  1. As Emma, check the yellow tent for Kid’s Letter Home if you want the clue, then follow either trail uphill. When the hunters catch a werewolf, let Run expire and leave any firework unused.   2. As Ryan at the pool house, choose Reassuring → Suspicious → Interested. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 7

The Past Behind Us

Laura · Questioning and the first cell visit

  1. Let Call Out? expire. Choose Compliant → Compliant → Intrigued → Pleading. Let Lash Out expire on the way back to the cell.
  2. With Max, choose Concerned → Disappointed. Choose Compliant when Travis returns and Worried when Max struggles.
Optional tarot · The World

Walk toward the bed, then back toward the bars. Take the card beneath the bed before using the spoon. If the camera prompt expires, repeat the walk. Card and pickup video

  1. During the first cell exploration, inspect the loose brick to the left of the bed, then interact with the bed to use the spoon. Making the space ends exploration. Read the limerick on the opposite wall first if you want the clue.
  2. Choose Fearful → Serious → Reflective → Encouraging → Empathetic. Back at the pool house choose Calm, then continue the flashback with Curious → Optimistic → Skeptical.
  3. Let Call for Help? expire while Laura is cuffed. At sunset choose Compassionate. Max transforms and injures Laura’s eye on this branch.

Laura · Search the station and hide the syringe

  1. When the station opens, use the main room and lobby for the downstairs pickups before heading upstairs.
Optional tarot · The Chariot

Walk between the tables in the main room. Step away and return to the gap if the prompt expires. Card and pickup video

Optional evidence · Cease & Desist Letter

Go up the short steps at the lobby corner and inspect the paper across from the wooden door. Pickup video

  1. Go upstairs. In the third office, open the locker and take the syringe. Pickups and the computer are still available before you go to sleep.
Optional achievement · Bizzare Yet Bonafide

Read the birthday card in the first upstairs office and the calendar in the third. Visit the computer in the downstairs corner office. Laura uses Travis’s birthday as the password and plays the recording. The second upstairs office has the Charred Sheriff’s Badge clue. Computer location and footage

  1. Return to the cell and interact with the space behind the brick to hide the syringe. Interact with the bed and choose Go to Sleep after the syringe is hidden.

Laura · Get out without shooting Travis

  1. Choose Honest when Travis returns, then Confident during his explanation. Let Take Gun expire.
  2. Choose Confused → Encouraging → Resigned → Empathetic. Press Up to hear more of Travis and Chris’s conversation.
  3. When Travis comes into the cell, press Left to inject him. If you miss the syringe prompt, take the gun, pass the Right prompts and choose Apologetic to lock him in unharmed. Gun escape and recovery inputs
  4. Choose Anxious after the escape. At the pool house choose Reassuring, then Show Bite after the island flashback. If Jacob’s cage scene plays, choose Pleading. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 8

The Belly of the Beast

Ryan and Laura · Walk to the Hackett house

  1. At the pool house, choose Uncertain → Curious → Suspicious. Laura makes a silver shell from Abi’s bracelet. Choose Guarded → Compassionate → Friendly to leave with Laura.
  2. On the walk, choose Inquisitive → Reflective → Compassionate. They fall into the quarry.

Laura · The flooded quarry

  1. Check the bottom of the stairs for Old Quarry Tools and the far side of the pool for the Miner’s Lunchbox if you want the clues. Climb up and follow the walkways.
Optional tarot · The Lovers

After the railing breaks, keep left on the next ledge. Take the card before approaching the stairs ahead. Card and pickup video

  1. Press Right when the stairs collapse. Search at the top before crossing the next metal walkway.
Optional evidence · Scarred Flesh

Inspect the patch on the ground before the next walkway. Both outcomes of the stair prompt leave it accessible. Pickup video

  1. Past the Cave In sign, choose Patient if you want to hear the full conversation. Pass the mash at the planks to enter the tunnel.
Optional tarot · Wheel of Fortune

Follow the tunnel and take the card when the camera swings around. If it expires, back out of the trigger and approach again before leaving through the basement door. Card and pickup video

  1. Continue through the tunnel and open the basement door when you’re done exploring.

Kaitlyn · Back at camp

  1. Choose Honest. If you gave Emma Max’s bite and skipped the hunters’ bite, she bursts out of the van. Choose Shoot and hit her as she crosses the reticle. The base window is 2.2 seconds with Abi alive.
  2. Hit Emma on the roof within about three seconds. If you miss and Dylan is bitten, hit her in the 3.9-second rescue window to save him.
  3. Choose Regular Shot at the ammo choice so Emma survives. A timeout selects silver and kills her. Abi hides in the shelter and Kaitlyn goes to the scrapyard with Dylan. Van shot timings and alternate branches
  4. If you came here on an unbitten Emma route, choose Encouraging when the group examines the van. There is no fight.

Ryan · Basement and the cages

  1. Use the side room and the straight corridor to explore before entering the red-lit passage.
Optional tarot · The Empress

Turn left from where you gain control and walk around the distillery room until the camera reveals the card. The Rum Still clue is in the same room. Card and pickup video

Optional tarot · The Emperor

Return to the starting corridor and walk straight. Take the card before entering the tunnel lit in red. Card and pickup video

  1. Go into the red-lit passage and press Down at the slippery floor. Let Open Cage? expire.
  2. Take Stop Laura when Laura aims at the werewolf. That’s Nick. Then choose Help Jacob.
  3. Select breaker 1, then breaker 2 to open Jacob’s cage. Choose Continue. Select breaker 2, then breaker 3 to open the passage. Nick stays safely inside his cage. Recover after a wrong pair
  4. At Eliza, choose Move On if offered.

Chapter 9 · Family Matters and attic branches

The Matriarch

Laura and Ryan · Constance and the bedroom

  1. On the road as Dylan, choose Curious → Curious with both hands or Positive → Curious after amputation.
  2. For Family Matters and No Soul Left Alive, win the shotgun mash to kill Constance. To spare her, fail the mash and choose Run. Press Right at the next dodge on either branch.
  3. After Bobby stabs Ryan, let Pull Knife? expire. The bedroom has a three-minute base timer. Collect anything you want before using the left door.
Optional evidence · Kaylee’s Letter

Open the small jewelry box on the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Death

Cross the bedroom toward the bed to trigger the camera change. Take the card before interacting with the left door or running out of time. Card and pickup video

  1. Check the left door’s keyhole, choose Block Door and pass both mashes. If you miss the first, press Up and pass the final mash to keep the knife. Bedroom recoveries

Laura · Jedediah and the power cut

  1. Choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe in the bathtub. In the blue room, skip the piano and go upstairs. Check the wall downstairs and the family tree upstairs if you want the clues.
Optional tarot · The Sun

Walk toward the windows in the room beyond the upstairs office. Take the card before you approach the door. Step away and approach again if the prompt expires. Card and pickup video

  1. Approach the upstairs door. Press Left, Left, Left. For Family Matters and No Soul Left Alive, choose Attack and win the mash to kill Jedediah. Choose Run if you want to spare him. Laura cuts the power either way. Recovery prompts

Ryan · Bobby and the bite

  1. Press Left → Right at the wall gap and pass Don’t Breathe. For Family Matters and No Soul Left Alive, take Stab when Bobby catches up. This wounds him so Chris can kill him. To keep Bobby alive and let him protect any surviving relatives, let Stab expire.
  2. Choose Sympathetic. For the island reunion, choose Decline when Laura offers the bite. To hunt Silas or kill Travis at the nest, choose Accept and let Pull Away? expire.

Dylan · Get Kaitlyn to the lodge

  1. Follow the path right of the cars into the garage. Flip the gate switch on the table by the downstairs stairs. Go through the gate and up the metal steps onto the containers.
Optional tarot · Judgement

Walk across the container roofs after opening the gate. Take the card at the camera change. The green sign between the far staircases is the Discarded Signage clue. Card and pickup video

  1. Descend on the far side, cross through the wrecked bus and go left toward the yellow crane.
  2. Choose Encouraging → Lift Car → Slam Car and pass the lever mash. Kaitlyn stays unbitten and Dylan reaches the lodge with her. For No Soul Left Alive, use the failed blowtorch mash instead to kill Dylan. Solo scrapyard recovery

Abi · Collect silver in the shelter

  1. After saving Abi at the van, explore the shelter before climbing the ladder. Emma, if still human, can do this alone if Abi died. Both counselors are dead by this point on No Soul Left Alive, so that route skips the shelter.
Optional tarot · The Hierophant

Walk toward the staircase on the right to reveal the card. The staircase and the ladder are separate. Take the card before leaving by the ladder. Card and pickup video

  1. Inspect the lit fuse box across from the stairs for silver. Use the ladder to take it up to Chris’s office. This gives Kaitlyn the silver shot at the lodge. Handoff requirements

Laura and Ryan · Decide the attic outcome

  1. As Laura, approach Chris to start the fight. If you wounded Bobby, Chris kills him here. After the fall, shoot Chris as Ryan. Chris is directly ahead. The target farther right is Laura. The base window is 4.1 seconds. Target comparison
  2. If you declined the bite and left Travis unharmed in Chapter 7, Ryan bleeds out. As Laura, press Up to grab the shotgun and shoot Travis within the 3.2-second base aiming window. Missing either input sends Laura to the basement. On success, continue to Max on the island.
  3. If you accepted the bite and left Travis unharmed, choose Suspicious when he asks for help. This unlocks Above the Law and sets up the Chapter 10 hunt.
  4. If you took The Hierophant, press Left at the flying stake in Eliza’s flashback. Otherwise choose Move On if she offers a reading.

Chapter 10 · Island, woods and lodge

Bricks & Mortar

Max · Reunite with Laura

  1. Follow the island path all the way down to the dock. If Laura shot Travis and escaped, she meets Max there and Should’ve Gone to the Motel unlocks. The reunion takes the place of the swim decision.
  2. If Max gets the swim decision, choose Stay to save him. For No Soul Left Alive, choose Swim to Shore to let Caleb kill him.

Kaitlyn · Explore before starting the lodge attack

  1. Go upstairs from the main hall. The Ancestral Portrait is on the chimney wall. Choose Keep Looking if you inspect it before taking the pickups.
Optional tarot · The High Priestess

Use the stairs next to the bunk room and walk toward the windows on the top floor. Take the card at the camera change. This is the last tarot pickup. Card and pickup video

Optional evidence · Trail Cam Footage

Check the boxes near the couch on the upper floor before starting the attack. This is the last evidence pickup. Pickup video

  1. Return to the portrait and choose Prepare for Attack. Lodge map and clue locations

Jacob · Tell Emma about the van

  1. The reunion plays if Jacob is free, alive and uninfected and Emma escaped the van alive with Max’s bite. Shooting Chris cured her.
  2. As Jacob, choose Honest → Remorseful → Apologetic. He tells Emma he sabotaged the van and Nobody’s Fool unlocks. Other dialogue branches

Kaitlyn · Kill Caleb with silver

  1. When Caleb approaches, choose Run → Beam and pass the balance mash. If you miss the mash, press Up at the recovery prompt.
  2. Choose Run again, then Investigate when Abi or Emma knocks. Kaitlyn takes the shell.
  3. Hit Caleb during the three-second base aiming window. He dies and Kaitlyn survives. The epilogue checks Family Matters when Caleb and all the other Hacketts are dead. Silver shot reference

Kaitlyn · Trap Caleb if you have the rabbit

After crossing the beam, choose Run again. If the office offers Investigate, choose Run there too. Pass Don’t Breathe in the kitchen, choose Freezer → Use Decoy and pass the door mash. Kaitlyn locks Caleb inside. For Family Matters, shoot Silas on the hunt to remove Caleb’s protection so he freezes. Freezer outcomes

Chapter 10 · The hunt

The White Wolf

  1. In the car, choose Inquisitive. Press Right at the attack. Choose Grab Gun → Take Cover and press Down to pull the handbrake.
  2. Outside, choose Determined. Choose Give to Ryan if he offers the blood vial. Continue with Probing → Reflective in the woods.
  3. At the cage, take Raise Gun and shoot Silas. You have six seconds to take the interrupt and about eight seconds for the shot on PC. The curse lifts and Laura, Ryan and Travis survive. Car recoveries · Nest outcomes

Family Matters · Keep Laura and Ryan alive

  1. Use the same car and woods steps, but let Raise Gun expire at the cage.
  2. When Travis grabs for the gun, miss Right. Laura accidentally shoots him. Then shoot Silas before the aiming timer runs out. For Family Matters, Caleb must also be dead. Travis’s recap variants

Replay · All counselors and Hacketts die

No Soul Left Alive

Start in Chapter 4 with the rabbit already in Abi’s bag, or replay Chapter 1 to break into the cabin and take it. The opening headline checks all nine counselors and five Hacketts. This route also kills Caleb and Silas.

  1. In Chapter 4, shoot Jacob during the second bush aiming window and open the treehouse trapdoor before Emma searches her bag. For all evidence, use the later deaths below.
  2. In Chapter 5, choose Desperate → Chainsaw at the radio hut and pass the mash if Dylan takes the bite. He must be uninfected at the crane. In Chapter 6, let Abi’s shot at Nick expire so he kills her. In Chapter 7, hide the syringe and sedate Travis with Left.
  3. In Chapter 8, let Stop Laura expire. She shoots Nick in the cage.
  4. In Chapter 9, kill Constance and Jedediah, stab Bobby, accept Laura’s bite, and fail the blowtorch mash to kill Dylan. Shoot Chris so Max is cured, then choose Suspicious to set up the hunt.
  5. In Chapter 10, walk Max to the dock and choose Swim to Shore. As Kaitlyn, use the portrait to Prepare for Attack after any pickups. Choose Run → Beam and pass the balance mash. Choose Run again and pass Don’t Breathe in the kitchen. Choose Freezer → Use Decoy and fail the door mash. Caleb pulls Kaitlyn in and kills her. Freezer outcomes

Laura · Crash the car and shoot Silas

  1. As Laura in the car, choose Inquisitive and miss Right at the first attack. Choose Grab Gun when paired with Take Cover. Miss Down when Laura can save Travis. Silas kills Travis and the crash kills Ryan.
  2. Win the crawl mash and press Up to shoot Silas before Laura dies. If you trapped Caleb, he freezes after losing the curse. Continue to the epilogue. Opening headline · Recorded crash and shot at 21:02

Keep Jacob and Emma alive for their evidence

  1. In Chapter 1, take Empty Vial, keep the rabbit and Steal Rotor Arm. Take Torn Bags in Chapter 2. In Chapter 3, choose Dive In → Grab, press Up, then choose Detangle to keep the Bloated Corpse evidence and save Jacob for Chapter 6.
  2. In Chapter 4, hold fire both times at the bushes. As Emma, choose Search Bag → Use Taser, then take Attack Photo. Press Down, pass the zipline mash, choose Spray and press Up twice. Back inside, choose Use Trapdoor right away, press Down at the cabinet and pass the ladder mash. At the second zipline choose Reel Quickly → Jump and miss Up. Emma falls and dies.
  3. Take Claw Marks in Chapter 5. In Chapter 6, take Memory Card from the boardwalk as Jacob. Take the right path after the stairs, press Down at the opening attack with Emma dead, choose Run and pass the mash. At the trap choose Pull Free, then miss Up so Jacob dies. Let Nick kill Abi as before.
  4. Take Cease & Desist Letter, Scarred Flesh, Kaylee’s Letter and Trail Cam Footage as you reach their chapters. Follow the deaths above. Recorded opening and closing headlines

Replay Chapter 10 for four closing headlines

Finish the all-evidence route first. Then replay Chapter 10 and repeat Max’s swim, Kaitlyn’s failed decoy and Travis’s fatal crash. Win Laura’s crawl mash every time. The footage and the final Up prompt determine the closing headline.

Collect Trail Cam Footage and press Up
The evidence shows the teens were defending themselves. Closing newspaper
Collect Trail Cam Footage and let Up expire
Caleb survives and is arrested over the massacre. Closing newspaper
Skip Trail Cam Footage and press Up
Hiker remains are found and the deceased Hacketts are blamed. Closing newspaper
Skip Trail Cam Footage and let Up expire
Hiker remains are found and one surviving Hackett is charged. Closing newspaper
