Set Aim Assist to Off and keep directional prompts, mashes, interrupts and Don’t Breathe on manual. Pass prompts unless a step calls for a miss. Continue past Death Rewind on planned deaths. Use Chapter Select from the first chapter where you need to change a choice, then play forward.

The Hacketts at the house
Start in Chapter 9 for Constance, Jedediah, Bobby and Chris. Kaylee’s death is scripted. For all eleven Travis recaps, use Ryan and Travis’s walkthrough.
Caleb outside the freezer
For Caleb’s woods recap, set up Kaitlyn to go alone at the van in Chapter 8, then skip her lodge attack in Chapter 9. For Caleb’s lodge survival, start in Chapter 9 if Kaitlyn still needs a bite. To shoot Caleb, set up an office handoff with Abi, Emma or both.
Caleb in the freezer
Replay Chapter 1 if you lack the rabbit. Replay Chapter 9 to set Kaitlyn’s infection. At the lodge, trap Caleb alone or fail the decoy with Dylan outside. Silas’s fate changes the recap.
The curse and the ending
Use Silas’s hunt for the nest and crash outcomes. For Family Matters, start in Chapter 9 with the correct jail and lodge setup. For a fresh game, begin with the Rough Night walkthrough.

Chapters 1–8 · Before the house

Prepare the rabbit, silver and jail branches

  1. Chapter 1, rabbit. As Abi choose Break In. Inspect the rabbit on the cabin shelf and choose Keep. In Chapter 5 Dylan takes a bite in the radio hut on this setup. Choose Desperate → Chainsaw and pass the mash to keep him human and available for the lodge. Radio hut and amputation inputs
  2. Chapter 4, Emma’s role. Keep Emma unbitten for the simplest silver handoff with both girls or with Emma alone. For Abi alone, choose Open Trapdoor to let Max kill Emma, or keep Emma elsewhere on one of her other branches. To send Kaitlyn to the scrapyard alone with Abi alive, give Emma Max’s island bite and skip Emma’s Run interrupt at the hunters in Chapter 6.
  3. Chapters 5–6, Kaylee and Abi. The group finds Kaylee dead in the pool after the radio-hut scene. Laura shot her off-screen and the death is scripted. At the pool house, shoot Nick as Abi to keep her alive, or hold fire for the Emma-only handoff. Kaylee’s recap · Exact pool-house choices
  4. Chapter 7, Travis. For the hunt or the Laura and Max reunion, hide the syringe behind the brick and press Left during the escape. The Right, Right → Apologetic gun escape also leaves Travis uninjured. For the revenge route, skip hiding the syringe, press Right, Right and choose Demanding → Shoot Travis. Station choices and recoveries
  5. Chapter 8, van. For the paired scrapyard route, leave Emma unbitten. For the solo route with Abi alive, hit infected Emma’s first charge, then let the roof shot and rescue shot expire with Dylan uninfected. If Abi is dead and Emma attacks, choose Run to go alone. Solo scrapyard setup
  6. Chapter 8, cages. Take Stop Laura? to save Nick. If Jacob is caged, choose Help, flip 1 + 2, choose Continue and flip 2 + 3. For Chris’s snare recap, keep Jacob uninfected and leave Emma dead so Jacob is alone for the chase. Cage route and bite setup

Chapter 9

Constance, Jedediah and Bobby

Laura · Constance’s shotgun

  1. When Constance grabs the shotgun, win the mash to shoot her, then press Right. Shotgun death recap
  2. To leave Constance alive through this scene, let the mash expire. Choose Run and press Right. Bobby stabs Ryan either way. Constance’s survival at the attic will depend on Bobby’s condition.

Ryan · The bedroom

Let Pull Knife? expire. If you took it, choose Stop Pulling. Search the room before using the left door’s keyhole or the three-minute base timer expires.

Optional evidence · Kaylee’s Letter

Open the jewelry box on the dresser to the right of the fireplace. Pickup video

Optional tarot · Death

Walk toward the bed and take the card when the camera changes. Pickup video

Check the keyhole, choose Block Door and pass both mashes to keep the knife. Missing the final mash gives Bobby the knife and removes Ryan’s later Stab prompt. Hide and recovery variants

Laura · Upstairs and Jedediah

Choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe in the bathtub. In the blue room inspect the Hunting Trophy Wall if you want the clue, then go upstairs. The office has the Hackett Family Tree. Skip the piano so you can finish exploring before the fight.

Optional tarot · The Sun

Walk toward the windows beyond the upstairs office. Take the card before approaching the door. Step away and return if the prompt expires. Pickup video

Approach the door and press Left, Left, Left. Choose Run to spare Jedediah here. To kill him, choose Attack and win the mash. A failed mash leaves him alive. Laura’s attack recap · Missed prompts and recoveries

Ryan · Bobby at the greenhouse

Press Left at the wall gap, Right to catch the knife, then pass Don’t Breathe. Decide what happens when Bobby catches Ryan.

  • Let Stab expire. Bobby survives the attack. If you already spared Constance and ran from Jedediah, Bobby saves them too. Bobby’s recap · Constance’s recap · Jedediah’s recap
  • Take Stab. In the attic confrontation Chris kills Bobby and any of the older Hacketts you left alive. For all three Chris kills, fail Constance’s gun mash and run from Jedediah, then stab Bobby here. Bobby’s recap · Constance’s recap · Jedediah’s recap

Choose Sympathetic → Accept and let Pull Away? expire for the Silas hunt. Choose Decline if you want to kill Chris and skip the hunt. Bite and revenge variants

Chapter 9 · Scrapyard, shelter and attic

Set up the lodge and choose Chris’s fate

At the scrapyard, go to the garage, use the gate switch by the downstairs stairs and take the metal steps onto the containers.

Optional tarot · Judgement

Cross the container roofs until the camera shows the card. Take it before going down the far stairs and through the bus. Pickup video

Go down the far stairs and through the bus, then head left to the yellow crane. Scrapyard map and clues

  1. With Dylan. Choose Encouraging → Lift Car → Slam Car. Pass the lever mash to keep Kaitlyn unbitten. Let it expire to give her a bite. For the joint freezer rescue, keep Dylan beside her. Crane and bite variants
  2. Caleb’s woods recap. Send Kaitlyn alone to the scrapyard. Choose Hide, fail Don’t Breathe for Silas’s bite, then hit Silas within 2.3 seconds. Pass the support-bar mash and let Left expire. Kaitlyn survives under the scrap and the lodge fight is skipped. Keep Silas alive for Caleb’s woods wording or kill him at the nest for the cured version. Still cursed · Cured
  3. Storm shelter. Inspect the lit fuse box to get or replace silver, then climb the ladder into Chris’s office. Keep Emma unbitten if she needs to carry it alone. Select the handoff
Optional tarot · The Hierophant

Approach the shelter stairs before climbing the separate ladder. Step back and approach again if the prompt expires. At the end of the chapter press Left at the flying stake in Eliza’s flashback. Pickup video

Ryan · Choose the attic target

As Laura approach Chris. After the fall, as Ryan aim directly ahead to hit Chris in the 4.1-second base window. To keep Chris alive, hit transformed Laura farther right or let the shot expire. Attic prompts and Travis’s outcomes

  • Chris dies. Max is cured. If Travis was uninjured in jail and Ryan accepted the bite, choose Suspicious or Inquisitive to start the hunt. If Ryan declined, shoot Chris and let Laura’s Up expire to keep Silas alive and leave Laura captured. Chris’s death recap

Chapter 10

Chris in the woods and Caleb at the lodge

If Chris died, follow Max’s island scene and choose Stay if offered. As Kaitlyn, explore upstairs before choosing Prepare for Attack at the chimney portrait. Choose Keep Looking if you want more time.

Optional tarot · The High Priestess

Climb the stairs next to the bunk room and approach the top-floor windows to reveal the card. Pickup video

Optional evidence · Trail Cam Footage

Inspect the boxes near the upper-floor couch before preparing for the attack. Pickup video · Other lodge pickups

Trap Chris alive

  1. Free Jacob in Chapter 8. Keep him uninfected and alone, and spare Chris in the attic. This opens Chris’s woods chase.
  2. When Chris chases Jacob, choose Run and pass the mash, or choose Hide and pass Don’t Breathe. Press Up at the snare. Jacob escapes and Chris is trapped alive. Chris’s snare recap

Spare Caleb at the lodge

With Kaitlyn bitten, choose Run → Beam and fail both the balance mash and the Up recovery. Caleb leaves alive. For the recorded office variant, pass the beam mash, choose Run → Investigate with silver available and let the shot expire. Caleb spares bitten Kaitlyn.

  • Keep Silas alive for Caleb’s dawn wording. He turns human at sunrise and still carries the curse. Dawn recap
  • Complete the hunt and shoot Silas. Caleb returns to human form permanently. Cured lodge recap

Shoot Caleb · Choose who passes silver

  1. Abi and Emma. Shoot Nick in Chapter 6 to save Abi. Keep Emma unbitten and let both reach Chris’s office in Chapter 9. Abi can use the shell made from her bracelet. If Kaitlyn spent it at the van, get the replacement at the shelter fuse box. Group handoff recap
  2. Abi alone. Save Abi at the pool house and keep Emma out of the office. The simplest route is to open the trapdoor as Emma in Chapter 4. Send Abi up the shelter ladder with her Chapter 8 shell or fuse-box silver. Abi’s handoff recap
  3. Emma alone. Keep Emma unbitten. Let Nick kill Abi in Chapter 6. As Emma in the shelter, inspect the lit fuse box and climb the ladder to the office. Emma’s handoff recap
  4. At the lodge choose Run → Beam and pass the mash or Up recovery. Choose Run downstairs, then Investigate when the office group knocks. Hit Caleb in the three-second base window. Handoff requirements

Use the rabbit · Choose who gets trapped

Keep Abi’s rabbit. At the lodge choose Run → Beam, pass the mash or Up recovery, then choose Run downstairs. Choose Run again if the office choice appears. Pass Don’t Breathe in the kitchen, then choose Freezer → Use Decoy.

  • Pass the door mash. Caleb is trapped alone. Leave Silas alive and Caleb survives the cold. Shoot Silas while Caleb is still trapped and he freezes after the cure. Trapped alive · Freezes
  • Fail the mash with Kaitlyn bitten and Dylan present. Caleb pulls Kaitlyn inside and her bite saves her. Dylan opens the door for both, even if he is unbitten. Kill Silas for the cured recap or spare him for the cursed one. Cured rescue · Cursed rescue
  • Fail the mash with Kaitlyn unbitten and Dylan present. Caleb kills Kaitlyn. Leave Silas alive for Caleb’s infected recap. For the cured variant, shoot Silas at the nest. The hunt marks trapped Caleb dead, then the joint kitchen recap resets his status and shows him alive and cured alongside Kaitlyn’s death. Infected recap · Cured recap

For the other kitchen branches, including Hide, leaving Dylan behind, and Kaitlyn’s joint freezer death, use Kaitlyn’s freezer walkthrough.

Chapter 10 · Silas

Choose when the hunt ends

For the White Wolf survival card, leave Chris alive in the attic so the hunt never starts. Let Ryan’s shot expire or hit transformed Laura. White Wolf recap

For the hunt, leave Travis uninjured in Chapter 7. Have Ryan accept the bite, shoot Chris and choose Suspicious or Inquisitive. Finish the lodge, then choose Inquisitive in the car, press Right and choose Grab Gun → Take Cover.

  • Shoot Silas after the crash. Let the handbrake Down and rescue Down expire. After the crash pass the crawl mash and press Up to shoot Silas. Laura, Ryan and Travis die. The curse ends and The White Wolf unlocks. Crash shot recap
  • Crash and leave Silas alive. Fail both Down prompts, then fail the crawl mash or the final Up. This gives Silas’s named survival recap, like holding fire at the nest.
  • Reach the nest. Press Down at the handbrake or the recovery to stop safely. Choose Determined. If Ryan has the vial, choose Take Vial to protect Laura or Give to Ryan to protect him. Choose Probing → Reflective on the trail.
  • Kill Silas at the nest. Take Raise Gun? within six seconds and hit Silas in the cage. Laura, Ryan and Travis survive. The curse ends and The White Wolf unlocks. Nest shot recap
  • Leave Silas alive at the nest. Take Raise Gun? and hold fire until the entire aiming window expires. Silas attacks the group and spares the person marked by the vial. Named survival recap · The struggle variants

Family Matters · Keep all nine counselors alive

  1. Follow Rough Night through Chapter 8 and leave Travis unharmed. Keep Abi alive and her Chapter 8 silver available for the lodge. Kaylee’s death happens in every run.
  2. In Chapter 9 win Constance’s shotgun mash and press Right. Keep Ryan’s knife through the bedroom. As Laura press Left, Left, Left against Jedediah, then choose Attack and win the mash. As Ryan take Stab? at Bobby and choose Accept when Laura offers the bite.
  3. Pass the crane lever mash to keep Kaitlyn unbitten. Keep Abi’s silver or collect the replacement in the shelter and get to the office. Shoot Chris in the attic after he kills Bobby. Choose Suspicious to join the hunt.
  4. As Max choose Stay. At the lodge choose Run → Beam, pass the balance mash or recovery, then choose Run → Investigate. Hit Caleb with the silver shell.
  5. Stop the car safely. At the nest let Raise Gun? expire, then let Right expire so Travis is shot during the struggle. Hit Silas in the next aiming window. Continue to the epilogue for Family Matters and Rough Night. Recorded Family Matters route

For Family Matters with Silas alive, use the same house deaths and silver shot, but leave Travis uninjured in jail and Decline the bite. After shooting Chris, press Up as Laura and hit Travis to trigger the island reunion. Ryan bleeds out and the hunt is skipped. The PC achievement checks the six Hacketts whose deaths can vary.

Eliza’s final speeches

The game uses the first matching outcome below.

  1. Silas dies. Use the nest shot or crash shot. Eliza threatens to haunt you. Speech recap
  2. Silas lives after you reach the nest. Take Raise Gun? and hold fire. Eliza gloats that the hunt failed, even if Caleb died at the lodge. Speech recap
  3. Caleb dies and the nest is never reached. Shoot Caleb with silver. Keep Chris alive or have Ryan decline the bite to skip the hunt. Eliza thanks you for leaving Silas alive. Speech recap
  4. Caleb and Silas live and the nest is never reached. Spare Chris in the attic and use the bitten beam fall. Eliza warns you about returning. Speech recap

If you skip Chapter 10, the Chapter 9 interlude can reach the warning if you took a Chapter 9 tarot card. The Hierophant plays its flashback first. With none of the four Chapter 9 cards, Eliza’s no-cards scene goes directly to the fate cards. Chapter 9 shortcut
