Before you start

Use the Rough Night walkthrough between pickups. It keeps the card scenes available and shows every story input. Choose Rocky Road in Chapter 2. Keep Jacob alive to reach his Chapter 6 walkway. Keep Abi or a human Emma available for the Chapter 9 shelter, and get Kaitlyn out of the scrapyard for the final card.

Check Pause → Tarot at each chapter break. The totals below are for the current game save. For Hard Pass, follow the ten refusal checkpoints as you collect. Watch or skip visions

For a different ending, the Massacre collection walkthrough gets 21 cards before all nine counselors die. Its Abi-only branch keeps the shelter scene, so you can get all 22 cards with Abi as the sole survivor.

Recover a missed card

If you can still explore the room or trail, back out until the normal camera returns and approach the pickup again. Strength is a one-time cutscene prompt. Once a scene has ended, finish the story and use Chapter Select. Start at the earliest missed card or the decision that blocked its scene. The game restores the cards held at the start of that chapter. Pick up every card from there onward again.

The Hanged Man · Chapter 2
Use Chapter 2 to change the fork to Rocky Road if you took Shady Glade. The Ranger Box needs its own Shady Glade run.
Justice · Chapter 6
Replay Chapter 6 if Jacob was alive and you passed the pickup. If he drowned, start in Chapter 3 and stay ashore with Help Abi or choose Detangle on the dive. If Ryan shot him, start in Chapter 4 and hold fire in both bushes windows.
The Hierophant · Chapter 9
Replay Chapter 9 if someone explored the shelter. If the scene was skipped, restart in Chapter 6 and shoot Nick to save Abi. If Abi only died at the van, start in Chapter 8 and hit Emma in the opening shot. Saving Abi gives you a way to pick up the card even when Emma died earlier.
If you want to keep Abi’s pool house death, bring human Emma to the shelter. If Emma died on the island, replay her Chapter 4 escape and keep her unbitten. If she survived with the island bite, replay Chapter 6 and take the hunters’ bite so she transforms after shelter exploration.
The High Priestess · Chapter 10
Replay Chapter 10 if Kaitlyn explored the lodge. If she died or stayed trapped under the scrap, start in Chapter 9. With Dylan use Lift Car → Slam Car and pass the lever mash. If Kaitlyn is alone, hit Silas, pass the support-bar mash and press Left to dodge the falling scrap. Solo scrapyard walkthrough

For a card missed in a scene you already reached, start from its listed chapter. For example, Strength is a Chapter 4 replay even though it appears at the lodge door between playable scenes.

Hard Pass walkthrough

Start with the Rough Night story choices to keep the pickup scenes open. For Hard Pass alone, take one of the cards listed for each chapter. For Decked Out too, collect the rest of the cards as you go. Choose Move On at each offer through Chapter 8. The numbers below show the credits you should have earned in that save.

  1. Prologue · 1 of 10. Take The Fool by the large rock while Laura explores the woods. Refuse at Eliza’s first visit after Max is attacked.
  2. Chapter 1 · 2 of 10. Take Temperance in the lodge kitchen before Jacob touches the bags. Refuse after the counselors decide to stay at camp.
  3. Chapter 2 · 3 of 10. Choose Rocky Road as Nick and take The Hanged Man before the stairs. Refuse after Truth or Dare. Shady Glade has no tarot card for this interlude.
  4. Chapter 3 · 4 of 10. Take The Tower before Abi reaches Nick in the woods, or The Star as Jacob at the boathouse. Refuse after Jacob’s snare scene.
  5. Chapter 4 · 5 of 10. Take The Magician at Emma’s dock before heading inland. If you missed it, take Strength as the group carries Nick into the lodge. Refuse at the chapter break.
  6. Chapter 5 · 6 of 10. Take The Devil by the picnic tables or The Hermit by the radio tower before Dylan enters the hut. Refuse after the group finds Kaylee in the pool.
  7. Chapter 6 · 7 of 10. Take The Moon in the third changing stall on Abi’s left before the pool house conversation ends exploration. Justice as Jacob also works. Refuse after Laura arrives at the pool house.
  8. Chapter 7 · 8 of 10. Take The World beneath Laura’s cell bed before using the spoon, or The Chariot in the station’s main room before sleeping. Refuse at the break after Laura’s story.
  9. Chapter 8 · 9 of 10. Take The Lovers along the quarry ledge after the railing breaks, or any of the three later Chapter 8 cards. Refuse after the cage scene.
  10. Chapter 9 · 10 of 10. Take The Hierophant by the shelter stairs before using the exit ladder. Stay through Eliza’s flashback introduction after the attic. The game adds the tenth credit before the flying-stake prompt. Press Left at the stake to follow the survival walkthrough’s flashback.

Finish Chapter 9 without The Hierophant

Collect Death, The Sun or Judgement. Have Kaitlyn escape the scrapyard to set up the Chapter 10 lodge scene. At Eliza’s visit before Chapter 10, choose Move On. A run that skips Chapter 10 skips this offer. The Hierophant still gives its flashback credit on that branch.

Replay a missed credit

  1. If you saw no offer before Chapter 9, check whether you collected a card in that chapter. Having cards from earlier chapters cannot trigger the missing offer.
  2. If you picked See More or missed a card, finish the story and use Chapter Select from the earliest missed credit. Start in Chapter 2 if you took Shady Glade. Pick up a card again in each replayed chapter and redo its refusal.
  3. If the shelter was skipped and you want The Hierophant, use the earliest replay that brings Abi or Emma there. Otherwise take one of the other Chapter 9 cards and play through the lodge route. The High Priestess in Chapter 10 can finish Decked Out, but there is no additional refusal credit.
